Commentary
  • Austria and Turkey both tried to take Greece with no coordination, resulting in a mutual bounce — Austria rejected Turkey's request to yield Greece, Turkey ignored Austria's request to hold BUL, and neither power gained the center they needed most.
  • Turkey's late pivot (A BUL → RUM supported by F BLA) was telegraphed to Russia but still bounced off the entrenched Russian fleet in RUM — Turkey wasted both its mobile units and ends 1901 with only Bulgaria as a gain, badly underperforming expectations.
  • Germany told Russia it was moving F DEN → SWE while simultaneously confirming to England a clean HOL/SWE split — in the end Germany held DEN and took only HOL, meaning Russia's warning about a Scandinavian collision may have caused Germany to abandon SWE entirely, handing Russia a free build.
  • England and France executed their western arrangement flawlessly — England took Norway and Belgium, France secured Portugal and Burgundy, with zero friction and explicit mutual messaging throughout, making them the most cooperative pair on the board heading into 1902.
  • Austria's attempt to pressure Russia out of Rumania (threatening A SER → RUM supported by A GAL) produced nothing — Russia held firm, Austria's units sat idle in SER and GAL, and Austria finishes 1901 with only Serbia despite having the most aggressive diplomatic posture in the east.
