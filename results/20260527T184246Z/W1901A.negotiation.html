We're fully aligned — A VEN holds this spring, no threat to Trieste whatsoever. I'm pushing my fleets westward into the Tyrrhenian and western Med. I hope we can build toward a real partnership in 1902 and beyond.

Let's keep Galicia demilitarized and focus our joint pressure southward.

Austria, I'm concerned about Turkey's growing position — F BLA threatens my RUM and their armies are pressing. I'd like to propose we coordinate: I'll push against Turkey from the north while you press from Serbia/Greece into Bulgaria. Can we agree to leave Galicia quiet while we both focus south?

I appreciate the offer to coordinate against Turkey, but I must be direct: A GAL sitting on my border makes any real cooperation impossible — it's a knife at my throat while we shake hands. Austria, I hear your concern about GAL — I want to reassure you that my army there is aimed squarely at Turkey, not Vienna or Budapest. I propose we coordinate: I'll support your move on Bulgaria from Serbia while I press from GAL toward RUM/BUL. Let's agree to demilitarize the Austro-Russian border and both focus south.

I've heard your reassurances but A GAL remains on my border with no concrete commitment to move it — I cannot ignore that threat while coordinating with you. If GAL moves toward RUM this spring, I'll believe your intentions are southern; if it stays or moves toward VIE/BUD, I'll know where we stand. This is my final ask before orders are committed.
RUSSIA → AUSTRIAR3
Austria, I'm moving A GAL this spring — away from your core, toward the south. I want you to know my intentions are directed at Turkey, not you. Let's keep our coordination intact and both focus on the Turkish problem.

However, I need to understand your intentions for Bulgaria first: are you planning to move A BUL toward Romania, or hold in place? I'm ready to coordinate — if you move F BLA into RUM this spring, I'll support pressure on Russia from Serbia and hold my position in Greece to keep the Balkans stable between us. Let's agree: Turkey takes Romania, I consolidate Serbia/Greece, and we both benefit from a weaker Russia. Deal?
TURKEY → AUSTRIAR3
This is the moment — I'm committing F BLA to RUM and A ARM to SEV this spring, directly striking Russia's southern position. If you move A GAL to support my attack on RUM or at minimum threaten WAR to pin Russian forces, we can cripple Russia's south in one turn. I need your answer now: are you in, or should I plan around going it alone?
