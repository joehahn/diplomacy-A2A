Commentary
  • France executed a clean betrayal of Germany: after sending reassuring "ceasefire" messages to Berlin, Paris drove A PIC into BEL with BUR support and took the center, leaving Germany's A BEL dislodged and exposed.
  • England double-crossed France: after France explicitly asked England to support A PIC into BEL from NTH, England instead confirmed to Germany it would support the Belgian hold — France took BEL anyway, but England's final-hour deception is now on record.
