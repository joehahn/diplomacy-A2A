Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Russia took Rumania (F RUM) and sits in GAL threatening BUD, while Italy holds my TRI and Turkey leans at Serbia — I'm boxed on three fronts and must consolidate my core. This turn I keep A SER and A BUD walled to hold Serbia and Budapest, throw A VIE and F VEN at Trieste to pressure Italy off my home center, and use A UKR to threaten GAL/RUM and keep Russia honest. A turn or two out I want Trieste recaptured or at least contested, Budapest and Serbia firmly held, and either a quiet deal with Russia conceding Galicia or a counterstrike to retake Rumania.
ENGLAND
Last turn my Kiel push didn't land — Germany held — so this turn I commit fully: F HEL into Kiel backed by A DEN, taking Germany's center for a seventh SC while F NTH holds the line and F NWY/F EDI anchor the north. France at 7 is the real long-term threat, but Germany's collapse is the open lane right now, so I dismantle Berlin and Kiel over the next two turns. I'll keep the Channel a buffer with France and Russia quiet in the north while I convert Germany into builds.
FRANCE
This turn I lock Spain by holding F SPA/SC with A MAR supporting, take my Winter build at Paris by moving A BRE there, and keep my northern fortress intact with A BEL/A HOL/A BUR mutually supporting against any German RUH probe. Germany keeps demanding a center I won't give—A RUH bounces off BUR if he tries—so I hold firm; meanwhile I stay quiet with England on the Channel to bank my 8th cleanly. A turn or two out I want Paris built, Spain/Portugal locked, and a freed Atlantic fleet to either coordinate west pressure on Germany's Kiel-Munich with England or read the Med with Italy before either northern power runs away.
GERMANY
I'm boxed at three centers and France keeps stalling on its RUH promise, so this turn I test whether France actually vacated RUH by walking A RUH straight into it for a clean fourth center while F KIE and F BER stay home holding my Baltic line. If France contests RUH or it bounces, I keep the line intact and pivot — but France at 7 is the runaway and England at 6 is consolidating, so I need real growth fast. A turn or two out I want A RUH sitting in RUH or HOL with a fleet ready to swing north into HEL/NTH against England's overextension once the western fight commits.
ITALY
I hold Trieste against Austria's assault while pressing his collapse: F TRI defends with A ALB supporting the hold, A GRE holds my center, and F ION/F NAP keep the Ionian line cohesive. With Russia in Galicia and Turkey cutting Serbia, Austria can't sustain his four-unit Trieste strike, so my wall should hold and I keep five centers with Rome open for a Winter build. A turn or two out I want Serbia or a cracked Vienna in reach, then I pivot my fleets toward Turkey's Aegean before he consolidates behind me.
RUSSIA
Austria's A RUM was dislodged to UKR last spring, so Rumania is now empty and undefended — I'm driving F SEV - RUM to reclaim my home center clean, but A UKR threatens my Sevastopol and Moscow approach now. I'll support F SEV into RUM with A GAL to guarantee it against any Bulgarian or Ukrainian interference, while keeping A STP and A FIN walling the quiet north against England. A turn or two out I want RUM consolidated for a Winter build, my home triangle secured against Austria's loose A UKR, and continued pressure on Austria's collapsing east alongside Italy.
TURKEY
My Serbia attack last turn bounced because Italy never gave the support, so Serbia remains Austrian. This turn I take Greece off Italy instead: with A GRE pulled toward Serbia historically and Italy overstretched, I'll drive A BUL - GRE backed by F AEG's support and convoy options, snatching a sixth center while CON and ANK cover home. A turn or two out I want Greece consolidated, F AEG pressing into the Ionian/Eastern Med against Italy's fleets, and my eastern stack positioned to keep growing as Austria collapses and France runs fat in the west.
