FALL 1904 MOVEMENT (F1904M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
My priority this Fall is to take Bulgaria with Russia: A SER - BUL with F GRE supporting and Russia's F RUM backing it, three against Turkey's anchored defense, while A GAL stays home to backstop the line and A BUD covers Serbia's rear. If BUL falls I gain my sixth center and a foothold to grind Turkey's Aegean line next, keeping VIE open for a Winter build. I'll keep F TRI home to wall against any Italian reversal in the Adriatic, since Italy's stack only shifted to ION rather than truly leaving my flank.
ENGLAND
France is the runaway at seven and grabbed Munich, so this turn I drive F NTH into Belgium with F EDI moving to NTH to back the push, while F SKA, F NWY, and A SWE anchor my north against any Russian flicker. My goal is to crack Belgium off France within a turn or two and have a North Sea fleet stack pressing France's western coast, keeping the Baltic quiet with Russia and Germany. If France walls Belgium hard I'll pivot toward Denmark or finishing Russia's St. Petersburg, but the priority is checking France before they reach eight.
FRANCE
I hold Munich firmly and press Kiel this turn: A MUN drives KIE with A RUH supporting, while A BEL takes HOL to grab a German center and A BUR backs the line. Germany's at four and stretched against Russia and England's north, so this fall I want Kiel and Holland banked for two fresh builds while keeping the Atlantic and Marseille quiet. A turn or two out I lean on Berlin with England carving Denmark, then watch for the moment to pivot my new builds against whoever leads.
GERMANY
France at 7 holding my Munich is the real threat, but I'm only four centers and boxed; this turn I consolidate my home line, keeping A KIE solid, F BER backing it, and F DEN anchored, while pushing A HOL toward Belgium with England to split France's western stack. I want Belgium contested and Munich threatened so France must defend west, and over the next turns I aim to retake Munich while keeping Russia's eastern border quiet per our BAL-pull deal. If France leaves Munich exposed I strike for it directly; otherwise I wall Kiel/Berlin and lean on Belgium with England's North Sea fleets.
ITALY
Last turn Turkey did not strike F GRE and Austria stayed walled in Greece, so the convoy-into-Greece plan is stalled again — but my stack is now in ION-adjacent waters with A ALB landed beside Greece. This Fall I'll convoy A ALB straight into GRE behind any Turkish pressure on F AEG/F GRE, with F ADR pinning Trieste and F TYS holding the Ionian approaches; if Turkey is committed I take Greece outright, and if not I have F ADR ready to support a Trieste strike with Serbia's help. A turn or two out I want an army in Greece, F ADR dominating the Adriatic against Austria's soft south, and Rome or Naples freed for a Winter build to deepen the eastern wedge.
RUSSIA
My BUL play with Austria bounced last Spring and Turkey is anchored hard, so this turn I keep F RUM supporting another Austrian push at BUL while holding my home triangle—A WAR pointing at home/Galicia, A STP and A FIN walling St. Petersburg against England, F BOT holding the Baltic corner. England agreed to stay off STP/NC and Germany pulled F BAL, so my real opportunity is grabbing PRU or LVN off Germany's thinned Baltic if his fleet truly stays west; a turn or two out I want BUL cracked with Austria, my home centers secure with MOS open for a Winter build, and a Baltic gain to start rebuilding while France's runaway 7 stays the table's shared problem.
TURKEY
Italy's convoy lane isn't ready, so I'll commit F AEG into GRE this Fall to hit Austria's F GRE while A BUL holds with F BLA and A CON anchoring it against the Austro-Russian squeeze. If I dislodge or pin F GRE, Italy convoys A APU from ALB into Greece next turn behind my strike, cracking Austria's southern line. A turn or two out I want F AEG and Italy's army established on Greece, then push toward Serbia while my Black Sea fleet keeps Russia off Rumania's western approach.

AUSTRIA
Italy committed to taking Greece with Turkish support, so F GRE is likely dislodged regardless; I'll order F GRE to ALB to preserve it and contest Italy's stack rather than sit and be removed. A SER holds back from Bulgaria as agreed with Turkey, but I'll position it to cover Trieste/the Adriatic given Italy's lean — A SER supports F TRI's hold, A BUD supports SER, and A GAL stays home backstopping the line. My plan: wall the Adriatic flank against Italy, keep VIE open for a Winter build, and reassess the Lepanto-versus-stab calculus with Italy next Spring once I see whether his fleets actually leave my coast.
ENGLAND
Germany has committed A HOL to support my F NTH into Belgium, so I'm driving F NTH - BEL with F EDI - NTH backing the line, cracking France's lone A BEL while Germany's support gives me the two-on-one. F SKA, F NWY, and A SWE all anchor my north against any Russian flicker, honoring the quiet-north deal with Russia. If Belgium falls I'll have a North Sea stack pressing France's western coast next year; if their A BUR walls it, I still tie France down off Germany's Kiel line and pivot toward Denmark or the German coast as the situation develops.
FRANCE
Germany walled Kiel with three units, so smashing into it head-on with only Munich and Ruhr would bounce and waste the turn. Instead I'll take the undefended center: A BEL moves to Holland, A RUH supports that move into Holland to overpower A HOL if it stays, while A MUN holds Munich, A BUR backs the western line, and A PAR/A MAR/F MAO/F BRE keep the Atlantic and home quiet. Next turns I bank Holland for a fresh build, keep Munich, and look to pry Kiel or Belgium loose once Germany's wall thins or England commits north.
GERMANY
I'm walling my home line against France: A KIE holds with F BER and F DEN both supporting it, while A HOL supports England's F NTH into Belgium to split France's western stack. This keeps Kiel and Berlin solid against a RUH+MUN push, denies France any gain in the north, and pressures their west alongside England; next turns I look to retake Munich once France is forced to split and keep my eastern border quiet with Russia.
ITALY
Turkey is striking F GRE with F AEG+A BUL, so I move A ALB into Greece with F ADR supporting the attack — if Turkey's strike dislodges or cuts F GRE, I land in Greece this turn rather than waiting; even if F AEG occupies GRE itself, my support gives me a clean shot at the square. F TYS holds the Ionian approaches and A TUN stays in reserve in the central Med covering home. My plan going forward is to secure a Balkan center, keep F ADR dominating the Adriatic against Austria's soft south, and free Rome or Naples for a Winter fleet build to deepen the eastern wedge.
RUSSIA
Austria refused the Bulgaria strike, so I'm pulling F RUM back to defend Sevastopol while A WAR marches into empty Livonia for a Winter build off Germany's thinned Baltic. In the north A FIN supports A STP holding and F BOT anchors the corner, keeping my STP wall intact against England as agreed. Next turns I want Livonia consolidated for a build, my home triangle secure, and to revisit Bulgaria with Austria only once he can actually pin Constantinople.
TURKEY
Italy refined the lane to want A ALB taking Greece itself, so I'll support his army into Greece rather than collide: F AEG supports A ALB - GRE and A BUL also supports A ALB - GRE, giving the convoy/march the strength to dislodge Austria's F GRE while landing Greece in Italy's hands this turn. F BLA stays defensive off Sevastopol to keep my clean Black Sea line with Russia, and A CON holds to cover the straits and keep my build options open. Next turn I pivot F AEG and A BUL toward Serbia and the southern approaches as Austria's line cracks, keeping the Black Sea sealed while I grow against the Austrian centers.
