SPRING 1901 MOVEMENT (S1901M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
My priority this Spring is securing two unowned centers: I'll send A BUD to SER and F TRI to ALB to set up a Fall grab of GRE, while keeping A VIE home to defend against Russia and Italy. I want a Lepanto-style or at least neutral arrangement with Italy so VEN stays quiet, and a Black Sea/Galicia understanding with Russia and Turkey so I'm not pinched while I take the Balkans. A turn or two out I aim to hold SER, GRE, and ideally RUM or BUL, building toward five centers by Winter 1901.
ENGLAND
My priority is securing Norway: I'll move F EDI to NWG and F LON to NTH to convoy A LVP toward Norway in the fall, taking that center uncontested. I want France friendly so the Channel stays quiet — I'll propose a DMZ in ENG and aim my fleets north toward Scandinavia, not at Brest. Two turns out I expect to hold EDI, LON, LVP plus Norway, with fleets positioned to contest the North Sea and Denmark against Germany or Russia.
FRANCE
Standard French opening: F BRE to MAO to set up convoy/Iberia, A MAR into SPA to grab my first neutral, and A PAR to BUR to pressure both Munich and Belgium while keeping options open. I want a friendly understanding with England over the Channel and Belgium, and a non-aggression with Italy so I can focus westward toward Spain and Portugal. By Fall I aim to take SPA and POR, eyeing Belgium if Germany and England leave it open.
GERMANY
My priority this Spring is to grab Holland and Denmark to reach 5 centers by Fall: F Kie to Den, A Mun toward Ruhr to claim Holland next, and A Ber holding to anchor home while I sort out the Russian border. I want to secure non-aggression with England in the North Sea and France in Burgundy so I can expand into the unowned centers unopposed, while keeping Munich flexible to bounce or support against a French Burgundy push. A turn or two out I aim to hold Den, Hol, plus my three homes, building a fleet and an army to press either west against France or east against Russia depending on who's weaker.
ITALY
My priority is securing Tunis with the fleet via Ionian while keeping my armies flexible against Austria and France. I'll send F NAP toward ION to claim TUN this fall, hold A VEN to watch Austria and the Tyrol border, and move A ROM toward APU or hold to support the Tunis convoy threat. A turn or two out I want to own TUN, keep VEN safe, and decide whether to ally with Austria against Turkey or pressure France in the west.
RUSSIA
My priorities are securing Rumania and Sweden this year while keeping Austria and Turkey friendly enough to avoid a two-front opening. I'll move F SEV toward RUM and push F STP/SC toward the Gulf of Bothnia to threaten Sweden, while keeping A WAR flexible for Galicia or a Black Sea-supporting role. I want a Black Sea DMZ with Turkey and a Galicia understanding with Austria so I can grow into Rumania and Sweden uncontested, aiming to hold both centers by Fall 1901.
TURKEY
My priority is grabbing Bulgaria this fall, so I'll open A CON toward BUL while moving F ANK to BLA to secure the Black Sea and contest Russia's access to RUM. I'll support that with A SMY moving up to ANK or holding to back the fleet, aiming to control BLA and take BUL by Fall 1901 for a likely build. I'll seek a non-aggression understanding with Russia on the Black Sea and probe Austria about splitting the Balkans, leaving GRE/SER to Austria if they leave BUL and RUM to me.

  • Turkey's A SMY bounced trying to reach ANK, which Turkey already held — a wasted move that leaves Turkey with only one unit in motion (toward Bulgaria) and signals poor opening coordination, putting Turkey behind its rivals in the south.
