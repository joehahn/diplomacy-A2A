SPRING 1902 MOVEMENT (S1902M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Russia is the clear eastern threat at six centers, and both Russia and Turkey are courting me against each other — I'll lean toward Turkey and strike Rumania this year. I'll move F GRE toward the Aegean/Bulgarian coast or hold Serbia to support a Black Sea push, position A SER to support an attack on RUM (with Turkey's BUL and F SEV pressure), and send A VIE-GAL to threaten Russia's flank and contest that key province. Two turns out I want RUM in Austrian or Turkish hands, GAL pressuring Warsaw, and Italy still convoying east so I'm not exposed on the Adriatic.
ENGLAND
I'm holding Norway and looking to expand: Russia is the runaway power at 6 centers, so I want to pivot the Baltic squeeze against him now rather than later. This turn I'll keep A NWY positioned to threaten Sweden/St. Petersburg, bring F NWG toward Norway's defense or Barents to pressure STP, and float F NTH/EDI to react to Belgium and Denmark developments. A turn or two out I want to be contesting Sweden with German Baltic help and aiming a fleet at St. Petersburg's north coast via Barents, while keeping France friendly with the Channel DMZ intact so my back stays clear.
FRANCE
This turn I take Belgium as agreed with England and Germany: I'll move F BRE up toward the Channel/Picardy line to convoy or escort A PAR into Belgium, while keeping Iberia banked with A SPA and A POR. My concern is the Channel DMZ with England — Belgium is reachable via PIC by land, so I'd rather route A PAR through PIC and keep my fleets out of ENG/NTH to avoid spooking England. A turn or two out I want to hold BEL, SPA, POR, BRE, PAR, MAR with a fresh build, positioned to pressure Germany's Munich/Ruhr if he overextends north.
GERMANY
I'm at five centers and need new ground, so this Spring I'll consolidate while setting up the Baltic squeeze on Russia: build flexibility by keeping Berlin and Munich able to bounce Silesia/Prussia, and position my Kiel fleet toward Baltic. France is taking Belgium per our deal, so my growth comes east against Russia's overextended fleets (BOT, SEV, RUM) with England pressing from NTH/NWY. A turn or two out I want a fleet contesting the Baltic and an army pressuring Silesia/Prussia, turning Russia's six centers into my next harvest while keeping France and England quiet.
ITALY
This is the turn to launch the Lepanto: I'll convoy A TUN to ALB through Ionian, supported by Austria's F GRE and A SER pressure, opening the eastern front against Turkey while Austria spearheads Serbia/Bulgaria. A VEN stays defensive to honor the Trieste DMZ, and F NAP covers home and Tyrrhenian. A turn or two out I want A ALB pushing into Greece or onward against Bulgaria with Austrian backing, F ION dominating the Ionian/Aegean approaches, and a new build at Naples to deepen the eastern stack — all while France and Austria keep my western and Adriatic borders quiet.
RUSSIA
I'm at six centers with three builds in, so this year is about converting position into more centers while keeping my borders quiet. My best growth target is Austria: I'll work with Turkey to point A WAR toward Galicia and pressure Budapest/Vienna, while keeping F RUM/F SEV anchored on the Black Sea front and Sweden/Bothnia stable against any German Baltic move. A turn or two out I want A WAR in Galicia threatening Budapest, fleets coordinated with Turkey on Rumania/Bulgaria, and my northern position holding so I can build again next Winter toward seven or eight centers.
TURKEY
Russia has fleets stacked north (BOT, SWE) and an isolated F RUM holding Rumania, so this is the moment to break the Black Sea DMZ and contest RUM. I'll push F ANK into BLA to threaten Rumania and Sevastopol, line up A BUL to support a move on RUM, and keep my Smyrna fleet ready to follow into the Aegean or back the straits. Two turns out I want F ANK in the Black Sea pressuring SEV/RUM while A BUL grabs Rumania, ideally with Austria pressing Russia from Galicia so Russia can't reinforce east.

A BUD will support the Galicia move to make sure Vienna gets in and Russia can't contest it. The plan ahead is to crack Rumania this Spring with Turkey, press Warsaw from Galicia with German pressure on the Baltic, and revisit the Lepanto with Italy once Russia is cut down.
ENGLAND
I'm moving A NWY into Sweden with Germany's F DEN supporting it, while F KIE pushes to BAL on his end — Russia's A SWE is unsupported by my read so this should crack cleanly and net me a fifth center. I'll bring F NWG to NWY behind the army to backfill and hold, F NTH supports the Sweden attack from the north, and F EDI stays put to anchor home and enable a Winter build. Going forward I keep squeezing Russia's north with Germany, aiming a fleet toward Barents/STP next year while the Channel stays a DMZ with France.
FRANCE
I'm staging for Belgium this turn by moving A Paris to Picardy, with F Brest advancing to the Channel to back the Belgium push next fall while keeping Iberia banked with A Spain and A Portugal holding their centers. F MAO stays in the Mid-Atlantic to guard the approaches and keep the convoy/Brest options open. Next fall I take Belgium overland from Picardy, ideally with fleet support, then look toward coordinating a western squeeze on Russia with Germany once Belgium is mine. Wait—I committed to keeping fleets out of the Channel through 1902, so I'll route F Brest to Picardy isn't possible alongside the army; instead F Brest holds or supports while A Paris moves to Picardy, A Spain and A Portugal stay put, and F MAO holds, taking Belgium from Picardy in the fall without breaking the Channel DMZ.
GERMANY
I'm committing fully to the Baltic squeeze with England: F DEN supports A NWY into Sweden to crack Russia's unsupported army, and F KIE pushes into BAL to threaten his Bothnia and Prussia. I'll hold A BER and A MUN at home to anchor and keep Silesia/Prussia options open, with A HOL staying defensive in Holland. Next turn I aim to take Sweden if England's army needs backup, build a second Baltic fleet at Winter, and exploit Russia's two-front collapse as Austria presses Galicia from the south.
ITALY
I'm holding this spring as agreed with Austria and Turkey: F ION holds in the central Med clear of the Adriatic, A TUN holds, A VEN stays defensive, and F NAP supports F ION to keep my central position solid. The plan is to let Austria and Turkey grind down Russia this year while I bank a quiet 1902, then revisit the Lepanto or a Greek landing next year once the Balkans open and I see whether Austria overreaches south. If Austria's Trieste fleet drifts toward me I'm positioned to react, but for now I grow uncontested and keep both eastern deals warm. ORDERS: F ION H F NAP S F ION A TUN H A VEN H
RUSSIA
I'll move A WAR into Galicia to open the Austrian front per my deal with Turkey, while F RUM holds Rumania as my anchor and F SEV stays defensive guarding the Black Sea approach. In the north, A SWE and F BOT stay coordinated to hold Sweden against any German Baltic push, A STP anchors home, keeping my northern lane peaceful with England and the Baltic understanding with Germany intact. Next turn I bring force to bear on Budapest and Vienna while watching that Turkey keeps BLA empty and doesn't slip toward Rumania.
TURKEY
I'm committing east on Russia: F ANK moves to BLA, A BUL attacks RUM with Austria's A SER supporting the assault, while A CON supports BUL's hold position and F SMY moves to AEG to extend my reach south. The key risk is that F SEV could support F RUM's defense — but with Austria's SER support I match it, and even a bounce forces Russia to commit defensively while Austria pins Warsaw from Galicia. Next turn I push into Rumania and Sevastopol if the front opens, watching whether Austria or Russia tries to stab me once Russia's eastern fleets are exposed.

Commentary
