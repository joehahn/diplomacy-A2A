FALL 1901 MOVEMENT (F1901M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
This Fall I'll take Greece with F ALB - GRE while holding A SER to guard against any Turkish reach into Serbia, and move A VIE to BUD to secure that center for a Winter build. That puts me at five centers (VIE, BUD, TRI, SER, GRE) heading into 1902, with two builds to convert into pressure either south against Turkey or as a hedge against Italy. A turn or two out I want fleets and armies positioned to push toward Bulgaria or the Aegean while keeping Galicia quiet with Russia.
ENGLAND
This turn I convoy A YOR into Norway via NTH for my fall build, with NWG supporting the convoy/move to guarantee it lands. France is honoring the Channel DMZ and Germany is taking Denmark, so my western and northern borders stay quiet — I expect to end Fall holding EDI, LON, LVP plus Norway for a build of four units. A turn or two out I want a new fleet from a home center pushed toward the North Sea/Skagerrak so I can contest Denmark or pressure Russia's Sweden if either grows too fast.
FRANCE
This fall I take both Iberian centers: A SPA holds onto Spain while A GAS swings to Portugal via convoy isn't needed—I'll send GAS to support or grab POR directly through the land route, settling on A SPA to POR and A GAS to SPA to keep both occupied, with F MAO covering. The cleaner play is A SPA - POR and A GAS - SPA, leaving F MAO free to guard the approaches and set up a possible Belgium or Brest move next year. By Winter I want Spain and Portugal banked for two builds, with my forces positioned to contest Belgium against Germany in 1902 if he overcommits north.
GERMANY
This Fall I take Holland with A RUH and hold Denmark with F DEN, putting me at five centers and earning two builds at Winter. A BER stays home to anchor and keep the option to bounce Silesia/Prussia or fill Kiel for a build. A turn or two out I want a new fleet in Kiel and an army in Munich, positioning to press either France (if Iberia overextends) or Russia in the Baltic depending on who weakens first.
ITALY
This fall I take Tunis by convoying A APU there through ION, claiming a fourth center for a Winter build. I'll keep A VEN holding to honor the Trieste DMZ and watch Tyrol, and once Tunis is mine I'll build a fleet at Naples to set up the Lepanto against Turkey next year. A turn or two out I want F ION and a new fleet supporting an eastward push into Greece and the Aegean while Austria presses the Balkans.
RUSSIA
This Fall I take Sweden by moving A LVN to SWE convoyed via F BOT, securing a fifth center while F RUM holds Rumania firmly per my deal with Turkey. A STP stays put to hold my home center and anchor the north, leaving MOS, SEV, and WAR vacant for Winter builds. A turn or two out I want fleets in the Baltic and Black Sea regions to press Germany or Turkey if either border heats up, while keeping England and Austria friendly so I can grow toward six or seven centers in 1902.
TURKEY
This Fall I'll take Bulgaria cleanly with A BUL holding to secure the build, keep F ANK home to honor the Black Sea DMZ with Russia, and leave CON vacant so I can build a fleet there in Winter. Next year I want to challenge Russia for Rumania and push F ANK into the Black Sea once the DMZ has served its purpose, growing toward Armenia and the eastern centers while Austria stays busy with Greece and Serbia. I'll watch whether Russia overextends—if their RUM fleet looks isolated, I break the DMZ and contest it with a Black Sea fleet build.
