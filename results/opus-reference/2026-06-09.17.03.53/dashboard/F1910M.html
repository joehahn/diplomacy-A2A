AUSTRIA
I now hold Albania at six centers with my core intact, and the next center off Italy is Greece — but A GRE is backed by Turkey's F AEG, so I'll instead consolidate and look at Trieste-area builds while pressing Greece only if I can mass three. This turn I keep A BUD and A VIE walling Budapest against any Russian shift, hold Serbia firm, and position A ALB and F ADR to threaten Greece or back Trieste, taking TRI vacant for a Winter build slot if I can free it. A turn or two out I want Greece pried off Italy with A SER and A ALB massed and F ADR/F ION pressure, my eastern border quiet with Russia honoring GAL, and England's runaway north drawing a coalition I can lean into.
ENGLAND
I now hold Berlin and sit at 8 centers with a tight Baltic and North Sea wall, while France at 7 is the only power who can race me to a solo, so this is the turn I stop pretending the buffer is permanent and turn west. I'll push F NTH and F IRI to contest the English Channel and threaten Belgium, with F LON backing the Channel break and F KIE/F BER/F PRU-coordination locking the Baltic so Russia can't slip in behind me. A turn or two out I want F ENG taken from France, A DEN convoyed or marched toward Belgium, and my fleet line squeezing France's BEL/BRE coast to break its lead before it grows unbreakable.
FRANCE
England now sits at 8 centers and is one good fall from threatening a solo, so I'm done feeding the clean buffer — this is the turn I start prying. I'll bank a home build by clearing PAR (A PAR-BRE) and pushing A SPA-POR to keep my coast covered, while holding my western wall (A BEL, A RUH, F ENG) and A MUN to keep England honest, and I'll finally commit F TYS toward WES to begin the Med pressure with Italy against England's Atlantic approach. A turn or two out I want a Brest build, my Tyrrhenian fleet in WES/NAF threatening England's southern reach, and Italy's Ion/Nap stack swinging west so we hit England on two fronts before he reaches 18.
GERMANY
I'm down to a single fleet in Prussia with Berlin lost to England, so my only realistic play is to keep this fleet alive and useful rather than waste it. I'll lean on the England-Russia tension in the Baltic: Russia has A WAR/A STP/A GAL nearby and England's wall sits at BER/KIE/BAL, so I'll move F PRU toward LVN or support a Russian move to ingratiate myself and stay relevant as a wall. Realistically I'm being squeezed out, but I'll keep talking both sides into preserving me as a flank check while watching for any seam to reclaim coastal ground.
ITALY
I'm stuck at four centers with my Greece grab and Serbia push both stalled, while England runs at eight and France at seven — the real game is now stopping a leader, not grinding Austria. This turn I'll keep A GRE either taking the Serbia/Bulgaria seam if it opens or holding firm, push F ION toward the Aegean or Tyrrhenian to keep my fleets mobile, and finally commit to swinging west with France against England rather than bleeding in the south. A turn or two out I want F ION/F NAP angling toward the Tyrrhenian to back France's Atlantic push, Rome open for a Winter build, and my eastern line either holding Greece or having converted into a real fifth center off Turkey's Bulgaria.
RUSSIA
I'm stuck at 5 and the real story is England runaway at 8 and France at 7, so my growth window is east where Austria is being ground down by Italy and Turkey. I'll keep F RUM in Rumania with A GAL supporting it to defense-two against Turkey's bluffed A BUL-RUM, hold A WAR/A STP/A FIN on my home triangle, and watch for Vienna or Serbia to crack between Italy and Austria so I can swing A GAL onto Vienna or Budapest next turn. The clean Black Sea border with Turkey holds another year only if F RUM stays planted, so I won't vacate it.
TURKEY
Italy now holds Greece alone with A GRE, F ION, and F NAP, and Austria's wall is intact, so my only realistic gain this turn is forcing Russia's F RUM. I'll drive A BUL - RUM with A CON moving up behind it for support, while F AEG and F SMY hold the strait south to keep my home and the Black Sea line covered. If Rumania cracks I finally bank my fifth center; if Russia walls it to defense-two I've lost only a turn and can pivot back onto the Balkans, but I need to stop trading empty promises and actually test the seam.

  • Italy held A GRE in place rather than vacating it for Turkey's A BUL-GRE swap, correctly reading Turkey's "concrete trade" as a simple grab of Italy's own center dressed up as coordination — Turkey's A BUL and F AEG sat idle as a result.

  • France's fleet retreating to Picardy keeps a naval presence on the Channel coast, suggesting England hasn't fully consolidated control of the English Channel despite its dominant 9-center position.
  • With Germany eliminated, England at 9 centers is the clear frontrunner and every remaining power should be eyeing opportunities to slow its growth before it reaches 18.
  • France pulling back to Picardy rather than disbanding signals Paris is committed to holding the line against English pressure in the west, preserving options for a Channel contest in 1911.
  • The Austria-Russia parity at 5 centers each in the east bears watching — neither has broken away, suggesting a tense standoff or cautious cooperation in the Balkans and beyond.
  • Italy and Turkey, both at 4 centers, remain the most vulnerable powers; whichever can ally with France or Russia first may survive the endgame squeeze from England's dominant position.
