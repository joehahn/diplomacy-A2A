FALL 1909 MOVEMENT (F1909M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Italy's F ALB sits exposed next to my Serbia and the Adriatic truce has stopped paying me — this turn I take Albania with A SER moving in, backed by F VEN and A TRI supporting the strike, to break Italy's thinning line and set up a sixth center while Turkey grinds his south. A BUD and A VIE wall Budapest against any Russian Galician push, keeping that border locked per my deal with Russia. A turn or two out I want Albania consolidated and an opening toward Greece off Italy's collapsing stack, with my eastern borders quiet while Russia and Turkey bleed each other on the Black Sea.
ENGLAND
I hold Kiel firmly with F BER gone — France took Berlin, Germany is down to F BER and F HEL, so the German corpse is nearly picked clean and I sit at 7 centers with a solid Baltic and North Sea wall. This turn I lock down Kiel against any German bounce and start the pivot west: I want F NTH or F LON probing toward Belgium and the Channel because France at 7-plus (now with Berlin) is the real threat I must break before its lead grows. A turn or two out I aim to have fleets contesting ENG/BEL and a tight Baltic line, squeezing France from the north while Russia presses Berlin's corner.
FRANCE
I took Berlin last turn and now sit at 8 centers with England the runaway at 7 fleets — he's my real threat, so I consolidate the German interior and pivot west/south. This phase I hold Berlin with A MUN supporting, keep Ruhr and Belgium as my western wall against England's North Sea stack, and swing F SPA/SC to LYO while A MAR covers the home line so my Atlantic hand frees toward the Med. A turn or two out I want Munich/Berlin/Ruhr locked, a fresh build off PAR, and my Lyon fleet coordinating with Italy against England's north before his fleets become unstoppable.
GERMANY
England now holds Kiel after my F KIE was dislodged to Helgoland, so my home center is already partly gone and I'm down to Berlin plus a fleet in HEL. My realistic play is to retake Kiel: F HEL and F BER can both strike at KIE, but England's F KIE will defend, so I need to coordinate one supporting the other while keeping Berlin from falling to Russia's A PRU. I'll throw F HEL at Kiel with F BER supporting that attack, accepting that Berlin may be lost to Russia, since reclaiming a coastal center I can build on beats holding an inland square I can't defend long-term.
ITALY
I'm stuck at four centers while France and England run at seven each, so I need to break my own stagnation: this turn I'll read Turkey's Aegean for a fifth center. If Turkey commits A BUL onto Serbia again, Bulgaria thins and I can push F ION toward AEG or pivot A GRE onto Bulgaria with F ALB/F ION backing once his stack swings west. I'll keep the Adriatic truce with Austria intact (F ALB pointed east, nothing toward Venice), but I'm done sitting — my goal a turn or two out is to pry Bulgaria or the Aegean off an overextended Turkey and convert Rome into a Winter fleet build.
RUSSIA
Berlin is the prize this turn: Germany's F KIE was dislodged so Berlin is now defended only by F BER, but England is sitting in KIE and BAL right next to it, so I must move A PRU-BER now and contest it before England's fleets swallow Germany entirely. I'll keep A GAL-BUD to cut A BUD for Turkey's Serbia push, hold F RUM defensive on the Black Sea, and pull A MOS-STP (or keep MOS open) to secure a Winter build while A FIN walls the corner. A turn or two out I want Berlin and Prussia banked off Germany's corpse, a Moscow/home build in hand, and RUM/GAL ready to swing onto Bulgaria the moment Turkey's fleets drift north.
TURKEY
Serbia has walled me three years running at defense three, so I'm done bleeding on it directly — this turn I pivot. Russia's A GAL sits adjacent to Rumania and F RUM is the only thing holding it; with A BUL - RUM backed by F AEG supporting (F RUM cut by my move into it isn't possible, but I can hammer it) I test the Black Sea seam I've promised to keep clean, while keeping A CON and F SMY covering home and the straits. A turn or two out I want Rumania or a renewed Serbia crack once Austria's wall thins, and my fleets positioned to read Italy's Ionian if he turns on Bulgaria as he's hinted.

Coming turns I bank the Moscow/Warsaw/StP builds, watch England's seven fleets carefully now that Berlin falls to him, and keep RUM/GAL ready to swing onto Bulgaria the moment Turkey drifts north.

F SMY and F AEG stay on the strait to hold the line and keep Italy honest. If Rumania cracks I gain my fifth center and a foothold on the Black Sea; if it bounces I've lost nothing and consolidate for a renewed Balkan break next spring.
