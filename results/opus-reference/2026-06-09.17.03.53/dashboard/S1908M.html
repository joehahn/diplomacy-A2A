SPRING 1908 MOVEMENT (S1908M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm down to four centers and boxed in, but my core (BUD, VIE, SER, VEN) is intact and the attackers are now distracted: Russia and Turkey are eyeing each other's Black Sea line, and Italy's stack is thin in the Adriatic. This turn I hold my Balkan wall firm—A BUD and A VIE bracing Budapest/Vienna, A SER holding Serbia with backing, F VEN covering Trieste's approach—while looking for the moment Italy's F TRI is undersupported so I can finally retake Trieste with VEN, VIE, and BUD. A turn or two out I want Trieste recovered for a build and Russia kept quiet in Galicia so I can turn fully on Italy.
ENGLAND
Germany is down to three centers (Kiel, Berlin, Munich) with two fleets stacked on the Baltic, and my Kiel attack keeps bouncing two-on-two; this turn I commit F HEL and A DEN both at Kiel with F NTH supporting to break through for a seventh center. France at 7 is the real runaway, so once I bank a German center I pivot my northern weight toward the Channel/Belgium line before France's lead becomes unbreakable. I keep Russia quiet in the corner and F NWY/F EDI/F LON anchoring the line so nothing flickers behind me.
FRANCE
Germany is down to 3 centers and stalled, so this turn I crack his west: A BUR to MUN with A MAR supporting from a vacated Marseilles line, and A HOL/A BEL pressing RUH and KIE while England hits Kiel from the Baltic. I'll build at home off PAR this winter and aim to hold MUN plus take RUH within a turn or two, swinging my freed Atlantic fleet eastward toward Italy's Med once Germany's collapse is secured. I keep F ENG anchoring the Channel buffer with England intact for now, but I'm watching for the moment England's northern lead makes him my next target.
GERMANY
France has stalled me for seven turns and yields nothing, and England's North Sea wall plus France's Belgium fortress leave me no clean western center, so I'm done pinning France for free. This turn I commit to the only real growth in reach: I throw F KIE at HEL to break England's overextended northern line, with F BER backing the Baltic and A RUH covering MUN/RUH, aiming to peel DEN or HOL off England over the next two turns. A turn or two out I want a fleet in HEL or DEN and A RUH poised at HOL, having forced England to choose between defending his coast and his French front.
ITALY
I lost A GRE to Turkey last fall, so I'm down to four centers with Austria still clinging to Trieste's home and Turkey now fat at five and pressing into my Greek flank. This turn I consolidate: hold F TRI with A ALB supporting, keep F ION/F NAP cohesive in the Ionian to wall against Turkey's Aegean stack, and free Rome for a Winter fleet build by keeping NAP productive. A turn or two out I want to fend off Turkey's Aegean advance, possibly retake Greece if Turkey overextends, and lean on Russia and Austria to keep Turkey's Balkan line split before he turns his full weight on me.
RUSSIA
I hold Rumania and Galicia at 5 centers and Austria's collapsing south is my growth lane, so this turn I drive A GAL - BUD with A WAR - GAL chaining behind it to take Budapest while Italy and Turkey keep A SER and A VIE pinned. F RUM stays defensive on the Black Sea border with Turkey, and in the north I pull A STP toward MOS to open a build slot while A FIN walls the corner with England's quiet front intact. A turn or two out I want BUD consolidated, MOS open for a Winter build, and continued pressure toward VIE as Austria breaks between me and Italy.
TURKEY
I hold Greece at six centers and now need to crack Austria's Serbia, which is the last weak target adjacent to me before Italy and Russia carve the rest. This turn I drive A GRE-SER backed by A CON support up through Bulgaria, while F AEG and F SMY hold the straits and A ANK covers home; with Russia pressing Galicia/Budapest and Italy on the Adriatic, Austria can't defend Serbia on three fronts. A turn or two out I want Serbia in hand, F AEG pressing into the Ionian against Italy's overstretched fleets, and my eastern stack ready to pivot on whichever of Italy or Russia emerges strongest as Austria finishes collapsing.

If Austria honors the truce I keep Trieste clean and free Rome for a Winter fleet build; if Turkey or Russia overextends I pivot to retake Greece or angle into the Aegean as my fleets consolidate. My through-line stays the same: hold my four centers firmly, lean on Russia and Turkey to keep eating Austria's east, and start turning my weight on Turkey before he laps me from the Balkans.
RUSSIA

Commentary
  • Austria successfully bluffed both Russia and Turkey into wasted attacks: Austria defended Budapest with VIE support and held Serbia with BUD support, bouncing both GAL→BUD and GRE→SER — Austria's "I'm committed west on Italy" messaging was cover for a full eastern defensive stand.
  • Austria also called Italy's bluff perfectly: rather than assaulting Trieste as threatened in early messages, Austria held all units in place, and Italy likewise held F TRI with A ALB support — both powers talked aggression but neither moved, leaving the Austro-Italian front completely static.
  • The England-France coordination against Germany partially misfired: France sent A HOL→RUH (displacing Germany's A RUH) but A BUR→MUN bounced Germany's A RUH→MUN, meaning neither power took Munich — Germany's two-front defense held the key center despite being outnumbered on paper.
  • England failed to crack Kiel despite three-on-two messaging: F HEL→KIE bounced Germany's supported F KIE hold, meaning England banked no build this turn despite coordinating extensively with France — Germany's F BER support held the line exactly as Germany promised it would.
  • Turkey's repositioning (A CON→BUL, A ANK→CON) suggests a consolidation and pipeline for future Balkan pressure rather than the immediate Serbia grab promised to Russia and Italy — Turkey advanced its queue but delivered nothing to its supposed partners this phase.
