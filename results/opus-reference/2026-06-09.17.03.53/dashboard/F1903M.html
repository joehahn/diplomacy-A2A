Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Italy moved F ADR beside Trieste and is openly threatening my flank, so my priority this turn is to wall off the Adriatic while keeping pressure on Turkey: F TRI holds or covers ALB, F GRE supports the front, and A SER threatens Turkish Bulgaria with A GAL backing Russia's RUM retake. I want to keep VIE open for a Winter build and convert my 5 centers into 6, expanding south against Turkey while neutralizing Italy through either a real Lepanto or a firm defensive line at Trieste/Greece. Two turns out I aim to hold Greece and Serbia firmly, crack Bulgaria with Russian help, and have Trieste secure against any Italian Adriatic push.
ENGLAND
I finally cracked Sweden last turn and I'm sitting on it now — this Fall I bank it as my fifth center by holding firm, and I want to convert that into a Winter build at home. Russia's north is crumbling: STP is weakly held with his A STP and F BOT exposed, so I'll position F NWY and A SWE to drive on St. Petersburg next year while keeping Germany honest in the Baltic. The Channel stays a quiet DMZ with France and I push F EDI or NWY toward NTH/Barents to set up the STP assault in 1904.
FRANCE
Germany's A SIL is now exposed against Russia's Warsaw, and the joint Warsaw drive depends on me backing Silesia, but I should weigh whether helping Germany grow is worth it versus turning Burgundy on his Munich while it sits empty. This turn I'll move A BUR to MUN if Germany leaves it open, stabbing for a quick German center while keeping A BEL on Belgium, A MAR/A PAR backing the western line, and my fleets guarding the Atlantic—Germany's whole force is committed east and can't defend Munich. A turn or two out I want Munich banked plus a fresh build, threatening Kiel/Ruhr while England stays pinned north and Italy fights Austria.
GERMANY
England took Sweden, so Russia's north is cracking — but England is now a four-center fleet power circling my coast and I have no build in sight from this front, so I need to seize Russian ground myself. This Fall I'll drive A SIL into Prussia (with BAL convoy/support backing) to grab that center while Russia's WAR is pinned, hold A BER to keep Kiel open for a Winter build, and keep F DEN/HOL anchored against any English pivot into the Baltic or North Sea. A turn or two out I want Prussia banked, a fleet built in Kiel to contest the Baltic against England, and a joint Warsaw drive with France's Burgundy unit while watching for England's inevitable turn on my northern centers.
ITALY
My stack is now parked against Austria's soft Adriatic-Balkan flank with F ADR beside Trieste, A APU ready to convoy, F NAP and A TUN in reserve. This turn I press Austria directly: I want F ADR supporting an attack on Trieste with A SER's help if Austria's distracted, or convoy A APU toward Trieste/Albania to open a Balkan center, while keeping Turkey committed to pin F GRE next turn. Within two turns I aim to hold Trieste or Greece, have F NAP push into Ionian, and free Rome or Venice for a Winter build to deepen the eastern wedge.
RUSSIA
The Rumania retake failed last turn because Turkey held with full Black Sea support, and I lost Sweden to England in the north — I'm down to a defensive crouch and need to consolidate. This turn I'll retake Rumania with F SEV backed by Austria's A GAL while A WAR covers Galicia/home, and in the north I'll pull A FIN back toward STP and use F BOT/A STP to wall the Baltic against Germany and England rather than overextend. A turn or two out I want Rumania recaptured and held with Austrian coordination against Turkey, my home triangle (MOS open for a Winter build, WAR, STP) secure, and the Baltic line stabilized so I'm not bleeding centers on two fronts.
TURKEY
Austria flipped and is backing Russia's retake of Rumania while A SER threatens Bulgaria, so this turn I consolidate the east rather than overreach: A RUM holds with F BLA, F ANK, and A CON all anchoring it against the SEV/GAL/SER pressure, keeping Rumania locked and the Black Sea sealed. F AEG stays south to cover Bulgaria and the straits near Greece. A turn or two out, with Italy already moving F ION into the Adriatic, I pivot south alongside him against Austria's Greece-Serbia line, throwing F AEG behind his Greece landing once my eastern hold is secure.
