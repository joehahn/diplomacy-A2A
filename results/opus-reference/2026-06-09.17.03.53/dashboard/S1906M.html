SPRING 1906 MOVEMENT (S1906M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm at four centers and stagnant; Turkey is fat at five with F RUM exposed, so my real play is to take Rumania this Fall with A GAL, A BUD, and A SER while Russia's F SEV cuts in too. This Spring I'll position for that strike — keep A GAL poised on Rumania's approach, A BUD and A SER ready to support, and F TRI walling the Adriatic against Italy. Two turns out I want Rumania mine, Vienna filled with a Winter build, and a foothold to keep grinding Turkey's weakened Balkan line.
ENGLAND
I lost Belgium and I'm boxed into the north at five centers with France runaway at seven and Germany now stabilizing at four — my best growth is to reclaim Denmark and shore up against any German Baltic move while keeping Russia quiet. This turn I'll push F SKA - DEN to threaten Germany's exposed F DEN, anchor F NTH and F NWY to hold the line, and keep A SWE flexible to support a Denmark grab or convoy west later. Two turns out I want Denmark in hand, fleets pressing the German/French coast, and the Russian border still quiet so I can free up force against whichever of France or Germany overextends.
FRANCE
England holds Belgium with a five-fleet North Sea wall and is the board leader at 5; my western stack is strong but can't crack Belgium head-on without German fleet help that never materialized. This turn I consolidate: hold Belgium and Holland firmly, keep Munich pressure via Burgundy, and reposition F MAO/F ENG to threaten England's Channel approaches rather than grind. A turn or two out I want to pull Germany into actively cutting England's North Sea support so I can retake Belgium, then convert my Atlantic fleets toward Brest/Channel dominance and a build at home.
GERMANY
I've reclaimed Munich and now hold four centers with three fleets in the north, so this turn I consolidate and look for growth: A MUN stays to garrison my home center and threaten BUR/RUH, while F KIE, F BER, and F DEN coordinate to either contest the Baltic or pivot against whoever overextends. France at 7 and England's heavy northern fleet stack are both threats, so I want to keep my eastern border quiet with Russia and look for a chance to throw F DEN at England's North Sea or Sweden once France and England grind each other in Belgium/Holland again. A turn or two out I aim to be pushing F DEN-NTH or supporting an attack on Sweden, with A MUN pressing into Ruhr if France leaves the west thin.
ITALY
Austria has only four units (SER, BUD, TRI, GAL) and his entire line is committed east at Turkey's Rumania, leaving Trieste soft. This turn I drive on Trieste: F ADR attacks TRI with A GRE supporting via SER's flank, F ION/F NAP keeping the Ionian cohesive and A TUN convoying up to add weight to the Adriatic push. A turn or two out I want Trieste in hand as a fourth Balkan center, F ADR dominating the Adriatic, and Rome or Venice freed for a Winter fleet build before Turkey — now fat at five centers behind me — grows strong enough to turn on me.
RUSSIA
I'm stuck at 4 centers while France runs at 7 and Turkey, England, Italy all sit at 5, so I need to break out and the cleanest opening is Turkey's RUM, which sits next to my F SEV and Austria's GAL/BUD. I'll keep my northern wall intact—A STP holding with A FIN supporting and A WAR backstopping home—while pressing F SEV toward RUM if Austria will throw GAL and BUD behind me, retaking my old center and growing south. A turn or two out I want RUM back in my hands, my home triangle and STP secure against England, and a second push into BUL or GAL once Turkey is on the defensive.
TURKEY
I now hold five centers and need to break Austria's southern line before France's seven becomes unstoppable. This turn I'll push A BUL into Serbia with support from A SER's neighbors while keeping F RUM anchored against Russia, and bring F AEG and A CON forward toward Greece/the Aegean to pressure Austria and keep Italy honest. Two turns out I want Serbia or Trieste cracked open, my Black Sea sealed, and a sixth center in hand so I can pivot my growing eastern stack westward against whoever leads.

Instead I'll drive F ADR into Trieste anyway with A GRE supporting the attack and A TUN convoying up through ION to add a third attacker, betting Austria's army is genuinely committed east; if it bounces I lose nothing and stay poised, while F NAP holds the Ionian line. Going forward I keep F ADR dominating the Adriatic, press Trieste each turn Austria stays stretched, and watch Turkey closely since he's now stalling on the Serbia deal — I'll free Rome or Venice for a Winter build to deepen my wedge before he turns on me.
RUSSIA
I'll back Austria's strike by ordering F SEV to support A GAL into RUM, guaranteeing it falls this turn while keeping my fleet safe rather than throwing it forward into a contested square — Austria takes RUM now and owes me F SEV - RUM next Fall to recover my center. In the north I'll hold A STP with A FIN supporting it and keep A WAR backstopping home, preserving my clean borders with England and Germany. Next turns I want Turkey on the defensive in the Black Sea, my old center back in Fall, and an opening into BUL or GAL once Turkey's southern line cracks.
TURKEY
Russia and Austria have both committed to a three-strength strike on Rumania, so I'm anchoring it hard: F RUM holds with A BUL supporting it, and F AEG supports F RUM as well to over-defend, while A CON and A ANK cover the straits and home. I'm holding Bulgaria rather than throwing it at Serbia, because pulling A BUL forward would cost me Rumania to the Austro-Russian three-stack for nothing. Next turn, once Italy's Trieste push pulls Austria's wall thin, I bring F AEG and A BUL onto Serbia with Italian support locked, keeping the Black Sea sealed and growing eastward before pivoting against France.

Commentary
  • Austria and Russia executed a coordinated strike on Turkey's Rumania, with Austria providing the attacking unit (A GAL→RUM) and Russia supplying the critical support (F SEV supports), exactly as negotiated — rare follow-through on a multi-power plan that successfully dislodged Turkey's F RUM.
  • Turkey's last-minute pivot to anchor F RUM with A BUL (as messaged to Italy) backfired fatally: A BUL stayed in Bulgaria supporting F RUM rather than cutting A SER, which meant Austria's three-unit strike landed clean while Turkey's defensive recalculation left it isolated.
  • Italy directly deceived both Austria and Turkey: it told Austria it would respect the border while privately coordinating with Turkey to crack Trieste, and told Turkey it would drive A GRE→SER while actually routing A TUN→ALB via convoy — Italy got neither Trieste (bounced) nor Serbia, burning both relationships for zero gain.
  • Germany refused France's repeated requests to throw F DEN into the North Sea, staying entirely defensive despite France's promises of future concessions — Germany is quietly free-riding on the England-France grind while rebuilding with A MUN→RUH.
  • England and France reached a de facto standoff: England anchored the North Sea wall and France fortified Belgium with three supports, but neither advanced — France's seven-center lead is stalling as its western offensive consumes units without progress.
  • Turkey's retreat of F RUM to BLA keeps its Black Sea presence alive, maintaining pressure on Russia and preserving options against both Austria and Russia in the east.
