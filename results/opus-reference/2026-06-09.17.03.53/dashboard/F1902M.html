Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
The Spring RUM attack stalled — my A GAL is sitting in Galicia pinning Warsaw, and Turkey's BUL bounced off Rumania while Russia held. This Fall I want to crack Rumania for real: A GAL supports F SEV/BUL into RUM (or moves on RUM directly if Turkey takes BLA), with A SER backing the assault, while keeping F GRE and F TRI defensive to honor the Italian DMZ. A turn or two out I aim to hold RUM, push A GAL toward Warsaw, and keep VIE open for a Winter build to deepen pressure on Russia's south.
ENGLAND
Last spring's Sweden attack bounced because Russia's A SWE was supported by F BOT, so I need to crack it properly this fall. With Germany's F DEN supporting and my A NWY attacking SWE, plus F SKA convoying or supporting, I want to overwhelm Russia's Sweden — I'll push A NWY into SWE backed by F SKA's support and German DEN, while F NWG and F EDI hold my northern coast and prepare a build. A turn or two out I want Sweden taken for a fifth center, fleets aimed at Barents/STP to press Russia's north further, and the Channel still a quiet DMZ with France.
FRANCE
This fall I take Belgium overland with A PIC, banking my sixth center for a Winter build, while keeping A SPA and A POR-quality holdings in Iberia and F MAO/F BRE guarding the western approaches. Belgium is open and uncontested per my deals with England and Germany, both committed against Russia. A turn or two out I want BEL, SPA, POR, BRE, PAR, MAR plus a fresh build, then I'll weigh a western squeeze on Russia with Germany or a stab toward Germany's Munich/Ruhr if he overextends east.
GERMANY
Last Spring the Sweden squeeze bounced — England's NWY and NWG both failed to take it and Russia held with BOT support, so my F BAL is now in position to actually break it this Fall. This turn I'll push F BAL into Sweden with F DEN supporting and coordinate England to either back it or hit STP, cracking Russia's overextended north; A MUN and A BER stay home to keep Kiel open for a Winter build and watch Silesia. A turn or two out I want Sweden taken, a second Baltic fleet built in Kiel, and Russia squeezed between my Baltic push and Austria's Galicia pressure so I harvest his northern centers.
ITALY
This fall I bank my fourth center quietly while keeping every deal warm: I'll hold A TUN, keep A VEN defensive, and keep F ION central with F NAP supporting it, honoring the Adriatic DMZ with Austria and staying clear of Turkey's Aegean. ROM is vacant for a build if I can free a center, but with no growth available this turn my real play is to position for a 1903 Lepanto—convoying east toward Greece or the Aegean once Austria and Turkey have ground Russia down and the Balkans open. A turn or two out I want F ION dominating the Ionian approaches, an army landing on the Balkan or Turkish coast, and a fresh fleet at Naples deepening the eastern stack while my western and Adriatic borders stay frozen.
RUSSIA
This Fall I commit fully against Austria: A WAR pushes into Galicia (or supports a Galicia gain) to threaten Budapest and Vienna while F RUM and F SEV anchor the Black Sea against any Turkish slip into BLA. In the north, A SWE and F BOT stay locked to hold Sweden against Germany's Kiel/Denmark fleets, and A STP anchors home so MOS stays open for a Winter build. A turn or two out I want to be in Galicia pressing Budapest with Turkish coordination on Serbia/Greece, controlling Rumania and Sweden firmly, and building toward seven or eight centers — while watching for Turkey's CON/ANK fleets edging toward Rumania, which is my real eastern risk.
TURKEY
My RUM attack bounced last turn because Austria never threw A SER support and Russia anchored with F SEV/F RUM mutual support. This turn I'll commit fully on Rumania: A BUL into RUM with F BLA supporting, while A CON backs BUL and F AEG holds the south. If Austria genuinely puts A SER behind the RUM assault this time I take Rumania outright; a turn or two from now I want F BLA pressuring SEV and my units pushing into Russia's exposed eastern centers while Austria pins Warsaw from Galicia.
