SPRING 1910 MOVEMENT (S1910M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
England at eight is the runaway threat now, not Italy or Turkey, but I can't reach it directly — so I'll consolidate my eastern core and look to grow off Italy's collapsing south. This turn I take Albania off Italy's lone F ALB with A SER moving in backed by A TRI and F ADR, while A BUD and A VIE wall Budapest against any Russian Galician push, setting up a sixth center for a Winter build. A turn or two out I want Albania consolidated, Greece pried off Italy next, and my Russian and Turkish borders quiet while England's expansion forces a coalition I can join from the east.
ENGLAND
I'm at 8 centers with a solid northern bloc, and France at 7 with armies in MUN, RUH, BEL, PAR is the only power who can outpace me, so this is the turn to break the buffer and turn west. I'll take Berlin with F BAL+F KIE to deny France the last German center and consolidate the Baltic, while shifting F IRI/F LON/F NTH to pressure the English Channel and Belgium line. A turn or two out I want fleets contesting ENG and threatening BEL, with Berlin and the western Baltic firmly mine, squeezing France before its lead becomes unbreakable.
FRANCE
England is the runaway threat at 8 centers and his Baltic/North Sea fleet wall now sits one step from my interior, so this is the turn I stop feeding him and start prying. I'll hold my western wall — A BEL, A RUH, F ENG, A PAR all anchoring Belgium/Channel/home — while A MUN keeps Berlin pressure honest, and I push F TYS toward the Tyrrhenian core to lock the Med coordination with Italy against England's north. A turn or two out I want my Lyon/Tyrrhenian fleets working with Italy's Ion/Nap stack to threaten England's southern flank while my interior holds Munich and Berlin solid, building at home off Brest to add weight for the long fight against England.
GERMANY
I'm down to a single fleet in Berlin with England's wall in the Baltic and Russia's A WAR/A PRU at my doorstep, so survival is the only realistic play this turn. I'll hold F BER and try to keep Berlin one more year by leaning on Russia's stated preference that a German Berlin checks England's fleets better than letting it fall — my pitch is that I'm a buffer worth keeping alive. Realistically I expect to be squeezed out within a turn or two, so I'll talk England and Russia into leaving Berlin alone and look for any seam where their interests diverge over the Baltic.
ITALY
I'm stuck at four centers and my Bulgaria grab failed last fall, so this turn I either retake Greece's seam or pry Albania-Trieste off Austria, but Turkey's full Aegean stack (F AEG, F SMY, A BUL, A CON) is the bigger prize since he's only four centers and his BUL is exposed. I'll point A GRE-BUL again backed by F ION and F ALB-side weight, keeping F NAP and F ION cohesive on the Ionian, while honoring the Adriatic truce so Austria keeps bracing Russia. A turn or two out I want Bulgaria taken, my fleets angled into the Aegean to grind Turkey's south, and Rome open for a Winter fleet build to finally break my stagnation against the runaway English north.
RUSSIA
England at 8 and France at 7 are the real runaways; I'm stuck at 5 with a quiet but stagnant home triangle, so I need to break for growth rather than keep walling. Austria's BUD remains defense-two, but with Italy pressing Austria's south and Turkey hitting Serbia, I'll throw A GAL-BUD with A WAR backing for a genuine attack-2 this turn while keeping F RUM defensive on the clean Black Sea and A FIN/A STP guarding the north. A turn or two out I want Budapest banked, my home centers secure, and pressure ready to pivot onto Vienna or back north against England's Baltic fleets if his clean-border promise frays.
TURKEY
I'm stuck at four centers and Serbia has walled me five years running, so this spring I pivot hard onto Rumania: A BUL - RUM backed by A CON moving up to support, while F AEG and F SMY stay south to keep the strait and Italy honest. Russia's F RUM is the only soft eastern center I can actually crack with two-strength now that Austria's wall is solid, and a turn out I want Rumania in hand and my fleets positioned to read whichever of Russia or Italy overextends. England's eight and France's seven are the real long-term threats, but they're out of my reach — my only path to growth runs through Russia's Black Sea seam.

AUSTRIA
Italy has pledged to walk F ALB to ION, so A TRI moves into Albania uncontested for a sixth center while A SER stays home to wall Serbia against A GRE, and A BUD holds with A VIE supporting it to break Russia's GAL-WAR push on Budapest. F ADR backs the Albanian move to cover any Italian double-cross. Next turns I consolidate Albania, watch whether Russia honors keeping GAL defensive, and lean toward a joint Rumania push with Turkey while keeping an eye on England's runaway northern growth.
ENGLAND
I'm taking Berlin this phase with F BAL moving in and F KIE supporting, dislodging the last German fleet to deny France the center and consolidate the Baltic. My A DEN supports the Berlin push, F NTH/F LON/F EDI anchor home and the North Sea, and F IRI swings toward the Channel to begin pressuring France, who at 7 is the only power that can outpace me. Next turns I bank Berlin for a ninth center, then pivot my northern stack hard toward Belgium and the Channel to break France's lead before it grows unbreakable.
FRANCE
I'm anchoring my entire western wall against England this turn: A BEL holds Belgium, A RUH supports A MUN holding Berlin's approach, F ENG holds the Channel, A PAR holds home, and A MAR shifts to Spain to keep my southern coast covered and free a Brest build path. F TYS holds the Tyrrhenian as my forward anchor with Italy, ready to push WES the moment his eastern fleets actually swing west. The plan stays: hold the clean Munich/Kiel boundary with England while quietly setting up the Med pivot, and if Italy's fleets don't move west by Fall I reassess whether England is worth fighting at all yet.
GERMANY
I'll hold F BER, since it's my only unit and Berlin is my last center; moving it accomplishes nothing while holding at least forces Russia to commit strength to take it. My plan is to lean on the slim hope that England bounces Russia's A PRU to keep Berlin as a buffer on his flank, but realistically I expect to be squeezed out within a turn or two and will keep talking England and Russia into preserving me as a wall against the other. If Berlin survives this phase, I keep it neutral and watch for any England-Russia falling-out in the Baltic that opens a seam.
ITALY
I'm sending A GRE into Serbia while Austria pulls A SER off to grab Albania, since Turkey is also throwing A BUL at Serbia — that bounce means I may not land it clean, but if Austria does vacate and Turkey holds back I take the seam. I'll walk F ALB to ION to consolidate my Ionian stack rather than feed it to Austria's attack-three, with F ION and F NAP staying cohesive on the line. Going forward I lock my southern position, watch for Austria's wall to crack between me and Russia, and keep my eastern fleets ready to swing west with France against England's runaway eight.
RUSSIA
Austria has confirmed a defended two-wall on Budapest, so I won't throw a dead attack there; instead I'll hold F RUM in Rumania with A GAL supporting it to defense-two against Turkey's bluffed A BUL-RUM, keeping the Black Sea closed. A WAR backs the eastern line, A FIN walls the northern corner, and A STP holds to keep my home triangle intact. Next turns I keep watching for Serbia or Budapest to crack between Italy and Turkey while staying ready to pivot weight north if England's Baltic fleets ever break the clean-border promise.
TURKEY
Italy has told me straight it's driving A GRE into Serbia this turn, so my own A BUL-SER would just bounce them and hand Austria the center; instead I'll let Italy take the seam and read Russia's Rumania, since Russia refuses to budge F RUM and only ever bounces Budapest. I'll drive A BUL-RUM with A CON moving up to support it, while F AEG and F SMY hold the strait south to keep my home and the Black Sea line covered. If Rumania cracks I finally bank my fifth center; if Russia walls it to defense-two I've lost only a turn and pivot back onto the Balkans next spring.
