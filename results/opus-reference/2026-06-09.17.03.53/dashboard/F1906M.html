FALL 1906 MOVEMENT (F1906M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I took Rumania last turn and now hold five centers, so this Fall I consolidate: I want A RUM to dig in toward Bulgaria's flank while A SER and A BUD wall Serbia/Trieste against Italy's Adriatic stack and keep VIE open for a Winter build. Turkey is now my real target since Italy keeps probing Trieste — I'll push toward Bulgaria with Russian help over the next turn or two, looking to strip Turkey's weakened Balkan line. Immediate priority is not losing Trieste to F ADR while keeping my Rumania gain locked.
ENGLAND
I'm boxed at five centers with France runaway at seven and my fleets idle in the north while everyone else grows — that's a slow death I have to break now. The cleanest target is Germany's Denmark: I'll break the Baltic truce and throw F SKA into DEN backed by A SWE supporting the move, using F NTH/F NWY to wall the north, since Germany's F DEN, F KIE, F BER are coordinated but I can crack DEN with two-on-one if Germany doesn't expect it. A turn or two out I want Denmark in hand for a sixth center, fleets pressing the German Baltic coast toward Kiel, and Russia still quiet so I can keep grinding into Germany's collapse rather than rotting in place.
FRANCE
I'm stuck at 7 and grinding fruitlessly on Belgium against England's North Sea wall, so this turn I shift to actually growing: I'll bait Germany by vacating RUH so A MUN takes it, freeing me to swing weight, but more importantly I'll move A PAR to BRE to open a Winter build at home and consolidate my fortress at Belgium/Holland. A turn or two out I want F MAO and F ENG positioned to threaten England's Channel/Brest approaches while keeping my western centers banked, and I'll look to flip Germany into a real anti-England partner by finally giving him Ruhr as the concession he's demanded. The real prize is converting my Atlantic strength into a build and breaking England's lead before he or Turkey runs away.
GERMANY
I'm at four centers boxed in, and the path to a fifth runs through France's western flank: A RUH attacks BEL only if I can break France's hold, but realistically I should push A RUH to BUR to pin France's western armies while keeping F DEN, F KIE, F BER walled at home against any English Baltic move. My real opening is reclaiming pressure on Holland or Ruhr next turn if France thins out, so this turn I keep my Russian and English borders quiet and probe whether France will actually vacate RUH or Holland for me. A turn or two out I want A RUH sitting in RUH or threatening HOL/BUR, with my fleets ready to swing into the North Sea once England and France are fully committed against each other.
ITALY
Austria has stalled me at Trieste for three turns with the BUD+SER+TRI wall, and now A RUM has joined his line, so a bare Adriatic strike still bounces. This turn I commit a real three-unit attack on Trieste — F ADR - TRI backed by A ALB S F ADR - TRI and F ION supporting via the flank — while A GRE pins or supports to stop A SER swinging back. A turn or two out I want Trieste in hand as my fourth Balkan center, then a Winter fleet in Rome or Venice to deepen my Adriatic wedge before Turkey, fat at five and stalling on every Serbia promise, turns on me.
RUSSIA
Austria took RUM for himself last spring instead of feeding it to me, so I'm at 4 centers with no easy southern recovery — Turkey now holds BLA again and Austria sits on my old center. My cleanest growth this Fall is Galicia: with Austria's stack committed forward at RUM/SER and Italy pressing his Adriatic, A WAR - GAL grabs an Austrian-bordered land province and threatens BUD next, while I keep A STP/A FIN walling the north and F SEV defensive against Turkey's Black Sea fleet. A turn or two out I want GAL consolidated, MOS open for a build, and to keep probing whichever of Austria or Turkey weakens fastest while my northern corner stays quiet with England.
TURKEY
My F RUM was dislodged and retreated to BLA, so Austria now holds Rumania — that's a stab I must answer. This turn I take Rumania back: F BLA into RUM with A BUL supporting, while A CON-BUL or A ANK shores up the line, and F AEG covers the straits/Greece. A turn or two out I want Rumania recovered, the Black Sea sealed, and F AEG plus A BUL pressing onto Serbia alongside Italy as Austria's south finally cracks.
