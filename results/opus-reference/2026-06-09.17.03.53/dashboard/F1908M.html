FRANCE
Germany held Munich last turn — the S1908 strike bounced because I couldn't out-muscle his covered home, and England's Kiel attack also bounced, so Germany's still at 3 with F KIE and F BER. This turn I commit real weight to Munich: A BUR-MUN with A RUH and A PAR both backing it, while A BEL holds the Belgium line and F SPA/SC stays on Spain to keep my 8th banked. A turn or two out I want Munich taken, Ruhr held, and a freed hand to watch England's six-center north before that buffer stops paying.

This turn I'll drive A GAL-BUD with A WAR supporting the attack (not just chaining) to overpower A BUD if Turkey's Serbia assault cuts A SER's ability to slide back, keep F RUM defensive on the Black Sea, and pull A MOS-STP to open MOS for a Winter build while A FIN walls the corner. A turn or two out I want Budapest taken, MOS open for a build, and pressure pivoting onto Vienna as Austria finally cracks between me, Italy, and Turkey.
TURKEY
Serbia is now empty — Austria pulled A SER back into Serbia but my A GRE - SER bounced last turn and Russia took Budapest pressure; this turn I drive A BUL - SER backed by A GRE supporting the move, while A CON holds Bulgaria's rear and F AEG/F SMY keep the straits sealed. With Russia at Galicia and Italy in the Adriatic, Austria can't hold Serbia against my two-strength push, landing me a sixth center. A turn or two out I want Serbia and Greece both consolidated, my eastern stack pivoting against whichever of Russia or Italy emerges strongest as Austria finishes collapsing, while watching France's seven in the west.
