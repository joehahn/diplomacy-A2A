SPRING 1903 MOVEMENT (S1903M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Turkey took Rumania and now sits at five centers with a Black Sea fleet that will turn on me next — he's my real southern threat now, not Russia. This turn I take Galicia toward Warsaw with German pressure on the Baltic, hold Serbia and Greece firm to wall off any Turkish push into the Balkans, and keep Trieste home to honor Italy's DMZ so my flank stays quiet. A turn or two out I want Warsaw cracking with German help, Serbia/Greece anchoring against Turkey's Bulgaria/Aegean fleets, and Vienna kept open for a Winter build to deepen my eastern presence.
ENGLAND
I'm stuck at 4 centers and need to break Russia's Sweden, which both Germany and I failed to crack last year — Russia holds SWE with F BOT support. This turn I'll throw everything at Sweden: A NWY into SWE with F SKA convoying or supporting and F NWG backing the army, while pressing Germany to commit F DEN/F BAL against Bothnia so Russia's support is cut. A turn or two out I want Sweden banked as my fifth center and a fleet probing toward STP via Barents, keeping France quiet on the Channel and Germany as my Baltic partner against the Russian north.
FRANCE
I'm at 6 centers with a clean western position and now I want to convert my Iberian-banked builds into real eastward pressure. This spring I'll push A GAS to BUR to threaten Munich and stage for the joint anti-Russia drive Germany and I agreed on, while keeping A BEL holding Belgium and A PAR backing the Burgundy thrust; A SPA shifts toward MAR to free a build slot and F MAO/F BRE guard the Atlantic. A turn or two out I want a unit pressing Silesia/Warsaw alongside Germany's Baltic push, with Marseille rebuilt and Belgium secure, watching whether Germany or Russia overextends so I can stab whichever leaves me an opening.
GERMANY
My Sweden squeeze stalled last Fall — F BAL bounced and Russia held with BOT support, so this Spring I push F BAL into Sweden again with F DEN supporting to finally crack it, while moving A MUN toward Silesia to pressure Russia's Prussia/Warsaw line. I'll keep A BER ready to back the Silesia push or fill toward Kiel, and lean on Austria to hammer Warsaw/Galicia so Russia can't reinforce north. A turn or two out I want Sweden banked, a foothold in Prussia, and a joint Silesia-Warsaw drive with France hitting from the west as Russia collapses.
ITALY
I've banked two quiet years but holding is losing — Austria is at five with Rumania contested by Turkey, and I need to convert my eastern position into centers. This spring I'll launch the real Lepanto: convoy A TUN to GRE through Ionian to seize Austria's Greek fleet's center if it's open, or to ALB to land on the Balkan coast, with F NAP and F ION backing the move. Two turns out I want an army established in the Balkans, F ION dominating the Ionian and Aegean approaches, and ROM freed for a Naples or Rome fleet build, while France keeps Marseille frozen so I can commit fully east.
RUSSIA
I lost Rumania to Turkey last Fall, so my Black Sea front needs immediate stabilization: I'll throw F SEV at retaking RUM, ideally with Austrian or Black Sea support, since Turkey is now my real enemy in the south. In the north I'll hold Sweden firmly with F BOT supporting A SWE and keep A STP anchored, while A WAR stays flexible to either back a Galicia play or defend home against Austria. A turn or two out I want F SEV or an army back in Rumania, Sweden held against Germany, and a working arrangement with Austria to jointly squeeze Turkey, who broke the DMZ and took my center.
TURKEY
I took Rumania from Russia last Fall and now hold a strong five-center eastern block; this turn I want to convert that into pressure on Sevastopol with F BLA supporting A RUM into SEV, or anchor RUM and push F BLA - SEV directly while A CON and F AEG hold the south. Austria is now the danger at five centers and growing if Russia folds entirely, so I'll keep coordinating with Italy to lean on his Trieste/Adriatic while I grab Sevastopol to reach six. A turn or two out I want F BLA and A RUM established on Sevastopol and Armenia, watching Austria's Galicia/Serbia line closely and ready to pivot my fleets toward Greece if he overreaches south onto Bulgaria.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'm flipping fully on Turkey with Russia this turn: A GAL supports F SEV into RUM to help Russia retake it, A SER moves to BUL to threaten Turkey's center, F GRE moves to AEG to confront the Turkish fleet, A BUD holds to backstop Galicia, and F TRI holds home to keep my Adriatic flank covered against Italy's ION incursion. The plan ahead is to grind Turkey's Bulgaria/Aegean line with Russian help while keeping VIE open for a Winter build, and watch Italy closely since his fleet is now parked beside Trieste—if he presses the Adriatic I'll pivot Trieste to defend rather than chase the Lepanto.
ENGLAND
I'm committing A NWY into Sweden with F SKA supporting that attack, relying on Germany's F BAL to cut Russia's Bothnia support and F DEN to add weight, while F NWG supports the army in Norway and F EDI holds to anchor my Winter build. If Germany delivers on Bothnia, Sweden falls and I bank a fifth center; next turn I'll push north toward St. Petersburg via Barents while keeping the Channel quiet with France and pressing for that promised French Baltic fleet in 1904. If Germany defects and grabs Sweden himself, I'll pivot immediately to contest his new northern foothold rather than reinforce his expansion.
FRANCE
I'll move A GAS to Burgundy as the staging point for the joint Warsaw drive with Germany, with A PAR backing Burgundy and A BEL holding Belgium to keep my northern flank firm. A SPA shifts to Marseille to free a build slot while F MAO and F BRE guard the Atlantic. Next turns I bring Burgundy toward Silesia to support Germany's hold and we drive Warsaw together, while I watch whether Germany or Austria overextends so I can pivot my new builds toward whichever leader needs checking.
GERMANY
I'm sending F BAL to BOT to cut Russia's Bothnia support and F DEN supporting England's A NWY into Sweden, while pushing A MUN into Silesia to threaten Russia's Prussia/Warsaw line with France staging Burgundy behind me. A BER holds to keep Kiel open for a Winter build and to backfill toward Silesia/Prussia, and A HOL stays defensive. Next turn I aim to bank the Baltic squeeze, drive Warsaw jointly with France's Burgundy unit, and grab Prussia as Russia collapses between my northwest pressure and Austria's southern shift.
ITALY
Austria refuses to vacate Greece or support a real landing, so I'm done waiting on him: I move F ION to ADR to park beside his Trieste flank, hold A TUN in reserve in the central Med, keep F NAP covering home and Ionian approaches, and bring A VEN to APU to ready a convoy toward the Balkans. This sets up a wedge against Austria's Adriatic line next turn with Turkey's promised F AEG support pinning F GRE, after which I convoy into Greece or Trieste's coast. If Austria reinforces the Adriatic I'll reassess, but my whole stack is now pointed at his soft southern flank while France keeps Marseille frozen behind me.
RUSSIA
I'm driving F SEV into Rumania with Austria's A GAL supporting the attack, so I retake my lost center this turn while keeping A WAR off Galicia (pointing it toward Galicia's defense or south as needed) to honor the flip on Turkey. In the north I'm holding the Baltic line — F BOT stays put supporting A SWE in Sweden and A STP anchors home — refusing Germany's demand to pull back and daring him to overextend while England sits quiet. Going forward I want Rumania recaptured, Sweden held, and a working Austrian alliance to grind Turkey out of the Black Sea while I watch Germany for any actual Baltic aggression.
TURKEY
Austria has flipped and is now backing Russia's retake of Rumania while threatening Bulgaria with A SER, so my Sevastopol push is too risky to commit cleanly. I'll consolidate east: A RUM holds with F BLA supporting its hold to anchor Rumania against the SEV/GAL pressure, A CON supports A RUM via Bulgaria coverage, F AEG defends the south near Bulgaria, and F ANK supports F BLA to keep the Black Sea locked. Next turns I pivot south with Italy against Austria's Greece-Serbia line once my eastern hold is secure, since Austria is now the real threat to my growth.
