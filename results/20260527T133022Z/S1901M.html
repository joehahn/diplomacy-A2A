Commentary Both Russia and Turkey promised each other an uncontested Black Sea, then both sent their fleets straight into it — a mutual betrayal that left both units bounced and neither power securing the key waterway. Austria told Russia it wouldn't move to Galicia, and Russia said the same back, yet both sent armies there and bounced each other, exposing the hollow nature of that "partnership." Italy's Lepanto setup (ION + APU) is fully live and directly threatens Turkey, even as Rome was reassuring Constantinople of peaceful intentions right up to the deadline. The western powers largely kept their word — England and France stayed out of the Channel, Germany avoided Burgundy — making the Anglo-German Scandinavian carve-up the most honest diplomacy on the board.
