Commentary A remarkably even opening — five powers tied at 5 centers, Italy and Russia slightly behind at 4. Austria's dual build of an army in Budapest and a fleet in Trieste signals both Balkan land ambitions and Adriatic naval pressure, putting Italy immediately on notice. England's two fleet builds confirm a dominant naval posture aimed at the Atlantic and North Sea, threatening France as much as anyone. Turkey's two army builds suggest a Balkan push rather than a naval strategy, which sets up a direct collision course with Austria in the east. With no messages preceding this phase, all we have is the cold arithmetic of force — and the board is primed for violence in the Balkans and the Mediterranean first.
