Commentary
  • Turkey executed a clean betrayal of Italy: despite Italy's offer of mutual non-aggression and Turkey's own earlier "standstill" framing, Turkey told Italy point-blank it was attacking GRE, then did exactly that with F AEG support — dislodging F GRE and taking the center.
  • Turkey's offer to Austria was genuine and Austria took the bait correctly: Turkey vacated BUL to attack GRE, Austria moved A SER → BUL, and Russia supported it — BUL falls to Austria, exactly as Turkey proposed, giving both powers what they wanted at Italy's expense.
  • Italy was double-crossed by Austria as well: Austria coordinated with Italy on paper (asking for F GRE S A SER → BUL), but Italy's support was cut by Turkey's attack on GRE, leaving Italy with a dislodged fleet and nothing to show for the "coordination."
  • Russia delivered on its commitment to Austria, using F RUM to support A SER → BUL, cementing a Russia-Austria working relationship in the Balkans — though Russia's reward is modest given Austria, not Russia, gains the center.
  • The Anglo-French squeeze on Germany fizzled entirely: England bounced off HOL (Germany's F BEL held support as promised), and France bounced off MUN — Germany successfully held both centers by staying put and using F BEL as a support anchor, neutralizing the coordinated pressure England and France had telegraphed in their messages.
  • Italy's fleet retreats from Greece to the Ionian Sea, keeping naval pressure in the eastern Mediterranean rather than disbanding — a sign Italy intends to contest that theater aggressively.
  • The retreat into Ionian positions Italy to threaten both the Adriatic and the Aegean, making Italy a continued nuisance for both Austria and Turkey despite losing Greece.
  • Turkey should be alarmed: an Italian fleet sitting in the Ionian can strike Aegean, Eastern Mediterranean, or support future moves into Greece, complicating any Turkish westward expansion.
  • Austria, having apparently pushed Italy out of Greece, cannot yet celebrate — Italy's Ionian presence means the Balkans remain contested and Austrian southern flank stays under pressure.
  • With all major powers clustered at 4-6 centers, this retreat signals Italy is not collapsing but repositioning, and the Ionian fleet could become a kingmaker in the Austria-Turkey rivalry.
