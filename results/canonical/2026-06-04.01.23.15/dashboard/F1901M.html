Commentary
  • Austria promised Turkey it would keep A SER clear of GRE, and did hold SER — but only after Turkey firmly pushed back and Russia mischievously warned both sides of a likely bounce; Austria's acquiescence looks less like goodwill and more like a forced climb-down, leaving Turkey with GRE and Austria with no new build from the Balkans.

  • Germany took BEL cleanly while France (as promised) held BUR and England (as promised) went to NWY — but France was actively urging England to stab Germany by taking BEL instead, meaning France's "clean deal" with Germany was being undermined in parallel messages; Germany got lucky England prioritized Norway.
