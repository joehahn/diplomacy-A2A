Commentary
  • Russia stabbed Italy cleanly: Russia promised to support Italy's F BUL/SC → GRE but instead sent F BLA → CON, leaving Italy's attack unsupported and getting Italy's fleet dislodged by Turkey's A CON → BUL supported by F GRE.

  • France and Germany fought to a standstill at Munich: France executed A RUH → MUN with A BUR support exactly as promised to England, but Germany countered with A TYR → MUN as threatened, producing a three-way bounce that left both powers empty-handed despite elaborate messaging on both sides.

  • Italy's fleet retreats to the Aegean, keeping naval pressure alive in the eastern Mediterranean and signaling continued competition with Austria and Turkey for Balkan influence.
