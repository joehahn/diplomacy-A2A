Commentary
  • Austria told Turkey it would hold A SER to let Turkey take BUL cleanly — and did exactly that, while simultaneously moving A BUD into TRI supported by F ADR, reclaiming its home center from what appears to have been an Italian occupation without triggering Italian resistance.
  • Turkey delivered on its commitment to Austria, taking BUL with a triple-supported move (A GRE→BUL, backed by A CON and F BLA), while simultaneously stringing Russia along with false reassurances — Turkey told Russia it was making "no moves toward RUM or SEV" but never disclosed it was racing Russia to BUL, a center Russia had explicitly told Turkey it was eyeing.
  • Russia told Germany it would redirect F SWE to BAL to avoid a DEN bounce, and followed through — but this means Russia voluntarily ceded DEN to Germany and BUL to Turkey in the same turn, gaining only BAL and LVN while already the board leader; a surprisingly passive spring for a 6-center power.
  • France told England it would push A BUR into MUN as part of a coordinated squeeze on Germany, but the move bounced — Germany held A MUN in place, suggesting Germany read France's intentions correctly and prepared accordingly, leaving the Anglo-French anti-German coordination producing nothing concrete this phase.
  • England told Germany it was heading to NTH rather than contesting DEN, and honored that — but England's F NWY→SKA now sits adjacent to DEN, positioning England to pressure that center in fall even as Germany just took it, creating a quiet but real Anglo-German tension over Scandinavia.
