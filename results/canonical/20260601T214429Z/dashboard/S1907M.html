AUSTRIA
Austria enters Spring 1907 holding BUL, GRE, BUD, TRI, VIE, and GAL with six units — the priority this turn is to push toward RUM (attacking with A BUL and A BUD) to reach a seventh center while defending GRE against Italy's F AEG and keeping the northern core secure against any Russian or Italian opportunism.
ENGLAND
My absolute priority is retaking YOR from Germany (A EDI - YOR) while defending LON with F LON and using F ENG to either press toward BEL or support defensive operations, as Germany at 7 centers with A YOR threatening my remaining home centers is an existential threat I must address before anything else.
FRANCE
My core goal this spring is to finally break Germany's HOL-RUH defensive wall by pressing A BEL - HOL with strong support, ideally securing England's fleet cooperation, while simultaneously using my Atlantic and Mediterranean fleets to consolidate POR and threaten expansion toward WES or into German-held territory. I need to reach 9-10 centers to stay competitive with Germany and Italy, both sitting at 7 and 6 respectively and growing.
GERMANY
I need to push A YOR toward London or Liverpool to finish off England and reach 8 centers, while holding HOL firmly against France's persistent pressure and watching Italy's growing fleet position in the Mediterranean that threatens to dominate the board if left unchecked.
ITALY
My core goal this spring is to capture GRE from Austria using F AEG supported by F EAS, while holding ANK and positioning my eastern fleet to threaten SMY or CON if Russia or Austria overreaches — I need to grow from 6 to 7 SCs this turn or fall irreversibly behind France and Germany who are both at 7.
RUSSIA
I need to find a path to grow from 5 centers — ideally by pressing into BUL or securing a new center in the Balkans — while keeping my core (WAR, MOS, SEV, RUM) intact against Austrian pressure and watching Germany's runaway 7-center position. My key relationships are with Italy (who supported me into BUL last spring) and Austria (who remains a direct rival over RUM and BUL), and I need to determine whether a genuine anti-Austrian or anti-German coalition is achievable this turn.
