Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Austria's opening priority is to secure the Balkans quickly — Serbia and Greece are the natural first targets — while keeping Italy at arm's length and building a working relationship with Russia to avoid a two-front war from the start.
ENGLAND
England's priority in 1901 is to secure the North Sea and Scandinavia: F EDI to NTH, F LON to ENG, and A LVP to YOR gives me control of key sea lanes and positions me to grab NWY and BEL while keeping France from encroaching on ENG. I'll seek a working arrangement with Germany to avoid conflict in the North while pressuring France diplomatically to stay out of the Channel.
FRANCE
France's opening priority is to secure Portugal and Spain in the west while positioning to contest Belgium, aiming for 5 supply centers by end of 1901. I need to keep England and Germany from boxing me in, so early diplomacy with both is essential to buy time and ideally coordinate against a shared rival.
GERMANY
Germany's opening priority is to secure the northern neutrals — Denmark, Holland, and Sweden if possible — while keeping France and Russia at bay through diplomacy rather than conflict. I'll pursue a western-leaning opening that grabs HOL and DEN quickly, and explore a working arrangement with England against France or with Russia against a common threat.
ITALY
Italy's opening priority is to secure Tunis quickly with the fleet while pushing into the Balkans (Greece, Serbia) in coordination with Austria, and keeping Venice defended against any Austrian opportunism. I need to open a dialogue with Austria to establish a Lepanto or similar arrangement, and signal non-aggression to France to avoid a two-front problem.
RUSSIA
Russia's opening priority is to secure the northern neutrals (Sweden, Norway) with the St. Petersburg fleet while pushing south to grab Romania with the Sevastopol fleet, and to coordinate with or neutralize Austria and Turkey to avoid a two-front war in the early game.
TURKEY
Turkey's opening priority is to secure Bulgaria and the Black Sea while keeping Russia and Austria uncertain about my intentions long enough to establish a strong Balkan foothold. I'll aim for the classic Turkish opening — fleet to Black Sea, army to Bulgaria — and use diplomacy to prevent an early Austro-Russian alliance against me.

  • England and France successfully coordinated a genuine non-aggression opening — France stayed out of the English Channel as promised, and England avoided the Channel entirely, suggesting this détente is real for now.
  • Turkey kept its fleet in Ankara as promised to Russia, and Russia moved Sevastopol toward Rumania rather than the Black Sea — both sides honored the Black Sea neutrality deal, making the Russo-Turkish relationship the most trustworthy on the board this turn.
  • Italy telegraphed competition with Austria over Greece by routing its fleet through the Ionian, even as both powers exchanged friendly messages — Austria's Albanian fleet and Italy's Ionian fleet are now set for a Fall collision over Greece regardless of their diplomatic niceties.
