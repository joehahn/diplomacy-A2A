AUSTRIA
My priority this spring is to hold Serbia firmly while pushing into Rumania with A BUD or A SER, capitalizing on Russia's F RUM being exposed and Turkey's weakened position, while keeping F ALB and A GAL as defensive anchors against Italian encroachment from GRE and Russian pressure from the north.
ENGLAND
France is at 6 centers and growing while I'm stuck at 4 — my top priority this spring is to finally break into BEL by pressing F ENG - BEL with German support, and I need to decide whether Germany's F NTH will actually back me or whether I need to find a different angle to crack France's western position.
FRANCE
My top priority this spring is to finally take Holland from Germany by pushing A BEL - HOL with A BUR support, while managing England carefully — either securing F NTH support for the attack or ensuring BEL is defended if England moves against it. I also want to advance A GAS toward PAR or BUR to free up options, and keep my Atlantic fleets positioned to threaten BRE or press into the English Channel if needed.
GERMANY
My top priority this spring is to finally capture Sweden by moving A DEN - SWE with F NTH support, giving me 6 centers, while holding HOL firmly against French pressure and working with England to keep France from expanding further east or into HOL.
ITALY
My priority this spring is to push into Turkey's home centers — targeting CON or SMY with F GRE and F EAS while F NAP moves toward ION to extend my naval reach — and I need to coordinate with Austria to ensure we're not stepping on each other in the Balkans while Russia's growing strength at 6 SCs demands I keep an eye on their intentions.
RUSSIA
This spring I need to press Turkey's remaining position by moving F RUM toward BUL/EC or BLA while keeping F SEV and A UKR secure, and I should coordinate with Austria to ensure SER stays Austrian while we jointly threaten CON — meanwhile I'll watch Germany's A DEN carefully since F SWE must defend against any push toward Sweden.
TURKEY
My top priority this spring is to consolidate BUL/SC and push toward either GRE or CON to expand from 4 centers, while managing the threat from Italy's F GRE and F EAS and seeking a working arrangement with Austria over the Balkans to avoid a two-front war.

Commentary

  • England is being diplomatically outmaneuvered by both France and Germany simultaneously — France refused to support F ENG → BEL and Germany refused to spare F NTH for BEL support, leaving England static at 4 centers while both neighbors grow.
