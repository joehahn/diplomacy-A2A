My goal is to end 1901 with at least 4 centers (BUL + GRE ideally) while maintaining the Austrian and Russian relationships long enough to avoid a two-front threat.

Commentary
  • Austria successfully took Greece by ignoring Turkey's request to redirect ALB and instead supporting its own army from Serbia — a clean win, but Austria told both Italy and Turkey contradictory things about Greece, burning trust with both powers simultaneously.
  • Italy was double-crossed by Austria: Austria explicitly confirmed to Italy that F ALB would not contest Greece, then moved A SER → GRE with F ALB support anyway, leaving Italy's F ION bounced and empty-handed despite Italy holding up its end of the bargain.
  • The Belgium three-way bounce is the defining diplomatic failure of the fall: England, France, and Germany all moved on BEL despite bilateral messages with each other — Germany ultimately grabbed it with the support of A RUH, while both England and France were left out, and France's attempt to recruit Germany's support against England backfired completely.
  • Turkey's seizure of the Black Sea directly contradicts its assurances to Russia that the move was "purely defensive" — Russia now faces a hostile fleet on its southern flank and must respond, undermining what had appeared to be a stable Russo-Turkish arrangement.
  • Russia's position is quietly dominant: F RUM holds Romania, F BOT takes Sweden, and Russia enters builds at 6 centers while successfully staying out of every conflict this phase — a textbook opening that leaves all neighbors uncertain about Russian intentions in 1902.
