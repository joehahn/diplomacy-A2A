  • England at 8 centers and Russia at 7 form the dominant bloc; France at 5 must carefully manage this retreat to avoid being squeezed between these two growing giants.
  • The Ruhr placement gives France flexibility — it can pivot toward the Low Countries (threatening English interests) or back east toward Munich, making France a wildcard in the England-Russia dynamic.
