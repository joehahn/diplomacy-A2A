Commentary
  • Italy executed exactly what it promised Austria: F AEG cut CON's support chain while F GRE backed A RUM into BUL — the coordination was clean, but Austria's bounce means Italy's F AEG is now sitting in CON with no reward yet for Austria.
  • Austria's attack on BUL failed despite the elaborate three-power coordination because Russia refused to move F SEV to BLA as Austria repeatedly requested — Russia explicitly told Austria it would hold SEV to honor its Turkey deal, and it did exactly that, leaving Turkey's support chain partially intact.
  • England promised France it would support A SIL into BER, France committed and moved A SIL - BER, but England instead used F KIE to support A SIL — the bounce occurred because Russia's F BAL held firm supporting A BER, exactly as Russia warned France would happen; England's support was real but insufficient.
  • Turkey told Russia that A ARM would move toward SEV "regardless" as pressure — and it did attempt SEV, but F SEV held and bounced it; Russia's border settlement with Turkey held defensively even as Turkey quietly tried to bite into Russian territory.
  • Germany's A MUN supported F KIE toward BER (a void order since England never moved F KIE to BER), meaning Germany wasted its only unit on a miscommunicated coordination with England — Germany is now effectively irrelevant militarily.
  • France is the phase's clearest loser diplomatically: it told Russia it was moving A SIL - BER while simultaneously coordinating with England to do the same, Russia predicted the bounce correctly, and France is now exposed in SIL with nothing to show for it.
