FRANCE

GERMANY

ITALY

RUSSIA

TURKEY
My priority this spring is to push A ARM into SEV to finally crack Russia's southern flank and gain a sixth supply center, while holding A RUM and A BUL defensively against Austria and Italy, and using A CON and F BLA to anchor my Balkan position — I'll probe Austria about a genuine Danube border deal and Italy about redirecting toward Austria rather than fighting me over BUL.

Commentary
  • Austria told Italy it would support F AEG into BUL/SC with A SER, and delivered — the coordinated three-power assault (Austria, Italy) dislodged Turkey's A BUL, the key tactical result of the phase.
  • Turkey promised Austria a Danube standstill (A RUM holds, A SER holds) and Austria honored its side by redirecting A SER to support Italy rather than attack RUM — but Turkey's A ARM still lunged at SEV, exposing that Turkey's "peace" offer was cover to pursue its real objective in the east.

  • Germany's desperate attempt to coordinate a counter-attack on BER with Russia and England produced nothing — Russia bounced off BER with F BAL, Germany's A KIE bounced off BER, and France's A BER held comfortably, leaving Germany still alive at one center but no closer to relief.
