Commentary
  • Turkey successfully retook CON from Austria despite Austria's messages claiming the supported hold would bounce the attack — Austria miscalculated because Italy's F ION moved to AEG, threatening BUL/SC and cutting the support that Austria was counting on, delivering Turkey a genuine victory against Austria's explicit assurances.

  • Austria told Italy that TRI was "not on the table," Italy responded by bouncing TRI anyway — the negotiation failed completely, and Austria now faces a two-front squeeze with Italy pressing from the west and Turkey retaking CON in the east.
  • The MUN triangle collapsed into a three-way bounce: France pushed A BUR → MUN, Russia pushed A SIL → MUN, and Germany pushed A MUN → BER — everyone wasted their move and Germany survives another turn entirely because no one coordinated who would actually take the center.
  • England cleanly took SWE from DEN, reaching 9 centers, while successfully convincing Russia to back off F BOT → BAL — Russia's attempt to contest SWE was abandoned, and England grows while Russia stalls, validating England's diplomatic dominance this phase.
  • Austria's retreat of the Constantinople army to Smyrna signals Turkey is being systematically dismantled, with Austria consolidating control over the Balkans and pushing deep into Ottoman territory.
  • With an army now in Smyrna, Austria threatens to finish off Turkey entirely, putting Ankara and the remaining Turkish home centers in direct jeopardy.
  • Russia, also at 6 centers, will be watching Austria's southeastern expansion nervously — an Austria that eliminates Turkey and absorbs its centers could surge past Russia in the standings.
  • England's commanding 8-center lead makes them the clear front-runner, and the mid-tier powers (Austria, Russia, France) face a critical decision about whether to check English growth or continue pursuing their own regional conflicts.
