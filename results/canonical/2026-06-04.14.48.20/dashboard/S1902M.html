Commentary
  • Austria and Turkey both tried to claim BUL diplomatically while simultaneously planning to take it by force — the resulting three-way bounce (Austria from GRE, Turkey from RUM, with Italy also probing GRE) leaves BUL empty and both powers weakened heading into fall.
  • Italy told France it would support A BUR into MUN, then ordered A TYR to support that same move — but France never moved BUR to MUN, instead retreating south to GAS and MAR; the Franco-Italian anti-Germany pincer never materialized, leaving Germany untouched and Italy's TYR wasted on a void order.
  • Sweden became a three-way comedy of errors: England, Germany, and Russia all pushed for it despite extensive messaging, producing a triple bounce that leaves it unclaimed — none of the three powers trusted the others' assurances enough to stand down.
  • Russia is diplomatically isolated: Austria used Russia's anti-Turkey overtures to extract coordination promises while actually prioritizing BUL over RUM support, and Germany flatly rejected Russia's SWE claim; Russia's SEV-RUM bounce leaves it at 4 centers with no gains and enemies on multiple fronts.
  • France's southward pivot (MAR, GAS, MAO) signals a Mediterranean strategy rather than a continental one, quietly abandoning the anti-Germany coalition it was pitching to Italy — Germany remains at 5 centers and structurally unchallenged in the center of the board.
