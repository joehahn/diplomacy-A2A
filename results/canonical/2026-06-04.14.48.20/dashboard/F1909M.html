  • Turkey ignored Russia's ceasefire overtures and pressed A ARM toward SEV as promised, though it bounced — the Russo-Turkish stalemate on the Black Sea coast continues, but Turkey's seizure of BUL represents a net gain that Russia's diplomacy failed to prevent.

  • England's 9-center position makes it the dominant power on the board; whoever holds Germany's last unit as a buffer (likely France) has reason to let it survive briefly.
  • The Austria-Turkey dynamic is critical: Bulgaria's retreat to Serbia signals Austria is ceding ground to Turkish pressure in the Balkans, with Turkey likely poised to absorb what remains.
  • France at 6 centers faces a strategic choice — coordinate with England to finish off Germany and Italy, or begin positioning against England before the gap becomes insurmountable.
