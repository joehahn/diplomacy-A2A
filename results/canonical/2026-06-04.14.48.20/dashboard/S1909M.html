Commentary
  • France lied to England twice in the same negotiation: first claiming it would push A RUH toward BEL, then "confirming" it would take England's HOL deal instead — and then actually took BEL with A BUR support. England, trusting the final message, voided its NTH support for HOL and got nothing while France grabbed a center at England's expense.
  • England successfully coordinated with Germany to crack KIE: England's F HEL and F DEN supported A MUN into KIE, dislodging Russia's A KIE despite Russia's repeated pleas for a northern ceasefire. Russia's attempt to use the Turkey threat as leverage to freeze England's Baltic fleets completely failed.
  • Austria and Russia's coordinated strike on RUM collapsed entirely: both powers confirmed the joint A BUL + F SEV attack on Turkey's A RUM, but Russia ultimately held F SEV in place to block Turkey's A ARM, and Austria's A BUL also held — neither moved. Turkey's A ARM bounced harmlessly off SEV, meaning all three powers wasted the season in the south.
  • Italy successfully advanced on Trieste despite Austria's last-minute offer to vacate peacefully: Austria did attempt F TRI → ADR, but Italy pressed anyway with A VEN → TRI supported by F ADR, dislodging Austria's fleet — suggesting Italy never trusted the offer or simply prioritized the guaranteed gain over the diplomatic arrangement.
  • Turkey's F GRE → BUL/SC failed to bounce Austria's stationary A BUL, meaning Turkey's threat to seize BUL if Austria moved was real but its actual attack was repelled — Turkey gained nothing in the Balkans this turn despite aggressive posturing on multiple fronts.
  • Russia's retreat to BER rather than a safer inland space is aggressive posturing, keeping an army in the heart of Germany and threatening to hold or expand there.
  • Austria pulling F TRI back to ALB signals Adriatic vulnerability — the fleet is repositioning defensively, likely under pressure from either Italy or Turkey in the south.
  • With Russia planting a unit in BER, Germany's lone remaining center is now directly menaced, suggesting German elimination is imminent and the scramble for its final supply center is on.
  • Austria's ALB retreat puts a fleet in a position to contest Greece or support Balkan operations, hinting at ongoing friction with Turkey or Italy over the southeastern supply centers.
