Commentary
  • Austria successfully took Greece as promised to both Turkey and Italy, but critically refused Turkey's request to support BUL→RUM with A SER — Austria used that army to secure its own gain instead, leaving Turkey to succeed on its own strength anyway. A functional but self-interested partner.
  • Turkey delivered on the RUM attack without Austrian support, dislodging Russia's fleet despite Russia's defiant messaging — Turkey's F BLA + A BUL proved sufficient, and Russia's bluff that "you'll fail and waste the move" was called and beaten decisively.
  • Germany and Russia both attempted SWE, bouncing each other out despite Russia's late message claiming to redirect to SWE to "avoid a bounce" — either the message came too late or Germany never intended to yield, leaving Sweden unclaimed and both powers short a build.
  • England cleanly took both Norway and Belgium as coordinated with Germany, while France held A BUR rather than pushing to Belgium — France's final messages suggested it would take Belgium if England went to Norway, but England took both NWY and BEL leaving France with only Portugal, a diplomatic win for England at France's expense.
  • Italy's move of A VEN→TYR, framed to Austria as "aimed at Germany," quietly puts pressure on Austrian home territory — Austria accepted the reassurance, but Italy is now one step from TRI or BOH with a forward unit and a fresh build incoming.
  • Russia's fleet retreats from Rumania to Sevastopol, surrendering any claim to Rumania this year and leaving Turkey or Austria well-positioned to contest that supply center.
  • Russia ends 1901 with only 4 centers despite early ambitions, signaling a difficult opening that will require careful diplomacy to recover from.
  • The retreat to Sevastopol at least consolidates Russian naval presence in the Black Sea region, giving Moscow a defensive anchor against Turkish expansion northward.
  • Austria and Turkey both emerge from Fall 1901 in strong positions relative to Russia, making the Russo-Austrian and Russo-Turkish relationships the most critical fault lines heading into 1902.
