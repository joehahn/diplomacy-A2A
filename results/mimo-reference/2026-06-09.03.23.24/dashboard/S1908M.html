I'll negotiate with Italy and France to coordinate pressure on Turkey's remaining holdings while keeping Germany quiet on my southern border.
ENGLAND
I'm stuck at 4 centers with four units while Turkey leads at 7 and Germany at 7 — I need to break through immediately. This turn I'll push F ENG into BEL to finally seize the French center (France has been bouncing me for two years and their position is weakening), move A NWY into STP to grab Russia's last supply center, and hold F NWG and F LON to maintain my Channel and northern positions, aiming to reach 6 centers and become a credible force against Turkey's eastern expansion.
FRANCE
Turkey at 7 with RUM, BUL, SEV, MOS is still the board leader, and Germany at 7 with a strong central stack is my immediate threat — I need to grow while containing both. This spring I'll push A BUR toward MUN to pressure Germany's interior and force them to split their forces, while positioning my fleets to support either a Channel defense or an Atlantic push. By fall I want to be at 8 centers, with Germany weakened enough that I can coordinate with Italy and Austria on Turkey's Balkans in 1909.
GERMANY
I'm at 7 centers tied with Turkey and need to grow immediately — France's A BUR is the most vulnerable target since I can attack it from both MUN and KIE with support. I'll push A MUN into BUR to reclaim it and threaten France's BEL/PIC, while A WAR moves toward LVN to pressure Turkey's northern flank and A HOL holds to defend my Rhine position. By fall I want to be at 8+ centers with BUR taken and positioned to contest either France's western holdings or Turkey's eastern territories depending on how the board develops.
ITALY
Turkey at 7 centers with BUL, RUM, SEV, MOS remains the board leader, but France at 6 and Germany at 7 are also threats — I need to push into Turkish-held BUL from SER with F ION's support while using A VEN to pressure toward PIE or TYR to keep Austria honest. My goal this turn is to take BUL, dropping Turkey to 6, then position to contest RUM or push into Austrian VIE within 1-2 turns if they don't cooperate on Turkish containment.
RUSSIA
I'm in a desperate position with only F STP/SC and 1 center — Turkey at 7 holds my former MOS and SEV, Germany holds WAR, and the major powers have ignored my pleas for coordinated action against Turkey for two years. My only move this turn is to hold F STP/SC defensively while I negotiate aggressively with Germany and England to finally commit to taking LVN from Turkey, which is the only northern center I can realistically contest. If Germany won't move A WAR → LVN with my support, I need to find another path back into this game before Turkey's lead becomes insurmountable.
TURKEY
I'm at 7 centers tied with France and Italy, so I need to grow fast before a coalition forms against me. This spring I'll push A LVN into STP to finally claim Russia's last center and reach 8, while using my Balkan armies (BUL, RUM) to pressure Italy's SER and Austria's BUD simultaneously. By fall I aim to be at 9-10 centers, positioning to close out the game before France or Germany can consolidate western power.
