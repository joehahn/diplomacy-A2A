SPRING 1904 MOVEMENT (S1904M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm at 3 centers and need to expand aggressively — Turkey at 6 remains the board leader, but Italy holding TRI is equally unacceptable since it's my home center. This turn I'll push A BUD to SER to reclaim that center from Turkey while using A GRE to either support that attack or pressure BUL, and I'll demand Italy return TRI or face coordinated pressure from Germany to the north. By end of 1904, I want 5 centers with SER secured and TRI either returned or under active siege.
ENGLAND
France at 6 centers with BEL and RUH is the biggest threat — I need to break their Channel dominance and reclaim influence over the North Sea approaches. I'll push F NTH toward ENG to challenge France's fleet position while moving A NWY toward FIN or STP to recontest the Baltic, and use F NWG and F LON to support whichever attack develops. My goal is to reach 6 centers by end of 1904 by taking a French Channel province or pressuring their northern coast, positioning myself to challenge France's lead before they hit 7.
FRANCE
I need to push into Germany's heartland this turn — A RUH into MUN with support from A GAS (via BUR adjacency) to seize a key center and drop Germany to 5. Meanwhile, F BEL and F BRE hold the Channel coast to block any English opportunism, and my Iberian units stay put to secure my 6-center base. By fall, I aim to have 7 centers and redirect toward Turkey's Balkan holdings with Italy.
GERMANY
I'll push A MUN toward TYR to claim that center and threaten Italy's rear, send A WAR toward MOS to seize an undefended Russian center, and use F SWE to support A NWY into DEN or contest the Baltic. By end of 1904 I want 9-10 centers, positioning to threaten France's BEL/RUH and Russia's remaining holdings while keeping Turkey checked through Austria's Balkan pressure.
ITALY
I'll push A ALB to GRE to take Austria's last center and reach 6 supply centers, while A VEN moves to TYR to block Germany's advance and secure my northern flank. F TUN and F NAP will hold to maintain Mediterranean control, and I'll position A TUS to support defensive operations. After securing GRE, I'll pivot east against Turkey's Balkan holdings with Austria as a reluctant partner.
RUSSIA
I'm going to push A SEV toward ARM to cut Turkey's eastern holdings and threaten ANK, while moving A MOS to LVN to reclaim territory from Germany's occupation and position for future builds. My goal is to reach 5 centers by winter through aggressive expansion, either by taking ARM or pressuring Germany to vacate LVN, and I'll negotiate with Austria to coordinate a two-front squeeze on Turkey's remaining Balkan positions.
TURKEY
I need to push for growth — I'm at 6 centers and Germany leads at 7, so I can't afford to sit still. I'll target Russia's SEV with a supported attack from RUM (using BLA support) while sending A SMY toward ARM to secure my eastern flank and threaten potential expansion. By fall I want to be at 7-8 centers, ideally taking SEV and positioning to challenge either Russia's remaining territory or Austria's BUD depending on how the board develops.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'll move A BUD to SER to claim the unowned center, support that move with F ADR, and hold A GRE in place to maintain my Balkan position. Germany at 7 is the real threat now, but I need to grow first — after securing SER I'll pivot to coordinate with Russia against Germany's southern flank while keeping Italy from attacking GRE. My fall plan is to push toward RUM or BUL with Russian support and start pressuring Germany's overextended position.
ENGLAND
I'm supporting France's attack on Holland with F NTH, moving A NWY toward FIN to contest the Baltic, and holding my Channel fleets in position. This knocks Germany down to 6 centers while positioning me to expand northward, and I'll look to challenge France's Channel dominance once Germany is weakened.
FRANCE
I'm pushing A RUH into HOL with support from F BEL, and England is committing F NTH to support as well — that's 3 strength versus Germany's 2, guaranteeing the take. Germany's deal to hold RUH at the border is irrelevant since I'm taking HOL, not pushing east, and their units are moving toward TYR anyway. After HOL falls, Germany drops to 6 centers and I'll consolidate the Rhine while redirecting toward Turkey's Balkan holdings with Italy.
GERMANY
I'll take TYR with A MUN despite Italy's threat to contest it, since it's empty and a critical center for my expansion. I'll hold my other units defensively — A HOL holds with KIE support, F SWE holds to maintain Baltic position, A BER holds, and A WAR holds to keep Russia contained. This gets me to 8 centers if TYR succeeds, but I need to prepare for France potentially attacking HOL from RUH with England's support, so next turn I may need to redirect forces west.
ITALY
I'll move A ALB to GRE to take Austria's last accessible center and reach 6 supply centers, move A VEN to TYR to block Germany's MUN push and secure my northern flank, and hold my fleets in TUN and NAP to maintain Mediterranean control. After this turn I'll pivot east against Turkey's Balkan holdings with Austria as a reluctant partner, while France handles Germany's western front.
RUSSIA
I'm moving A MOS toward WAR to reclaim my home center from Germany, pushing A SEV toward ARM to cut Turkey's eastern holdings and threaten ANK, and holding F STP/SC to maintain my northern position. My plan is to take WAR back this spring while weakening Turkey's eastern flank, then coordinate with Austria in fall to pressure Germany's southern border through TYR if they don't vacate WAR voluntarily.
TURKEY
I'll move A RUM into SEV with BLA support to seize Russia's southern center, send A SMY to ARM to secure my eastern flank and threaten future expansion, and hold BUL and SER defensively with CON holding as well. This gets me to 7 centers and positions me to push toward 8-9 by fall, targeting either Austria's BUD or further Russian territory depending on how the board develops.
