FALL 1903 MOVEMENT (F1903M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Turkey is still the runaway leader at 6 centers, so my priority is reclaiming SER with A BUD while using A GRE to support an attack on BUL or hold position against Turkish pressure. I need to coordinate with Russia's push on RUM to squeeze Turkey from both sides, and I'll demand Italy return TRI soon but keep focus on the Turkish threat for now. By end of 1903, I aim to be at 4-5 centers with SER secured and Turkish holdings reduced.
ENGLAND
I'm pushing F NTH into HOL to seize that supply center from Germany while France attacks RUH, squeezing Germany from both sides. I'll use F NWG to support the HOL attack, move A WAL toward YOR to prepare for future Channel operations, and let Russia retake STP since defending it is futile. By end of 1903 I want 6 centers (adding HOL) and to position for a Channel push or further German pressure in 1904.
FRANCE
I'm pushing A RUH into HOL to cut Germany's northern position while F PIC supports from the Channel coast, and I'll hold my Iberian units to keep my supply base secure. Germany at 6 centers with RUH contested means they're stretched thin — if I take HOL, I reach 7 centers and force Germany to choose between defending MUN or losing ground elsewhere. In the next turn or two, I want to consolidate the Rhine border and pressure either MUN or BEL depending on England's movements.
GERMANY
France's A RUH is the immediate threat to my position — I need to either defend RUH with support or accept losing it and pivot south. My priority this fall is securing my Rhine border by either bouncing France out of RUH or cutting their advance, while pushing A LVN toward WAR or PRU to contain Russia and position for northern expansion. In the next two turns I want to reach 7-8 centers, ideally by taking WAR or BOH and keeping France contained west of the Rhine.
ITALY
I'll push A ALB to GRE to take Austria's last accessible center and reach 6 supply centers, while A VEN holds TYR defensively against Germany's threatened attack and F TUN holds to maintain Mediterranean control. After securing GRE, I'll pivot east to pressure Turkey's Balkan holdings — specifically SER and BUL — with Austria as a reluctant partner against the runaway leader at 6 centers.
RUSSIA
I'm pushing A SEV toward RUM to cut into Turkey's holdings while supporting F STP/SC to hold my reclaimed home center. Germany's A PRU moving toward LVN is a concern — I need to decide whether to defend LVN or press south against Turkey. My goal is to reach 4-5 centers by winter and set up a two-front squeeze on Turkey with Austria in 1904.
TURKEY
I'll defend my Balkan holdings by supporting SER's hold with BUL, push A RUM toward SEV to reclaim Russia's pressure, and send A SMY toward ARM to secure my eastern flank while keeping F BLA flexible for defense or convoy support. My goal is to hold 6-7 centers through 1903 and position for a push toward 8-9 by spring 1904, targeting either Austria's remaining centers or Italy's eastern Mediterranean positions depending on how the board develops.
