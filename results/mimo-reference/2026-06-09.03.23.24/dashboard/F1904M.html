FALL 1904 MOVEMENT (F1904M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm at 3 centers and need to grow — Turkey still holds SER, BUL, RUM, and my home TRI is in Italian hands, while Germany at 7 is the board leader. This fall I'll push A BUD to SER to reclaim it from Turkey (supported by F ADR), hold A GRE to maintain my Balkan foothold, and demand Italy return TRI or face coordinated pressure from Germany's north and my south. By end of 1905, I want 5+ centers with SER secured and TRI either returned or under active siege, while working with Russia to contain Germany's explosive growth.
ENGLAND
France at 7 centers after taking HOL is now the board leader and the primary threat — I need to challenge their Channel dominance and prevent them from running away with the game. I'll push F NTH toward ENG to contest France's fleet presence while moving A FIN toward STP or SWE to expand in the Baltic, positioning myself to reach 6 centers by year's end. Within two turns I want to control the North Sea approaches and have a path into either French Channel territory or the Scandinavian/Baltic region.
FRANCE
I'm pushing A HOL into KIE to cut Germany's support structure and seize a key interior center, while F BEL and F BRE hold the Channel against England. After taking KIE, I'll have 7 centers and can redirect toward Turkey's Balkan holdings with Italy, who's already pressuring from TYR.
GERMANY
France took HOL despite my diplomatic efforts, so I'm at 6 centers and need to recover. I'll push A MUN to TYR to claim that center and threaten Italy's rear, hold my other units defensively on the Rhine and eastern front, and negotiate with England to contain France's western expansion while I rebuild momentum.
ITALY
I'm taking GRE with A ALB this fall to reach 6 centers, then pivoting east to pressure Turkey's Balkan holdings (SER, BUL) while keeping my fleets in the Mediterranean. Germany at 7 is the runaway leader, so I'll seek coordinated pressure from France and potentially Turkey on Germany's northern positions in 1905.
RUSSIA
Germany at 7 centers is the runaway leader and must be stopped before they hit 9. I'm pushing A MOS to WAR to reclaim my home center while A SEV holds to prevent Turkey from taking it, and F STP/SC supports or holds to maintain my northern position. My goal is to reach 4 centers by winter and coordinate with Austria and Turkey to squeeze Germany on multiple fronts in 1905.
TURKEY
I'll push A SEV into MOS to seize Russia's home center while they're weakened at 3 units, support the move from BLA, and use A BUL to hold SER defensively with F CON supporting. My goal is to reach 8 centers this fall and position to challenge Germany's lead by threatening either GAL through Austria or pushing into Italy's TRI if they overextend toward GRE.
