SPRING 1901 MOVEMENT (S1901M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll push into the Balkans aggressively — A BUD to SER and A VIE to GAL to grab unowned centers before Turkey or Italy can, while F TRI moves to ADR to threaten Greece and support future expansion. I need to secure an early alliance with either Italy or Turkey to avoid a two-front war, and I'll offer Italy a deal on Trieste or Albania to keep them friendly while I consolidate the east.
ENGLAND
I'll send both fleets north to claim Norway and the North Sea while moving my army to Edinburgh, setting up to take Norway and Denmark in Fall and build a second army. I want to secure the northern flank against Russia and potentially work with Germany against France, keeping my options open for a Channel or North Sea strategy depending on how negotiations develop.
FRANCE
I'll push A PAR to BUR to pressure Germany and contest the center of the board, send F BRE to MAO to claim Spain or Portugal, and move A MAR to SPA to secure that supply center early. My goal is to secure two neutral centers (SPA and potentially POR) by fall while keeping Germany honest on the Rhine.
GERMANY
I'll push north to grab Denmark and Holland early with F KIE, while A BER takes Prussia and A MUN moves to Burgundy to pressure France or support a western expansion. My goal is to secure two neutral centers (DEN, HOL) by fall and position for Belgium or Sweden in 1902, building a strong northern base before turning south.
ITALY
I'll push into the Mediterranean immediately: move F NAP to ION to claim sea control, send A ROM to TUS to threaten Tunis and position for a Tunis grab, and move A VEN to PIE to pressure France or Austria through the Alps. My goal is to secure Tunis within the first year or two while keeping options open against both France and Austria, building toward a 2-center gain by Fall 1901.
RUSSIA
I'll push into the Balkans and Scandinavia to grab early supply centers: A WAR to GAL or UKR to contest RUM, F SEV to RUM or BLA to pressure Turkey/Austria, and F STP/SC to FIN or LVN to threaten Norway/Sweden. I'll negotiate with Turkey for a mutual non-aggression while probing Austria's eastern border, aiming to hold 5-6 centers by end of 1902.
TURKEY
I'll push A CON to BUL and A SMY to ARM to grab two unowned centers quickly, while F ANK moves to BLA to secure the Black Sea and threaten Russia's SEV. My short-term goal is to expand into the Balkans and Caucasus before either Russia or Austria can contest them, setting up a strong mid-game position with 5-6 centers by Fall 1901.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'll move A BUD to SER, A VIE to GAL, and F TRI to ADR, securing two unowned supply centers while establishing a strong position in the Balkans and eastern Mediterranean. This honors my deals with Turkey (they get BUL, I get SER) and Italy (DMZ at TRI/TYR), while setting up for a fall push toward GRE or ALB depending on how the board develops.
ENGLAND
I'll send F EDI to NWG, F LON to NTH, and A LVP to EDI, securing the North Sea and positioning to convoy into Norway next turn. With Germany taking Denmark and Holland and Russia conceding Norway, my northern expansion is uncontested, and I'll follow up by taking Norway in Fall and preparing to build a second army.
FRANCE
I'll send A PAR to GAS, F BRE to MAO, and A MAR to SPA, securing two Iberian supply centers while respecting my agreements with Germany and England. Since Germany is taking BUR and I've committed to not contesting it, I'll focus entirely on the southwest and aim to hold both SPA and POR by fall. Italy's move into PIE doesn't threaten me, so our western front stays quiet for now.
GERMANY
I'll move F KIE to Denmark, A BER to Prussia, and A MUN to Burgundy, securing two neutral centers in the north while establishing a western presence despite France's objections. France may try to bounce me in Burgundy, but I'm committed to taking it — if the bounce happens, neither of us gets it and I'll redirect in fall. My plan is to consolidate Denmark and Holland by fall, then use my northern position to pressure Sweden or pivot south depending on how alliances develop.
ITALY
I'll move F NAP to ION to control the central Mediterranean, send A ROM to TUS to position for Tunis, and advance A VEN to PIE as agreed with France. Austria confirmed the DMZ at TRI/TYR, so my northern border is secure for now. My plan is to grab Tunis in Fall 1901 while keeping both Austria and France cooperative, then reassess whether to push into the eastern Med or turn west toward Iberia.
RUSSIA
I'll move A WAR to GAL to position for RUM in fall, send A MOS to UKR to support that push, and send F SEV to RUM directly since Turkey confirmed they won't contest it and F STP/SC to FIN to threaten Sweden and Norway. This secures RUM as a near-guaranteed pickup in fall while positioning for further Balkan expansion, and I've honored all my deals — Turkey gets BLA and BUL/ARM, Germany keeps the Baltic coast clear, and England gets Norway unchallenged.
TURKEY
I'll move A CON to BUL, A SMY to ARM, and F ANK to BLA as planned, securing two new supply centers and the Black Sea. My deal with Austria holds — I stay out of SER and GRE while he takes those, and I've convinced Russia not to contest BLA or RUM. Next turn I can use BLA to threaten SEV or support further Balkan expansion.
