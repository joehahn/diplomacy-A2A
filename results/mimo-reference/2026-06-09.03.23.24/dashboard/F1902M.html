My goal is to end 1902 with 5-6 centers and position for a northern alliance with England against Russia, keeping France pacified by leaving BUR vacant as promised.
ITALY
I'll push into the Balkans this turn — moving A TYR toward TRI to pressure Austria's last accessible center while supporting A APU into VEN or holding it as a forward base. My goal is to grab a second center this fall (either TRI or supporting Turkey's squeeze on Austria) and position for a 5-6 center Italy by winter, then pivot toward either the eastern Med against Turkey or further Austrian centers depending on how the board develops.
RUSSIA
I'm in a dire position with only 3 units after losing F RUM to Turkish aggression, while Turkey leads with 5 centers. This fall I need to reclaim SEV from Turkey with A UKR supported by A LVN, push F FIN toward STP to retake my home center from England, and negotiate hard with Austria and Germany to contain Turkey's dominance. By end of 1903 I need to be at 5+ centers or I'll be overrun.
TURKEY
I'll push A BUL to SER to grab another unowned center and pressure Austria's remaining Balkans, move A RUM to BUD to threaten Austria's home centers, and use F BLA to support one of those attacks or hold defensively against Russia. By end of 1902 I want 7-8 centers with a strong position to challenge Austria for TRI and possibly Italy for NAP within two turns.
