SPRING 1905 MOVEMENT (S1905M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
France at 7 is the runaway leader and must be contained immediately. I'll push A BUD toward GAL to pressure Germany's southern flank while using F ADR to support A GRE into SER, reclaiming that center from Turkey and growing to 4. By fall I aim to have SER secured and be coordinating with Russia to squeeze France's overextended position from multiple directions.
ENGLAND
France at 7 centers is the runaway leader and must be contained — I'm pushing F ENG into BEL to cut their Channel dominance while A FIN moves into STP to grab an undefended Russian center, getting me to 6. Germany at 6 is weakened but still a factor; I'll try to peel them toward France rather than letting them recover quietly, while keeping F NWG and F LON positioned to control the North Sea approaches.
FRANCE
I'm at 7 centers leading the board, so I need to consolidate my Rhine position while preparing to push into Germany's remaining holdings. This turn I'll move A PAR to BUR to threaten MUN and KIE from the west while holding HOL and BEL defensively, and I'll reposition my fleets to prepare for a potential English conflict in the Channel. By fall, I want to have 8 centers and be positioned to take either MUN or KIE, forcing Germany to choose which interior center to defend.
GERMANY
France at 7 centers with HOL, BEL, RUH is the board leader and my most immediate threat — I need to reclaim BEL or HOL this turn to stay competitive. I'll push A RUH into BEL with support from A KIE, while A MUN moves to TYR to secure that center and A BER supports the push or holds defensively. By end of 1905 I want 8 centers, positioning to contest France's western holdings while keeping Russia contained in the east.
ITALY
I'll take GRE with A ALB this turn to reach 6 centers, move A VEN to TYR to block Germany's northern advance, and position my fleets to support Mediterranean operations. After securing these positions, I'll pivot east against Turkey's Balkan holdings (SER, BUL) with Austria as a reluctant partner while France handles Germany's western front.
RUSSIA
I'm in dire straits at 2 centers with only 2 units while France leads at 7 and Turkey and Germany each hold 7 and 6. My immediate goal is to reclaim WAR from Germany with A MOS while using F STP/SC to secure the north, then negotiate with England and Austria to form a coalition against the leaders. By fall I need to either take WAR or LVN to reach 3 centers, and within two turns I aim to be at 4-5 centers by exploiting Germany's overextension and Turkey's inability to defend all their holdings simultaneously.
TURKEY
I'm at 7 centers tied with France, while Germany leads at 6 with WAR and other northern positions. I'll push A SEV toward MOS to reach 8 centers and secure Russia's last home SC, while using BLA to support the move and keeping my Balkan positions fortified with BUL and SER holding. By fall I want to be at 8 centers and positioned to pressure either Austria's BUD or Italy's TRI depending on how the board develops.

AUSTRIA

After spring, I'll have 4 centers and can pivot to pressuring Germany's south from GAL in fall while demanding TRI from Italy as agreed.
ENGLAND
I'll support Germany's attack on BEL with F ENG, move A FIN into STP to grab an undefended Russian center, and hold F NWG and F LON to maintain my northern positions. This gets me to 5 centers while knocking France down from 7, and in the coming turns I'll look to consolidate my Channel position and push toward 6-7 centers by pressuring either France's remaining holdings or expanding further in the north.
FRANCE
I'll defend BEL against Germany's RUH push by supporting F BEL's hold with F MAO, while moving A PAR to BUR to consolidate my Rhine position and threaten MUN from the west. A HOL holds to guard the eastern border, and my Iberian units stay put to secure my supply base. After this turn, I'll coordinate with Italy on Turkey's Balkans while keeping Germany pinned in a two-front defensive posture.
GERMANY
I'll push A RUH into BEL with support from A KIE, while A MUN moves to TYR to secure that center, and A BER supports A KIE to ensure the BEL attack succeeds. A WAR holds with support from A BER to defend against Russia's MOS push, and F SWE holds to maintain Baltic control. After this turn I'll be at 7 centers with TYR and BEL reclaimed, positioning me to contest France's remaining western holdings while containing Turkey's eastern expansion.
ITALY
I'll take GRE with A ALB, move A VEN to TYR to block Germany despite our earlier talk — TYR is too valuable to concede — and hold F TUN and F NAP for Mediterranean control while A TUS supports A VEN's hold on TYR. Austria's offered to vacate GRE and push SER themselves, so I get GRE cleanly; in fall I'll push east toward Turkey's Balkan holdings with Austria as a partner while Germany sits at TYR as a buffer to the north.
RUSSIA
I'll move A MOS to WAR to reclaim my home center from Germany, while holding F STP/SC to maintain my northern position. Germany claims A WAR holds with A BER's support, but this is my only path to growth — if it bounces, I've lost nothing since MOS is threatened by Turkey's A SEV anyway. After this spring, I'll negotiate with Austria to coordinate a two-front squeeze on Germany's remaining positions and work toward reclaiming SEV from Turkey in fall.
TURKEY
I'll move A SEV into MOS with BLA support to reach 8 centers, while holding my Balkan positions with BUL supporting SER and CON supporting BUL, and keeping SMY and ANK in reserve. Austria's threat to push BUD into SER is bluffed—my BUL support holds it—so I can focus on securing Russia's last home center. After this spring I'll be at 8 centers tied with France, positioning me to challenge for the lead by pressuring either Austria's BUD or Italy's TRI in fall depending on how the board develops.
