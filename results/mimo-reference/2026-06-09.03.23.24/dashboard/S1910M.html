SPRING 1910 MOVEMENT (S1910M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I need to push into Turkey's SEV with A RUM supported by A GAL, while A BOH and A VIE hold to guard my northern flank and F TRI holds to maintain my Adriatic position. Italy has 5 centers and I have 5 — we're tied — but Turkey at 5 and Germany at 8 are the real threats, so I must grow eastward now before Germany becomes unstoppable. Within two turns I want RUM consolidated and SEV taken, positioning me at 6-7 centers to challenge the board leaders.
ENGLAND
Germany's runaway 8-center lead is the immediate crisis — they hold MOS, WAR, and DEN alongside their home centers, and if unchecked they'll reach 10 by fall. I need to coordinate with Turkey and France to squeeze Germany from multiple directions: I'll push F BEL into NTH to project power toward DEN and HOL, move A LVP toward EDI to consolidate my home centers, and send A NWY toward FIN to threaten Germany's eastern flank. Within two turns I want to be at 6-7 centers while Germany is forced to defend on multiple fronts.
FRANCE
Germany is now the clear board leader at 8 centers with a massive stack including MOS and WAR, while I'm stuck at 5 and need to grow immediately. I'll push A BUR into RUH to reclaim it from Germany (they're holding it with HOL support, so I'll need to cut that support or attack with enough strength), while positioning my fleets to threaten the Channel or Atlantic depending on England's response. My goal is to reach 7 centers by winter through reclaiming RUH and potentially pressuring GAS or other German-held territories, then pivot east against Turkey in 1911 once Germany is contained.
GERMANY
I'm at 8 centers leading the board, but Austria (5), England (5), France (5), and Turkey (5) are all close behind and could gang up on me if I overextend. This spring I'll push A MOS toward SEV to seize another Turkish center and reach 9, while holding my western positions with HOL supporting RUH to deter any French or English aggression. By fall I want to be at 9-10 centers with the east consolidated, then pivot to pressure France's western holdings or Austria's Balkans depending on how the other powers react to my growth.
ITALY
I need to break through into Turkish territory this turn — A SER into BUL with F ION support is my primary attack, finally taking the center that's eluded me for three turns. Germany at 8 centers is now the board leader and the real long-term threat, so after securing BUL I'll position to check their expansion while keeping Austria stable by vacating TRI in winter as promised.
RUSSIA
Germany now holds 8 centers including my home WAR and LVN, while Turkey dropped to 5 after losing RUM to Austria — the board has shifted dramatically in Germany's favor. I need to negotiate with England and possibly France to contain Germany's dominance, while holding F STP/SC defensively to keep my last center alive. My goal this turn is to survive and find an opening to reclaim a home center once the major powers start fighting each other over Germany's lead.
TURKEY
I'm at 5 centers and need to recover from losing RUM and disbanding two units — Germany's runaway lead at 8 with MOS makes them the priority threat to contain. This turn I'll push A SEV into RUM to reclaim my center from Austria while supporting BUL defensively, and I'll move F CON to AEG to position for future Mediterranean expansion. My goal is to reach 7 centers by fall and align with England and Italy to stop Germany from reaching 18.

FRANCE

If the attack succeeds, I reach 6 centers and position to push into HOL or MUN in fall; if it bounces, I reassess whether England truly committed or played both sides.
GERMANY

The west is tense — France and England are both posturing aggressively — but I need to keep growing east before a coalition forms to stop me. After this turn I'll reassess whether to consolidate or pivot west depending on how the western powers commit.
ITALY

RUSSIA
I'll support England's A NWY into FIN with F STP/SC, taking a center off Germany and beginning to contain their lead. This honors my deal with England while pressuring Germany from the north, and after fall I'll push for a path back to WAR if the coalition holds.
TURKEY
I'll push A SEV into RUM with F BLA support to reclaim my center from Austria, while A BUL holds defensively against Italy's threatened attack and A ANK holds. F CON supports A BUL to guard against any Italian push, and I'll reassess after seeing how Germany responds to their own threat against SEV.
