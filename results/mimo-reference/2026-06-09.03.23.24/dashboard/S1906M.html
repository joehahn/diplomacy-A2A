AUSTRIA
Turkey at 8 centers is the runaway leader and must be contained immediately — I'll push A BUD into SER to reclaim that center and cut Turkey's Balkan holdings, while F ADR supports and A ALB moves to TRI to retake my home center from Italy. After spring I should be at 5 centers (BUD, VIE, GRE, SER, TRI), positioned to pressure BUL/RUM in fall with Russian coordination.
ENGLAND
France at 7 centers remains the board leader and I'm at 4 — I need to grow aggressively or fall irretrievably behind. This turn I'll push F ENG into BEL to seize a French-held center while moving A FIN into STP from Russia's last remaining center, aiming to reach 6 centers and position myself to contest France's western holdings in the Channel. By fall 1906 I want to be at 6-7 centers with a strong Channel presence and pressure on either France's remaining western empire or Germany's northern positions.
FRANCE
I'll retake BEL from Germany by supporting A HOL into BEL with A BUR and F PIC, while holding my Iberian units to secure my supply base. After reclaiming BEL, I'll have 8 centers and position to pressure Germany's KIE or MUN from BUR and HOL. Turkey at 8 centers with MOS is the growing threat, so I need to consolidate quickly and coordinate with Italy on their Balkans next turn.
GERMANY
France at 7 centers is still the board leader and I need to contain them while growing to 8+ centers myself. I'll push A RUH into BUR to reclaim a center from France's interior and threaten MAR/PAR, while A TYR holds to maintain my position in the south and A WAR holds with support to keep Russia contained. I'm negotiating with England to coordinate against France's western holdings, and I'll aim to reach 8 centers this fall by taking BUR and potentially pressuring HOL or PIC next turn.
ITALY
I'm pushing A TRI toward SER to take a Turkish center and reach 6 supply centers, while supporting with F APU and holding my other positions. After securing SER, I'll coordinate with Austria on a joint push against Turkey's remaining Balkan centers (BUL, RUM) in fall, keeping Germany contained at TYR and France checked in the west.
RUSSIA
I'm in a desperate position with only F STP/SC and 1 center — Turkey holds MOS and SEV, Germany holds WAR, and England wants STP. My only unit is a fleet, so I need to negotiate survival: I'll offer England safe passage in the north if they don't take STP, and try to convince Turkey to let me keep STP while they focus on Germany and Austria. My goal is to survive to winter and rebuild, hoping the major powers keep each other occupied so I can eventually reclaim MOS or SEV.
TURKEY
I'm at 8 centers tied with France as co-leaders, with Germany at 6 and Italy at 5. This spring I'll push A MOS into LVN to threaten WAR and STP, positioning myself to grab Russia's last center and potentially pressure Germany's eastern border, while holding my Balkans and Black Sea positions secure. By fall I want to be at 9+ centers and start pressuring either Austria's BUD or Italy's TRI to break away from the pack.
