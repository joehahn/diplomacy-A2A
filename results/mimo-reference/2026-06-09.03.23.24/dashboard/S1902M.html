SPRING 1902 MOVEMENT (S1902M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Turkey betrayed our deal by taking Greece despite promising to stay out, and now sits at 5 centers with armies poised to push into my sphere. I need to contain Turkey immediately — I'll move A BUD to SER to block any western advance, send A ALB to GRE to contest or reclaim that center, and reposition F ADR to support the push against Turkey while keeping Italy satisfied with a continued DMZ at TRI/TYR. My two-turn plan is to secure the Balkans by knocking Turkey back, then pivot north toward Galicia if Russia becomes aggressive.
ENGLAND
I'll take Belgium with F NTH, move A LON to Wales to prepare for a potential push into France or support BEL, and send A NWY toward St. Petersburg to pressure Russia's northern border. My goal is to secure 5 centers by end of 1902 and position for a Germany-Russia squeeze or a Channel push depending on how alliances develop.
FRANCE
I need to push into Burgundy to challenge Germany's position there — A PAR - BUR with support from A GAS, while F BRE supports to either attack or defend as needed. My Iberian units stay put for now, and I'll look to expand toward Belgium or North Africa in the next turn or two depending on how Germany and England respond.
GERMANY
I'll push into Holland with A BUR and send F DEN to Sweden to contest it with Russia, while A PRU holds or moves to Livonia to pressure Russia's northern border. My goal is to end 1902 with 5-6 centers by securing HOL and possibly SWE or BEL, positioning for a strong northern base before turning south toward Austria or Italy in 1903.
ITALY
I'll push into the eastern Mediterranean to pressure Turkey's expansion, moving F TUN toward ION or TYS to project power while sending A PIE toward TYR or VEN to secure my northern border. My goal is to secure a second center this year — either by convoying A NAP to APU or pressuring Greece — while keeping Austria cooperative and watching France's western moves.
RUSSIA
I'll push A GAL toward BUD to pressure Austria's home center while supporting F RUM to hold against any Turkish reversal, and move F FIN to SWE to claim that center before Germany can contest it. My goal is to reach 5-6 centers by end of 1902 by taking Sweden and threatening Austria's interior, setting up a potential stab of Turkey once I've consolidated the north.
TURKEY
I'll push A SEV to RUM with F BLA's support to seize Russia's only southern center, move A GRE toward SER to pressure Austria's Balkans, and send A CON to BUL to reinforce my position. By end of 1902 I want 7-8 centers with a strong Black Sea grip, positioning me to challenge Austria for TRI and possibly Italy for NAP within two turns.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'll move A BUD to SER to claim the unowned center, send A ALB to GRE to contest Turkey's hold there, and hold F ADR in place to honor my commitment to Italy and maintain Adriatic flexibility. My plan for the coming turns is to consolidate the Balkans by securing SER and pressuring Turkey out of GRE, then pivot to coordinate with Russia against Turkey's eastern positions if they continue expanding, while keeping Italy cooperative on the western front.
ENGLAND
I'll take Belgium with F NTH as agreed with Germany, move A LON to Wales to prepare for future Channel operations, and send A NWY toward St. Petersburg to pressure Russia's northern border while my fleets control the sea lanes. Germany is taking Holland and contesting Sweden, so I'll honor our deal for now while positioning to challenge Russia's northern flank and keep options open for a potential anti-German coalition with France down the road.
FRANCE
Germany's offer to vacate BUR by moving A BUR to HOL is a trap — if I don't contest BUR, Germany takes BEL and HOL, reaching 6 centers while I stay at 5 with no path forward. I'm attacking BUR anyway with A PAR supported by A GAS, and I'll move F BRE to ENG to support future northern pressure, while holding A SPA and F POR to keep Iberia secure. England won't help directly but Germany's units will be split between BEL and HOL, making BUR vulnerable to my two-unit attack.
GERMANY
I'll move A BUR to HOL to secure my second neutral center, send F DEN to SWE to contest Sweden with Russia, and hold A PRU to guard against any Russian push into Prussia or Livonia. After securing Holland, my plan is to use the northern base to pressure Belgium or Livonia in fall while keeping France cooperative on the western front.
ITALY
I'll move A NAP to APU to secure that center from Austria as agreed, send A PIE to TYR to establish a forward position and honor the DMZ gesture, and hold F TUN to maintain Mediterranean control. After this turn, I'll reassess whether to push into the eastern Med toward Greece or pivot west depending on how France and Germany resolve their conflict and whether Turkey's expansion threatens my interests.
RUSSIA
I'm pushing F FIN to Sweden to claim that center, sending A GAL toward SEV to reclaim my home center from Turkey, supporting F RUM to hold against Turkish pressure, and moving A WAR to LVN to position for future expansion. This commits me to a confrontation with Turkey over SEV while keeping my northern flank secure, and I'm abandoning any cooperation with Austria since Turkey's 5-center lead makes them the priority target.
TURKEY
I'll push A SEV to RUM with F BLA's support to seize Russia's last southern center, send A GRE to SER to grab another unowned center and pressure Austria, and move A CON to BUL to reinforce my Balkan position while A ANK holds defensively. This gives me 7 centers by end of 1902 and positions me to contest Austria's remaining territory next year.
