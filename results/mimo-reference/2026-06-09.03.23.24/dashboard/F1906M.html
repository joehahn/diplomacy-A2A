After securing SER this fall, I'll grow to 4 centers and position myself to pressure BUL and RUM in 1907 with Italian coordination.
ENGLAND
Turkey at 8 centers is the runaway leader and I'm stuck at 4 — I need to grow aggressively or become irrelevant. This fall I'll push F ENG into BEL to seize a French center, move A FIN into STP to grab Russia's last supply center, and hold F NWG and F LON to maintain my Channel and northern positions, aiming to reach 6 centers and position myself to challenge Turkey's eastern expansion or France's remaining western holdings in 1907.
FRANCE
I'm at 7 centers and just took MUN from Germany, so I need to consolidate my Rhine position while preparing to push into Germany's remaining holdings (KIE, BER, TYR). This turn I'll hold MUN defensively against Germany's likely counterattack from RUH/BUR, keep BEL secure against England, and position my fleets to support an eastern push. By winter, I aim to be at 8-9 centers with a clear path to Turkey's Balkans through coordinated pressure with Italy.
GERMANY
I need to reclaim MUN from France to restore my home center and reach 7 supply centers, so I'll push A TYR into MUN with support from A BER. With A BUR I'll threaten France's BEL or PIC to keep them contained in the west, and A KIE holds to defend the Rhine. A WAR holds to maintain my eastern buffer against Turkey's LVN push, and F SWE holds to keep Baltic control — positioning me at 7 centers by winter with MUN reclaimed, ready to pressure Turkey's northern flank in 1907.
ITALY
Turkey at 8 centers is the runaway leader and must be stopped immediately — I'm pushing A TRI toward SER to cut their Balkan stack while supporting from F APU, and I'll coordinate with whoever will actually commit forces against Turkey this fall. My 1-2 turn goal is to take SER, then push into BUL or RUM with an ally to reduce Turkey to 6 centers while I grow to 6 or 7.
RUSSIA
I'm holding F STP/SC to defend my last center against England's A FIN push, since England has wavered on their commitment and I can't afford to lose my only unit. My goal is to survive to Winter 1906 and rebuild, hoping Turkey at 8 and France at 7 keep each other occupied while I negotiate for a path back — likely by aligning with Austria or England against Turkey once I have more units.
TURKEY
I'm at 8 centers leading the board, and I need to keep growing aggressively. This fall I'll push A LVN into STP to claim Russia's last center and reach 9, while moving A BUL into RUM to reposition toward Austria's BUD or Italy's TRI depending on how the western powers respond. My goal is to hit 10-11 centers by end of 1907 and start pressuring multiple fronts simultaneously to close out the game.
