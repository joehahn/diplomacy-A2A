AUSTRIA
Turkey at 8 centers is the runaway leader and must be contained immediately. I'll push A BUD into SER to reclaim that center and cut Turkey's Balkan stack, while A ALB moves to TRI to retake my home center from Italy with F ADR support. After spring I should be at 5 centers (BUD, VIE, GRE, SER, TRI), positioned to pressure BUL and RUM in fall with Italian or German coordination against Turkey's eastern holdings.
ENGLAND
Turkey at 8 centers is the runaway leader and I'm stuck at 4 with four units — I need to grow immediately or become irrelevant. This turn I'll push F ENG into BEL to seize a French center, move A FIN into STP to grab Russia's last supply center, and hold F NWG and F LON to maintain my Channel and northern positions, aiming to reach 6 centers. After fall, I'll redirect my Channel fleets against Turkey's expanding eastern empire or pressure France's remaining western holdings depending on how the board realigns.
FRANCE
Turkey at 8 centers with MOS, SEV, and the entire Balkan stack is the runaway leader — I need to grow to 8+ and become a credible anti-Turkey force. This turn I'll push A RUH into BUR to reclaim it from Germany (they have A BUR there but no support to hold it against my 2v1), while A BEL holds defensively and my Iberian units stay secure. By fall, I plan to coordinate with Italy on a two-front attack on Turkey's BUL from the north while they pressure from TYR, forcing Turkey to split their massive eastern army.
GERMANY
France holds RUH and BEL at 7 centers while Turkey dominates the east at 8 — I'm at 6 and must grow immediately or become irrelevant. I'll push A BUR into PAR to claim a French center and threaten BRE, while A MUN moves to TYR to position against Turkey's northern flank; A KIE supports A BUR into PAR to guarantee the take, and A WAR holds to maintain my eastern buffer. By fall I want to be at 7+ centers with pressure on both France's remaining western holdings and Turkey's Balkans.
ITALY
Turkey at 8 centers is the runaway leader and must be stopped — I'm pushing A TRI into SER to cut their Balkan stack while coordinating with Austria to take BUL in fall. I'll move F APU to ION to control the central Mediterranean and position for future support, while keeping A VEN and A TUS flexible to respond to Austria's TRI pressure or push toward TYR if Germany cooperates on Turkish containment.
RUSSIA
I'm in a dire position with only F STP/SC and 1 center — Turkey at 8 holds my former MOS and SEV, Germany holds WAR, and England has repeatedly threatened STP. My only move this turn is to hold F STP/SC defensively, as I have no army and can't reclaim anything alone. My plan is to survive and negotiate aggressively: I need England to not take STP, I need Turkey to overextend against France or Germany, and I need to align with whoever opposes the board leader so I can eventually rebuild when winter adjustments come.
TURKEY
I'm at 8 centers leading the board, and I need to keep growing aggressively while the western powers fight each other. This spring I'll push A LVN into STP to claim Russia's last center and reach 9, while moving A BUL into RUM to reposition toward Austria's BUD or Italy's TRI depending on how the western powers respond. My goal is to hit 10-11 centers by end of 1907 and start pressuring multiple fronts simultaneously to close out the game.
