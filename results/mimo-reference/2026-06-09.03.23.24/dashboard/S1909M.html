SPRING 1909 MOVEMENT (S1909M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm at 4 centers and need to grow — Turkey leads at 7 with a massive eastern stack while Germany sits at 7 in the west. My plan this turn is to push A BUD into RUM to seize a Turkish center, use A GAL to support or threaten from the north, and work with Italy to squeeze Turkey's Balkans. Within two turns I want to be at 5-6 centers with RUM and possibly BUL, positioning myself as a serious contender rather than a peripheral power.
ENGLAND
I'm at 4 centers with 4 units while Turkey and Germany both lead at 7 — I need to grow this turn or become irrelevant. I'll push F ENG into BEL to finally seize the French center (France has been bouncing me for years and their position is crumbling with A RUH disbanded), and I'll move A NWY toward STP to grab Russia's last supply center before Turkey's A LVN does. By fall I need to be at 6 centers to have any chance of challenging the two leaders.
FRANCE
Turkey at 7 centers with a massive eastern stack (RUM, BUL, SEV, MOS) is the board leader and growing, while Germany at 7 with RUH, HOL, KIE, MUN, BER, LVN, WAR is consolidated in the center. I'm at 6 and need to grow immediately — I'll push into Germany's RUH with A BEL supported by A BRE, while positioning my fleets to threaten either the Channel or the Atlantic depending on England's response, and I want to coordinate with Italy and Austria on a two-front squeeze of Turkey's Balkans by fall 1909.
GERMANY
I'm at 7 centers tied with Turkey and need to grow immediately — Turkey's stack at ANK, SMY, CON, BUL, SEV, MOS, F BLA gives them eastern dominance and they're expanding into Russia's last center. This spring I'll push A LVN into MOS to seize a Turkish-held center (rising to 8 while Turkey drops to 6), supported by F SWE if needed, while my western units hold to maintain my Rhine and Scandinavian positions. By fall I want to be at 8+ centers with MOS taken, positioned to pressure Turkey's remaining Balkans (BUL, SEV) and potentially France's western holdings depending on how the board develops.
ITALY
Turkey at 7 centers with BUL, RUM, SEV, MOS remains the board leader, and Germany at 7 is tied with them — I need to break into the Balkans this turn by pushing A SER into BUL with fleet support while coordinating with Austria to contest RUM. My medium-term plan is to secure BUL and RUM within two turns, reaching 7 centers myself, then pivot to check whichever western power (France or Germany) is growing fastest. I'll negotiate with Austria for coordinated attacks on Turkish BUL and RUM while keeping France and Germany focused on each other rather than my Mediterranean expansion.
RUSSIA
Turkey at 7 is the runaway leader and Germany just took LVN off them, dropping Turkey to 6 — but Germany rose to 8 and now holds WAR, LVN, and most of the north. I'm stuck at 1 center with only F STP/SC and need to survive while the major powers fight each other. My plan this turn is to hold F STP/SC defensively on STP, negotiate with England to keep the peace in the north, and push Germany to continue pressuring Turkey's southern and Balkan fronts so Turkey keeps losing centers while I stay alive and look for an opening to reclaim MOS or SEV once the board reshuffles.
TURKEY
I'm at 7 centers and need to grow aggressively before Germany (7) or France (6) pull ahead. This spring I'll push A SEV into ARM to claim an undefended Turkish home center and threaten Smyrna's flank, while A MOS moves to LVN to pressure Germany's eastern border and force them to split attention. I'll also push A BUL into GRE to reclaim an Austrian-held center and expand my Balkan position toward Italy's SER.

TURKEY
