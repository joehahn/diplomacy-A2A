FALL 1909 MOVEMENT (F1909M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm at 4 centers and need to grow — Turkey leads at 7 and Germany at 7, so I must seize ground now rather than wait. This fall I'll push A RUM into SEV to cut Turkey's eastern holdings while supporting Italy's BUL attack, aiming to reach 5-6 centers and position for further Balkan gains in 1910.
ENGLAND
I'm taking BEL with F ENG this fall — France won't contest it after their agreement to stay out of the Channel. A NWY holds in place to maintain my northern position and keep options open for STP or FIN later. Once I hit 5 centers, I'll push east against Turkey's northern holdings in 1910, using my Channel fleets to project power into the North Sea and beyond.
FRANCE
Turkey at 7 with RUM, BUL, SEV, MOS remains the board leader, and Germany at 7 with a strong central stack is the second threat — I need to grow while containing both. I'll push A BEL into RUH to reclaim it from Germany (they're withdrawing east), while keeping my fleets and Iberian units in defensive positions to hold my 6 centers. By winter I want to be at 7 centers with RUH reclaimed, positioned to coordinate with Italy and Austria on pressuring Turkey's Balkans in 1910.
GERMANY
Turkey's LVN→MOS bounce last spring and Italy's BUL bounce mean Turkey still holds 7 centers — I'm still tied for the lead and need to break out. This fall I'll push A LVN into MOS again (now potentially with Russia's support cut since Turkey holds it), while sending A RUH toward BUR to pressure France's western position, and coordinating with Italy to attack Turkish BUL from SER. By winter I want 8-9 centers with either MOS or BUR taken, positioned to squeeze Turkey from multiple directions in 1910.
ITALY
I need to break through into Turkey's Balkans — A SER into BUL with F ION support is my primary attack to cut Turkey from 7 to 6 centers. A VEN holds to guard against Austrian aggression from TRI/GAL, while F NAP and F TUN maintain Mediterranean control. After securing BUL, I'll pivot toward RUM in fall or winter to push Turkey further back and reach 7 centers myself.
RUSSIA
Turkey is the runaway leader at 7 centers and must be stopped; I'll hold F STP/SC on STP to keep my last center alive while pushing Germany to continue pressuring Turkey's southern fronts. My goal is to survive and rebuild — I need Germany to eventually return WAR, and I need Turkey to keep losing centers to the major powers so the board reshuffles enough for me to reclaim MOS or SEV in the coming turns.
TURKEY
I'm at 7 centers tied with Germany, and my STP push bounced again — I need to pivot. This fall I'll push A MOS into STP with support from F BLA to finally claim Russia's last center and reach 8, while defending BUL against Italy's threatened attack by supporting it from F CON. I'll hold SEV, ANK, and SMY defensively and reassess whether to pressure Italy's SER or Austria's GAL once I secure the north.

After this fall, I'll reassess whether to push further into Germany or redirect east against Turkey depending on how the board shakes out.

Austria confirmed they'll hold still and not interfere, so BUL should fall this turn — then I vacate TRI in winter as promised, reaching 6 centers and positioning to push toward RUM or GRE next year while Turkey crumbles.

Germany confirmed they're pushing A LVN → MOS with my support, but my support was already committed last spring and bounced — this turn I'm supporting again with F STP/SC S A LVN - MOS to help weaken Turkey's grip on the north while I survive and wait for the board to shift enough to reclaim my home centers.
TURKEY
I'm pushing A MOS into STP with F BLA support to finally claim Russia's last center and reach 8, while defending BUL from Italy's threatened attack by holding it with support from F CON. A SEV holds to guard my southern flank, and A ANK and A SMY hold defensively. My plan for spring 1910 is to pressure Italy's SER or Austria's GAL once I secure the north, depending on how the board develops after winter.
