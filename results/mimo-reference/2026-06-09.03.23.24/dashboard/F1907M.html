FALL 1907 MOVEMENT (F1907M)

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll push A BUD into RUM to seize a Turkish center while they're reeling from losing SER, move A TRI to VIE to reposition for future builds, and hold F ADR defensively to protect my Adriatic flank. My goal is to reach 4 centers this fall and position myself to challenge Turkey's remaining Balkan holdings in 1908.
ENGLAND
Turkey at 8 centers is the runaway leader and I'm stuck at 4 — I've bounced off both BEL and STP for multiple turns and need to break through somewhere this fall. I'll push F ENG into BEL (France has been holding it but their western position is weakening with A RUH dislodged again) and move A FIN into STP while Russia's F STP/SC holds, creating a double stack that either bounces Turkey's A LVN or lets me grab STP if Turkey doesn't commit. By winter I need to be at 5-6 centers or I'm irrelevant to the Turkey crisis.
FRANCE
Turkey at 8 centers with RUM, BUL, SEV, MOS is the runaway leader and the only power I need to worry about — Germany is contained at 6 and England can't break through BEL. This fall I'm going to push into Germany's interior to grow, taking either KIE or MUN with support from my Rhine units, while keeping BEL and HOL secure. By winter I want to be at 8-9 centers and positioned to coordinate with Italy and Austria on a two-front squeeze of Turkey's Balkans in 1908.
GERMANY
I need to reclaim MUN from France's HOL position and push east against Turkey before they consolidate at 9 centers. I'll move A RUH into BUR to threaten France's western holdings while positioning A TYR toward MUN or BOH to pressure Turkey's northern flank. My goal is to reach 7+ centers by winter and set up a coordinated push against Turkey's Balkans in 1908.
ITALY
Turkey at 8 centers with RUM, BUL, SEV, MOS, and their home stack is the runaway leader — I need to push into BUL from SER this fall with support from F ION to cut their Balkan stack further. I'll hold A VEN and A TUS defensively to guard against Austria's TRI ambitions, while F TUN maintains Mediterranean control. In the next turn or two, I aim to take BUL and position for RUM, coordinating with Austria on Turkish containment while keeping Germany and France from growing unchecked.
RUSSIA
Turkey at 8 is the runaway leader and my only realistic path back into this game is to survive at STP while the major powers — France at 7, Germany at 6 — keep each other occupied and eventually turn on Turkey. I'll hold F STP/SC to defend my last center, continuing to negotiate with England to avoid losing STP while pushing Germany and Austria to pressure Turkey's eastern and Balkan fronts. My goal is to survive to Winter 1907, rebuild if possible, and position myself to reclaim MOS or SEV once Turkey starts losing centers to coordinated pressure from multiple directions.
TURKEY
I'm at 8 centers after failing to take STP again, so I need to pivot aggressively. This fall I'll push A LVN into STP to finally claim Russia's last center and reach 9, while moving A BUL into SER to reposition toward Austria's BUD or Italy's TRI. My goal is to hit 10+ centers by end of 1908 and start pressuring multiple fronts to close out the game before France's 7-center position becomes unstoppable.
