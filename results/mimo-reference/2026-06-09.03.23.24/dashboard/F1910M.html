After taking RUH I reach 6 centers and position to push into HOL or MUN in fall, with the coalition squeezing Germany from multiple directions before they can consolidate their eastern gains.

I'm at 8 centers leading the board, but France attacked RUH last spring (bounced off HOL's support) and England is posturing in the Channel — the western threat is real but manageable if I defend smartly. This fall I'll push A MOS toward SEV again to seize another Turkish center and reach 9, while defending RUH with HOL's support against any renewed French attack and keeping my western units in place. By winter I want 9-10 centers with the east consolidated, then pivot to pressure France's western holdings or England's BEL depending on how the coalition develops.

I took GRE from Turkey last turn, reaching 6 centers, but Germany at 8 is pulling away as the board leader and I need to join the coalition against them. This turn I'll push A GRE toward BUL to threaten Turkey's remaining Balkans while positioning A VEN to move into TYR or PIE to pressure Germany's southern flank — I need to coordinate with France and England on a multi-front attack on Germany before they reach 10 centers.

England took FIN off Germany in spring, dropping Germany to 7, and I need to keep pressuring their north while building a broader coalition. I'll hold F STP/SC defensively this fall to protect my last center, since England's A NWY is now in FIN and there's no immediate move I can make to reclaim territory. My goal is to survive through winter while Germany fights on multiple fronts, then push for a path back to WAR or SEV once the major powers weaken each other further.
TURKEY
I'm at 5 centers and need to reclaim RUM from Austria while defending SEV from Germany's A MOS threat. This fall I'll attack RUM from SEV with BLA support to recover my center and stabilize at 6, while BUL holds defensively against Italy's potential push and ANK guards the home front. My goal is to reach 7 centers by winter and align with England and Italy to contain Germany's runaway lead at 8.
