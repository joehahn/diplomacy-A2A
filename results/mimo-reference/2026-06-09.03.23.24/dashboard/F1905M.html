FALL 1905 MOVEMENT (F1905M)

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Italy took TRI by retreating there after being dislodged from ALB — that's my home center in enemy hands, and I need it back. This fall I'll push A ALB into TRI to reclaim it, supported by F ADR, while moving A BUD to SER to take that center from Turkey and grow to 5 centers. By winter I'll be at 5 with SER and TRI, positioned to pressure BUL/RUM next year while France's 7-center lead remains the board's real threat.
ENGLAND
I'm supporting Germany's attack on BEL with F ENG to knock France down from 7 centers, while A FIN moves into STP to grab an undefended Russian center and grow to 5. After this fall I'll have BEL influence and a northern foothold, positioning me to challenge France's remaining Channel holdings and push toward 6-7 centers by spring 1906.
FRANCE
I need to retake BEL from Germany while consolidating my Rhine position — A BUR will support A HOL into BEL to reclaim it with 2v1 strength, while F PIC holds defensively and my Iberian units stay secure. After re-taking BEL, I'll have 8 centers and can pressure Germany's remaining holdings (KIE, MUN) from BUR and HOL while coordinating with Italy on Turkey's Balkans.
GERMANY
I'll push A MUN to TYR to claim that center and reach 7, while A RUH moves into BEL with support from A KIE to reclaim it from France — F ENG's support should guarantee the take. A BER holds with support from A WAR to defend the eastern front, and F SWE holds to maintain Baltic control. After this turn I'll be at 8 centers with TYR and BEL, positioning to pressure France's remaining western holdings and contain Turkey's eastern expansion.
ITALY
I'll push A TRI toward SER to pressure Turkey's Balkan holdings while supporting with my fleets in the Mediterranean, aiming to take SER this fall and reach 6 supply centers. From there, I'll coordinate with Austria on a joint push against Turkey's remaining Balkan centers (BUL, RUM) in 1906, while keeping Germany contained in the north.
RUSSIA
I'm in a dire position at 2 centers with only 2 units, so I need to grow immediately or I'll be eliminated. I'll move A MOS to WAR to reclaim my home center from Germany — even if it bounces, it keeps pressure on them — and hold F STP/SC to protect my last northern center. My goal over the next two turns is to reach 4 centers by taking WAR now and then negotiating for either LVN or SEV, leveraging France's dominance at 7 to keep Germany and Turkey distracted.
TURKEY
I'm at 7 centers tied with France, while Germany leads at 6 with WAR and other northern positions. I failed to take MOS again this spring — Russia bounced me — so I need to regroup and find a different path to growth. My plan this fall is to push A SEV into MOS with BLA support to finally secure Russia's last home center, while keeping my Balkan positions fortified and preparing to pressure Austria's BUD or Italy's TRI once I reach 8 centers.
