FALL 1901 MOVEMENT (F1901M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll push A SER to GRE and A VIE to BUD to claim two more Balkan centers while I have the chance, and reposition F ADR to support or threaten Italy if they break the DMZ. By winter I should have 5 centers and be positioned to contest the eastern Mediterranean or push toward Albania depending on how Turkey and Italy behave.
ENGLAND
I'll convoy A EDI to Norway via F NTH and F NWG to claim that supply center, while positioning F NTH to support the convoy or move to Belgium if France leaves it open. By winter I should have 4 centers and a build, giving me a strong northern base to project power into Scandinavia and the Low Countries next year.
FRANCE
I'll secure Portugal with F MAO and push A SPA toward POR to claim a second Iberian center, while holding GAS to maintain pressure on BUR and keep Germany honest. My A GAS can support a future move into BUR or defend PAR depending on Germany's next move. By end of 1901 I want SPA and POR locked down with three units positioned to contest the western center of the board heading into 1902.
GERMANY
I'll take Holland with A BUR - HOL to secure my second neutral center, move F DEN - SWE to contest Sweden and pressure Russia's northern ambitions, and hold A PRU to guard against any Russian push into Prussia or Livonia. My goal is to end 1901 with 5 supply centers (DEN, HOL, BER, KIE, MUN) and position for Belgium or Sweden in 1902 while keeping France cooperative and Russia contained.
ITALY
I'll grab Tunis with A TUS - TUN and F ION supporting the move, while advancing A PIE toward either MAR or TYR depending on what France and Austria do. My goal is to secure Tunis this fall for a 4-center Italy by winter, then push into either Iberia or the eastern Med in 1902. I need to check whether France or Austria will contest my moves before committing.
RUSSIA
I'll take RUM with F RUM (it's unoccupied and mine by prior agreement with Turkey), move A UKR to GAL to threaten Austria's VIE/BUD, and push F FIN toward Sweden to claim another center. My goal is to hold 5-6 centers by end of 1902 by securing the Balkans and Scandinavia while keeping Turkey and Germany cooperative.
TURKEY
I'll push A BUL toward RUM to contest Russia's foothold there, move A ARM to SEV to threaten Russia's southern center, and keep F BLA in position to support either attack or convoy. My goal is to reach 5 centers by end of Fall 1901, positioning myself to challenge Russia for control of the Black Sea region and set up a strong mid-game push toward 7-8 centers by 1903.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'll move A SER to ALB to claim an unowned center, A VIE to BUD to reclaim my home center, and hold F ADR in place to honor my commitments to Italy and maintain flexibility in the Adriatic. Turkey is taking GRE as discussed, and Russia is pushing into GAL, so I need to consolidate my Balkan gains and prepare for potential friction with Russia over Galicia in 1902.
ENGLAND
I'll convoy A EDI to Norway via both F NTH and F NWG to secure that supply center, while holding the fleets in position for future flexibility. With Germany confirming Holland is theirs and France staying out of the north, this convoy is uncontested. After winter builds I'll have a second army and can start pressuring Russia or expanding into the Low Countries in 1902.
FRANCE
I'll move F MAO to Portugal to secure that supply center, while holding both A SPA and A GAS to maintain my Iberian position and keep pressure on Burgundy from the south. My plan for 1902 is to consolidate Iberia, then potentially contest Burgundy or push into North Africa depending on how Germany and Italy develop, while keeping England as a counterweight to German growth.
GERMANY
I'll move A BUR to Holland to secure my second neutral center, send F DEN to Sweden to contest it with Russia (forcing a bounce since he's sending F FIN there), and hold A PRU to guard against any Russian aggression toward Prussia or Livonia. After winter builds I'll have five centers and can pressure Belgium or Livonia in 1902, while keeping France cooperative and Russia contained in the north.
ITALY
I'll order F ION to support A TUS into Tunis, A TUS to move to Tunis, and A PIE to hold, securing my fourth supply center while honoring the DMZ with Austria and keeping peace with France. After winter builds, I'll reassess whether to push toward the eastern Mediterranean against Turkey or turn west toward Iberia depending on how the board develops.
RUSSIA
I'll take Sweden with F FIN, push A UKR into GAL to pressure Austria, and hold F RUM in place as promised to Turkey. My plan for 1902 is to consolidate my gains at 5 centers while building pressure on Austria from GAL, then coordinate with Turkey to split Austrian centers — GAL and possibly BUD for me, GRE and SER for Turkey. Germany's threat to bounce Sweden was a bluff I'm calling since F DEN can't reach SWE from its current position, and even if it could, we'd both lose a turn rather than me backing down.
TURKEY
I'll send A BUL to GRE to grab my third supply center as negotiated, move A ARM to SEV with F BLA's support to pressure Russia's southern position, and keep F BLA supporting that attack. After this turn I'll have three new centers (BUL, ARM, GRE) for a total of six, positioning me to push into RUM or SER next year while Russia and Austria are distracted fighting each other in Galicia.
