Negotiation before SPRING 1902 MOVEMENT

AUSTRIA ENGLAND FRANCE GERMANY ITALY RUSSIA TURKEY
AUSTRIA ⇄ ITALY
AUSTRIA → ITALYR1
Spring 1902 — I want to keep our quiet border intact. I have no designs on Venice or Trieste, and I hope you're not eyeing Trieste either. If we can confirm mutual restraint again this year, we both benefit from focusing our energy outward.
ITALY → AUSTRIAR1
Turkey now has Greece and is growing fast — this is a serious threat to both of us. I propose we coordinate directly: I'll push F TUN into ION and then toward Greece, while you pressure Bulgaria or hold Turkey's attention in the Balkans. If we squeeze Turkey together now, we can both grow at their expense before they become unstoppable.
AUSTRIA → ITALYR2
I'm open to coordinating against Turkey, but I need to know what you're actually committing to — are you moving F TUN to ION this spring, and if so, what's the target after that? I can apply pressure from the north if you're genuinely pushing south, but I won't expose myself if you hold back.
ITALY → AUSTRIAR2
I'm glad we're aligned — Turkey at 4 SCs with Greece is already a threat that will only grow. I'm moving F TUN into the Ionian this spring to set up a push on Greece in the fall. If you can pressure Bulgaria or threaten Turkey's northern flank, we can squeeze them from two directions. Can you confirm you're willing to coordinate against Turkey this year?
AUSTRIA → ITALYR3
I'm holding Romania against Russian pressure this spring, so my main focus is defensive — but F ALB can move toward Greece or ION to complement your push if you're genuinely moving F TUN to ION. Confirm your move and I'll coordinate accordingly; I won't expose my flank without knowing you're committed.
ITALY → AUSTRIAR3
Confirmed — I'm moving F TUN into the Ionian this spring, exactly as discussed. I expect to be in position to threaten Greece by fall. Please apply whatever pressure you can from the north — even holding Bulgaria's attention helps. This is the moment to act before Turkey consolidates further.
AUSTRIA ⇄ RUSSIA
AUSTRIA → RUSSIAR1
Spring 1902 — I want a stable arrangement. I hold Romania and intend to keep it; I have no designs on Ukraine or Sevastopol. If you accept Romania as mine and focus north and west, I'll actively support your interests elsewhere and we can build a durable border. A prolonged fight over Romania only benefits Turkey and Italy.
RUSSIA → AUSTRIAR1
Austria took RUM last fall — I need to be honest that I can't accept that long-term. I'm moving A UKR toward RUM this spring and F SEV to BLA. If you'll negotiate a handover of RUM to me, I'll support your position in Serbia and the western Balkans and guarantee GAL stays yours indefinitely. A prolonged fight over RUM only benefits Turkey.
AUSTRIA → RUSSIAR2
I hear your position, but I won't negotiate away Romania — it's my only eastern gain and I need it to remain competitive. If you push A UKR into Romania, I'll defend with GAL support and we'll both waste moves while Turkey and Italy grow unchecked. I'd far rather we find a stable line and turn our energy outward. What would it take for you to accept the current border and focus north?
RUSSIA → AUSTRIAR2
I hear your offer, but RUM is Russian by natural geography and I cannot accept Austrian control there long-term. I'm moving A UKR toward RUM this spring — if you support that move rather than defending, I'll guarantee GAL stays yours and actively support your Serbian and Balkan position against Turkey and Italy. A peaceful transfer now avoids a costly war that only Turkey wins.
AUSTRIA → RUSSIAR3
Final word: I'm defending Romania this spring with Galicia support — A UKR into Romania will bounce. A war between us only hands the game to Turkey and Italy, who are both growing fast. I urge you to redirect A UKR and F SEV elsewhere; I'll hold my current line and support Russian interests in the north if you accept a stable border now.
RUSSIA → AUSTRIAR3
Final round — I'm moving A WAR to GAL and A UKR to RUM this spring. This isn't a bluff. If you want to avoid losing GAL as well as potentially RUM, the smarter move is to support A UKR into RUM from GAL and let me have it peacefully — you keep GAL, Serbia, and my full support against Turkey and Italy. The alternative is a two-front squeeze that benefits only Turkey.
AUSTRIA ⇄ TURKEY
AUSTRIA → TURKEYR1
Our quiet border has served us both well. I'm holding Romania and looking south with F ALB — I'd be interested in coordinating if Italy starts pressing into the Balkans or Ionian. What are your plans for spring?
TURKEY → AUSTRIAR1
Our cooperation continues to serve us both well. I'm consolidating my Balkan position this spring — pushing back into BUL — and have no designs on SER or RUM. Let's make sure we're not stepping on each other: what are your plans for the Balkans this spring?
AUSTRIA → TURKEYR2
Russia is moving A UKR toward Romania and F SEV toward the Black Sea this spring — they're pushing hard in your direction too. A strong Russia in the Black Sea threatens us both. I'm defending Romania firmly; if you can pressure Bulgaria or hold Greece, we keep Russia from dominating the south. Let's coordinate rather than let Russia expand unchecked.
TURKEY → AUSTRIAR2
Italy is signaling a desire for spheres in the eastern Mediterranean, which I'm open to, but I'm more concerned about Russia contesting the Black Sea this spring. If we can maintain our quiet border, I'd like to discuss whether we might coordinate against Russian expansion — they're at 5 centers and growing. What's your read on the situation?
AUSTRIA → TURKEYR3
Russia is pressing hard on Romania and the Black Sea — they're the real threat to both of us right now. I'm holding firm in Romania and watching your northern flank. Let's keep our quiet border and make sure Russia doesn't dominate the south; I'm open to closer coordination against them if you're willing.
TURKEY → AUSTRIAR3
Russia is moving SEV to BLA regardless — I'm contesting it but a bounce is likely. The bigger picture: Russia at 5 centers and growing is a threat to both of us. I'm consolidating BUL this spring; let's keep our quiet border and discuss coordinating against Russian expansion in fall.
ENGLAND ⇄ FRANCE
ENGLAND → FRANCER1
France is now the largest power on the board and I'm watching carefully. I have no desire for conflict, but I need your army in Burgundy to move east or south — if it heads toward Belgium or the Channel coast, I'll have to respond in kind. What are your plans this spring?
FRANCE → ENGLANDR1
Spring 1902 — I'm considering pushing A BUR east toward Germany this turn. If you're planning to move on BEL from NTH or elsewhere, I'd rather aim for MUN and avoid a bounce. What's your plan this spring?
ENGLAND → FRANCER2
Good to hear BUR is heading east — that's the right move and keeps things calm between us. I do want to flag that F BRE is a concern for me; if it stays in Brest or moves to the Channel, I'll need to respond defensively. Can you confirm BRE stays out of the English Channel this spring?
FRANCE → ENGLANDR2
Thanks for the message — I want to be clear: A BUR is moving east toward Munich this spring, not toward Belgium or the coast. I have no interest in conflict with you and hope we can maintain our friendly arrangement. Let me know if you'd like to coordinate against Germany together.
ENGLAND → FRANCER3
Glad BUR is heading east — that keeps things smooth between us. My one firm line: F BRE must stay out of the English Channel this spring and beyond, or I'll have to treat it as a hostile act. If you can confirm that, we're fully aligned.
FRANCE → ENGLANDR3
Final confirmation: A BUR is moving to Munich, F BRE stays in Brest this spring — no Channel moves from me. I'm focused entirely on Germany and have zero interest in conflict with you. Let's keep our arrangement solid.
ENGLAND ⇄ GERMANY
ENGLAND → GERMANYR1
France has 5 centers and a Burgundy army that threatens both of us. I'd like to coordinate this spring — I'm thinking F NTH toward Denmark or Holland, and I can support you if needed. Are you willing to work together to check French expansion?
GERMANY → ENGLANDR1
Spring 1902 — I'm planning A KIE to Denmark and want to push A SIL toward either Munich... wait, Munich is already mine. I'm thinking A SIL - BOH or A SIL - WAR to pressure east. I'd love F NTH support into Denmark if you can spare it, or at least no interference. France at 5 centers is the real worry — want to discuss coordinating pressure on them?
ENGLAND → GERMANYR2
Confirmed — I'm supportive of A KIE moving to Denmark and can back that up. F NTH can support A KIE - DEN if you'd like, which gets you a new center cleanly. France pushing BUR east is good news for both of us; let's coordinate to contain them while we both grow. What's your read on where their Brest fleet is heading?
GERMANY → ENGLANDR2
I'm moving A KIE to Denmark this spring — if you can support from NTH or SWE that would be great, but I understand if you need those units elsewhere. The key thing: can we agree F NTH doesn't move to Denmark or interfere? France's A BUR worries me more than anything — if they push MUN I'll need to respond, and that might mean we need to jointly pressure France soon.
ENGLAND → GERMANYR3
All confirmed — F NTH S A KIE - DEN this spring. You get Denmark cleanly and we both grow stronger. France moving BUR east is good news; let's keep coordinating and discuss joint pressure on France in the fall if their fleet moves aggressively.
GERMANY → ENGLANDR3
Final confirmation: A KIE is moving to Denmark — please support with F NTH S A KIE - DEN as discussed. France has told me A BUR is heading for Munich, so I'm forced to respond defensively there. This makes coordinating against France even more urgent — I'm fully on board for joint pressure on them this year.

If A BUR stays put or moves away from Munich, we have no friction. But if it moves toward MUN, I'll have to treat that as aggression and respond accordingly — I wanted to be equally transparent with you.
FRANCE → GERMANYR3

GERMANY → FRANCER3

TURKEY → ITALYR3
