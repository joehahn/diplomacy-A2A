Commentary
  • Austria stabbed Russia cleanly: despite Russia's repeated pleas to be supported into Rumania, Austria moved A SER → RUM with GAL support, taking the center for itself and leaving Russia empty-handed in the south.
  • Russia's F SEV bounced off the Black Sea against Turkey's F ANK, meaning Russia failed on both its southern objectives — no Rumania, no Black Sea — a disastrous fall despite holding five centers only due to Sweden.
  • England and Germany executed a textbook coordinated grab: England supported F HOL → BEL from NTH exactly as promised, while France's A PIC → BUR (not BEL as France told Germany and England it would do) avoided the bounce but also missed the center — England warned France of the German move, and France quietly pivoted to Burgundy, which now threatens Germany's flank.
  • France's move of A PIC → BUR is the sleeper threat of the phase: France told Germany its army was going to Belgium, but instead it landed in Burgundy, putting pressure directly on Munich and setting up a potential western squeeze on Germany in 1902.
  • Italy defied Turkey's repeated requests and moved F ION → TUN rather than contesting Greece, taking Tunis cleanly — Turkey got Greece as a result, but Italy's choice to avoid the bounce was pragmatic self-interest, not generosity, netting Italy a safe fourth center while leaving the Ionian question
