This fall I'll pivot aggressively: A TUN convoys north to contest the center and force France to defend multiple fronts, F ADR and F ROM support a coordinated push into the Ionian and Tyrrhenian to weaken France's southern flank, and F TUS advances to secure my Mediterranean corridor while positioning for a winter build. My goal is to reclaim BER from France by spring 1909 while maintaining my non-aggression pacts with Turkey and Austria—if France breaks faith or falters under Russian pressure, I'll be positioned to seize undefended Balkan centers and pivot toward the straits. By winter 1908 I aim to return to 6 centers and set up a two-front containment of France for 1909.
RUSSIA
I'm at 4 centers (MOS, STP, WAR, RUM) facing slow strangulation while Italy and Turkey consolidate at 5-6 each and France dominates the west at 6. This fall I must break the deadlock by pressing F RUM into SEV to reclaim my southern rebuild slot, anchoring my core defensively with A WAR, A LVN, and A PRU to hold against Austria's escalation, and keeping A GAL mobile to reinforce the chain or pivot if Austria presses harder—by winter I need 6 centers (SEV) or the game is lost. TURKEY
Austria has pivoted south to defend Italy rather than press Russia as promised, breaking our Balkans-east dual squeeze pact—so I'm reverting to pure defense in the east and straits while I watch for board fractures. This fall I'll hold A SEV and A SYR defensively, anchor my fleets (F BLA, F AEG, F BUL/SC, F ION) to lock the straits and eastern Mediterranean, and signal to Italy that our non-aggression pact remains firm while I monitor whether Russia collapses or the northern powers (England, France, Germany) exhaust each other. If either happens next spring, I'll pivot west into the chaos and press toward the center of the board; if the board locks into stalemate, I'll consolidate my 6 centers and prepare for a long grind, but for now the cleanest path to 18 is to lock the eastern half and let the north grind itself down before I move.
