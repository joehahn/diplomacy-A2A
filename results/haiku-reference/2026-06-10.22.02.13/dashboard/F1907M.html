My only survival path is to pivot east as a spoiler: I'll hold F NTH defensively in the North Sea to maintain flexibility, signal to Russia that I'm abandoning the west and willing to destabilize the board if forced to choose between elimination and chaos, and position myself to support Austria and Turkey against Russia's northern flank next turn if the eastern powers offer me a non-aggression pact or build opportunity. I'm at 5 centers (MOS, STP, WAR, RUM, NWY) after Spring 1907's failed attempt to reclaim Sevastopol—Turkey held the line and I'm now locked out of the south. This fall I must consolidate my core defensively and prepare for a pivot west: I'll hold WAR, LVN, PRU as an anchor chain against Austria and Germany, press F RUM into Sevastopol one more time to break Turkey's grip on my rebuild slot, and keep A GAL mobile to support the chain or pivot toward contested ground if the western board opens. By winter I aim to stabilize at 6 centers (reclaiming SEV), then pivot west next spring to bleed Italy and Turkey before one runs away with the game—England and France are already consolidating unchecked, and the Mediterranean powers are now the immediate threat.
