SPRING 1906 MOVEMENT (S1906M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Italy's fleet in GRE held last fall despite my two-unit attack, so I need to shift approach: I'll offer Turkey a deal to take GRE together this spring, using my A SER and F ALB plus his F AEG, then pivot toward RUM with Turkey in the fall. Germany is at 7 centers and finishing France, so I must stabilize the south quickly before he turns east — taking GRE this spring and coordinating with Turkey against Russia's RUM is my path to 6+ centers and a viable position.
ENGLAND
Germany is at 7 centers and closing in on France, but I'm still at 4 and can't let him run away with the game. This turn I need to pivot: hold my northern position while probing for an anti-German coalition with Italy and Russia, positioning to take Denmark or Holland if Germany overextends. In two turns I want to be at 6 centers by claiming a German coastal province while the coalition grinds him down from three sides.
FRANCE
I need to reclaim MAR from Italy this year while Germany consolidates at 7 centers. I'll use F ENG and F MAO to convoy A PAR to Spain, then push into MAR from two sides next fall. My goal is to retake MAR by end of 1906 and build a fleet at BRE to challenge Germany's naval dominance in the North Sea.
GERMANY
I'll push A PIC into PAR this spring with support from A BEL and A RUH, while A HOL holds and my Baltic fleets stay in position to keep Russia quiet. France is down to three centers and cannot defend both PAR and BRE — I'll take Paris this turn and set up a fall push into Brest to eliminate him entirely. After France falls, I'll turn east to contain Italy's growing fleet presence or pivot against England if he continues to block the Channel.
ITALY
I'll take Spain this turn with A SPA - POR to claim a second French center, while my fleets in the east hold position and probe for an opening against Austria or Turkey. My goal is to reach 8 centers by the end of 1906, then pivot to contain Germany's northern dominance with England and Russia.
RUSSIA
Turkey took BUL last fall and now has 5 centers to my 4, while Germany sits at 7 and Austria at 4. I need to reclaim SEV this year to reach 5 centers and rebuild my build capacity, so I'll move A MOS toward SEV with support from A RUM and A GAL, while F STP/NC threatens NWY to keep Germany's fleets tied up. Two turns from now I want to hold SEV and RUM with 5-6 centers, then pivot against whichever of Turkey or Austria is weaker while England and Germany bleed each other in the west.
TURKEY
Italy's move into AEG last spring while promising to support me into BUL was a setup — he's now positioned to take BUL/SC himself and has been playing me for two turns. I'll hold my line this spring (F AEG, A BUL, A SEV, F BLA, F ANK) and see whether Italy attacks BUL/SC or supports me, then pivot to either crush him with Austria's help or take SER with Italy if he proves trustworthy. My goal is to reach 7 centers by end of 1906 by taking one more from Austria (SER) or Italy (GRE) while keeping Russia contained in the north.
