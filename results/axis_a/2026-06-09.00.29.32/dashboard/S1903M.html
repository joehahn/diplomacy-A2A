SPRING 1903 MOVEMENT (S1903M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Russia's fleet was disbanded and he's down to 4 units on land, while I hold Bulgaria and have 5 armies in the Balkans. This spring I'll secure my position by supporting my hold on BUL from GRE and moving A BUD toward RUM to pressure Russia's isolated position there, while A TRI holds and A VIE supports in case Russia tries to slip through Galicia. In two turns I want to own RUM and either SER or have Turkey pushed out, giving me 7+ centers and the dominant position in the southeast.
ENGLAND
I'll hold my northern position this turn and focus on securing a build for Winter 1903 by keeping my units safe and uncommitted to risky attacks. My goal is to use this spring to negotiate a coordinated squeeze on Germany with Russia and France, then strike KIEL and BELGIUM in the fall with support from both sides to cut Germany down to 3-4 centers and open the continent for my expansion.
FRANCE
I'll move F BRE into the English Channel to threaten England's coast and probe his intentions, while A PIC holds to maintain pressure on Belgium and keep Germany honest. F MAO will convoy A SPA to GAS to position for a push into BUR or MAR next turn, and F MAR will move to LYO to threaten Italy's rear or support into Spain. By fall I want a fleet in ENG, an army in GAS, and the option to either strike England's home centers or pivot against Germany if he overreaches.
GERMANY
I need to take Holland this turn — it's the only unowned supply center left and I'm at 6 centers to France's 5, so claiming it puts me ahead. England is bottled in the north with 4 centers, France is consolidating Iberia, and Russia is stalled in the Balkans after losing his fleet in BUL/EC. I'll use A KIE to take Holland supported by A BEL, while my fleets in BAL and SWE hold the Baltic and Scandinavia, and A MUN stands ready to support the center or pivot toward France if he gets ideas about Burgundy. After claiming Holland, I'll have 7 centers and can build another unit next winter, positioning me to decide between pressuring England's North Sea or France's eastern flank in 1903.
ITALY
I'll move my fleet from Tunisia into the Ionian Sea to threaten Austria's hold on Greece. I'll keep my armies in place for now, sounding out Turkey for a coordinated attack on Austria. Within two turns, I aim to take Greece with Turkish support and build in Venice.
RUSSIA
Austria has betrayed me and taken Bulgaria, leaving me with only RUM and a destroyed fleet in the Black Sea. I need to rebuild my southern position by taking SEV from Turkey while keeping my northern fleet in BOT to hold Sweden against Germany and England. Over the next two turns, I want to consolidate Rumania, seize SEV, and prepare to hit Austria in the Balkans with Turkish help if possible, while keeping the northern front quiet with Germany.
TURKEY
I'm going to seek an alliance with Austria to crush Russia's southern flank. Austria already holds BUL and wants RUM; I can support him into RUM with my fleet in the Black Sea while I take SEV with my army from ARM. That gives each of us a center
