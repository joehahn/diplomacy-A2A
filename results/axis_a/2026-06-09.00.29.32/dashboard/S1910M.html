SPRING 1910 MOVEMENT (S1910M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll take SER this turn to reach 6 centers, then pivot against Turkey at BUL next fall with Russian support from SEV. I need to keep Germany quiet on my northern border and Italy neutral in the Adriatic while I consolidate the Balkans.
ENGLAND
Germany is at 9 centers and just built two armies; I'm outnumbered and must avoid direct confrontation this turn. I'll hold the North Sea line while pressing Italy to finally move into MAO and urging Russia to attack Sweden, using diplomacy to build a coalition before Germany crushes me. Two turns from now, I want Italy in MAO threatening Germany's Channel, Russia pressuring the Baltic, and my fleets positioned to either support an invasion of Scandinavia or defend my home islands.
FRANCE
I have one army at MAR and Germany holds 9 centers with forces at BRE, BEL, BUR, and RUH all adjacent to me. My only realistic path to rebuild is to take SPA from Italy this turn while his fleet is stuck at SPA/SC and POR, then use a winter build at MAR to field two units and coordinate with England against Germany's western flank in 1911. If I cannot take SPA this turn, I will hold MAR and negotiate with Germany for survival while the eastern powers fight over the Balkans.
GERMANY
I have 9 centers and a large army advantage over everyone else. This turn I'll secure my western flank by taking PIC with A BRE to threaten England's last continental foothold, while moving A KIE and A MUN toward the eastern front to prepare for a push against Russia or Austria. In two turns I aim to have 11-12 centers by taking Norway from England and then striking east against whoever is weakest.
ITALY
I'll finally break through into Spain this turn by moving A POR to SPA and F WES to SPA/SC, securing my 8th center and giving me a direct springboard into MAO next turn to threaten Germany's Channel flank. My Med and Adriatic fleets hold—F ION, F TUN, F NAP, and F VEN stay put—keeping quiet with Austria while Germany focuses north on BEL against England. Once Spain is mine, I push F WES into MAO in the fall to squeeze Germany between England's Channel fleets and my southern presence, forcing him to split his navy and opening opportunities to take MAR or land in Iberia.
RUSSIA
I'll retake SEV this spring with A UKR supported by A SEV while A WAR moves to GAL to threaten Austria's VIE and keep him honest. F STP/NC holds in position to keep my northern flank secure while I consolidate the south. After securing SEV and building in MOS this winter, I'll pivot north to hit Sweden in 1911 with England's support while keeping Austria neutral by threatening BUL from SEV and UKR.
TURKEY
Germany is at 9 centers and growing—I need to move toward containing him before he hits 18. I'll hold my position in the east while probing for an alliance with Germany or Italy to break the leader's momentum. In 1–2 turns I want to push into the Mediterranean and secure a 7th center, then coordinate with a western power to cut Germany down.

This supports Russia's attack on Sweden to cut Germany's supply line, while I keep my fleet line intact and avoid a direct confrontation with Germany's overwhelming force. If Russia takes Sweden cleanly, we coordinate on Denmark in 1911 while I rebuild and look for an opening to retake the initiative.
FRANCE
I'll order A MAR to SPA this turn, taking the undefended supply center from Italy while his forces are split at POR and SPA/SC. Even if Italy contests, I'll bounce him and keep MAR safe, then rebuild to two units in winter and coordinate with England against Germany's western flank in 1911.
GERMANY
I'll commit F DEN to move into SKA and A KIE to convoy into SWE via F BAL, aiming to take Norway from England this fall while A BEL and A HOL hold the Channel coast. A BRE will move into PIC to secure my western flank, A MUN and A RUH will hold in central Germany, and A KIE, A MUN, and the Baltic fleets will position for a northern push. My plan is to reach 11-12 centers by the end of 1910 by taking Norway and PIC, then pivot east against whichever of Austria or Russia is weaker.
ITALY
I'll move A POR into SPA and F WES to SPA/SC to finally secure my 8th center, while holding F ION, F TUN, F NAP, and F VEN in place to keep the Adriatic quiet with Austria and maintain my Med position. After Spain falls, I'll push F WES into MAO in the fall to threaten Germany's Channel flank as promised to England, timing the squeeze for when Germany is committed to taking Norway from the north. I'm honoring my defensive pact with Austria by keeping F VEN and F ION holding, and I'll wait to see if Austria actually attacks BUL before committing to any coordination with Turkey against him.
RUSSIA
I'll order A UKR to hold and A SEV to hold, honoring the quiet border agreement with Turkey, while F STP/NC moves to SWE with England's F NWY supporting. A WAR moves to GAL to threaten Austria's VIE and keep him honest about not moving on my flank. After taking Sweden this spring and building at MOS this winter, I'll pivot south to support Austria against Turkey in 1911 while keeping Germany contained from the north.
TURKEY
I'll hold all my units in place this spring as agreed with Russia and Italy, keeping the eastern border quiet and the Mediterranean stable. I'm trusting Russia to hold UKR and STP while I focus on building toward a 7th center and preparing to push west against Italy or Germany in the coming turns. If Austria does attack BUL as Italy warned, I'll be ready to defend and coordinate a counter with Italy next turn, but for now I'm consolidating and watching the leader's growth.
