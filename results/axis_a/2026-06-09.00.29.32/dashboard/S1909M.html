SPRING 1909 MOVEMENT (S1909M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm betraying Turkey this spring to honor my deal with Russia. I'll order A BUD to support Russia's A UKR into RUM, and A GRE to support that same attack, cutting Turkey's supply line and giving Russia his center back. In exchange, Russia will leave VIE alone and we'll coordinate against Germany's southern flank in the fall, putting me in position to take BUL or SER next year.
ENGLAND
I'll take BEL from Germany this spring by convoying A YOR to BEL via NTH, with F LON and F EDI supporting the operation to hold the Channel and North Sea. I need Italy to push F WES into MAO this turn to threaten Germany's Channel coast and force him to split his fleets, while I position to take DEN next fall from NTH with Russian help from NWY. Two turns from now, I want to hold BEL and DEN, have Italy on Germany's southern coast, and be squeezing Germany down from 7 centers to 4 while I grow toward 7-8 myself.
FRANCE
I'll hold A BRE this turn and focus on rebuilding my position. My immediate goal is to retake PAR from Germany next year with English and Austrian support, while Italy remains preoccupied with Turkey and Russia. In two turns I aim to control BRE, PAR, and PIC as a springboard to reclaim MAR from Italy.
GERMANY
I'll take BRE this turn with A PAR supported by A BUR, securing my 8th center and eliminating France as a threat. I need to hold my other positions against England's army in BEL and his fleet in NTH, so I'll keep A HOL and A RUH in defensive support posture while my Baltic fleets hold to contain any Russian or English naval moves. After this turn, I'll have 8 centers and can pivot to reclaim BEL from England, likely with Italian help or at least Italian neutrality, while keeping my eastern border quiet with Austria.

RUSSIA
I'll move A MOS to SEV with support from A UKR to reclaim my lost home center, while F STP/NC moves to NWY to coordinate with England against Germany's Baltic flank. After taking SEV and building a new army at WAR this winter, I'll push A UKR toward RUM or GAL and turn north with England to take Sweden in fall 1909.
TURKEY
Austria and I finally have Russia neutralized and Italy's fleets overextended. This spring I'll push F AEG into ION to contest Italy's naval dominance, while A SEV moves to UKR to threaten Russia's last centers and secure my eastern flank. Within two turns I want to break Italy's ION position, take GRE with Austrian help, and build toward a 7-8 center position that lets me dominate the eastern Mediterranean.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'll order A BUD S A UKR - RUM and A GRE S A UKR - RUM to honor my deal with Russia, cutting Turkey's supply line and letting Russia retake RUM this spring. A TRI and A VIE both hold to keep the Adriatic quiet with Italy and maintain a stable northern border with Germany. After RUM falls to Russia, I'll coordinate with him for a joint strike on BUL next fall while keeping my defensive line intact against any Turkish counterattack from the south.
ENGLAND
I'll take BEL from Germany this spring by convoying A BEL to HOL via NTH, using F NTH to convoy and F LON supporting the operation, while F EDI moves to NTH to reinforce the North Sea and F NWY holds to maintain pressure on Scandinavia. Germany is consolidating at 7 centers and Italy refuses to move MAO this turn, so I'll secure my position in the Low Countries now and prepare to coordinate with Russia on Sweden in the fall after he retakes SEV.
FRANCE
Germany has committed A PAR and A BUR against BRE with no way for England to save me this spring, so I will order A BRE to hold in the hope that Germany leaves my army alive to disband in winter, buying time for England and the eastern powers to pressure his gains while I rebuild next year.
GERMANY
I will commit A PAR to move into BRE with support from A BUR, eliminating France and securing my eighth supply center. All other units will hold or take defensive postures to maintain my positions against England and keep the Baltic and northern fronts stable. After this turn, I will have the strength to reclaim BEL from England in the fall while keeping my other borders quiet.
ITALY
I'll execute the long-planned Spanish capture: A POR moves into SPA and F WES moves to SPA/SC, finally securing my 8th center. My eastern line stays quiet with F VEN, F ION, F NAP, F TUN, and A ROM all holding — honoring my agreement with Austria to keep the Adriatic stable while Germany remains focused west. After this turn, I'll push F WES into MAO in the fall to threaten Germany's Channel flank as promised to England, timing the squeeze for when he's committed to reclaiming BEL.
RUSSIA
I'll order A MOS to SEV supported by A UKR, and F STP/NC to NWY to position for a future strike on Sweden. Turkey refused every truce offer and Austria's proposed RUM attack is a trap—he's still aligned with Turkey and I cannot trust him. After retaking SEV and building at WAR this winter, I'll pivot north to coordinate with England against Germany's Baltic flank in fall 1909.
TURKEY
Italy refused to coordinate and Austria never confirmed support for my ION push, so I'll secure my eastern gains instead. I'll order F AEG to hold, F CON to hold, F ANK to hold, F BLA to hold, A BUL to hold, and A SEV to hold — consolidating my 6 centers while Russia and Italy posture. This lets me build toward a 7th center at SMY in Winter and pivot into the Med in 1910 with a stronger fleet position.
