FALL 1901 MOVEMENT (F1901M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll take Greece this fall with F ALB and hold Serbia with A SER, while A VIE moves to TRI to secure my home center and prepare for a build. Russia took Galicia and Rumania as agreed, so I need to claim Greece now to match his gains and keep the Balkan split balanced. Next spring I'll push into Bulgaria or Rumania depending on whether Turkey or Russia leaves an opening.
ENGLAND
I'll take Norway with F NWG and convoy A YOR to Belgium via F NTH, securing two neutrals this fall. Germany has Holland, Russia has Sweden, France has Iberia — I need to grab my share before the board locks down. After this turn, I'll have 5 centers and can build a fleet in London for 1902, then coordinate with France or Germany against whichever grows fastest.
FRANCE
I'll take Portugal with A SPA this fall, convoying via F MAO, while A BUR moves to PIC to stay out of Germany's path into Belgium and position for a future push into the Low Countries or Munich. If England moves into Belgium as he hinted, I'll be ready to pivot against him with Germany next year; if Germany takes Belgium cleanly, I'll secure Iberia fully and look toward the Channel or Italy's rear.
GERMANY
France honored our deal, and I'm set up perfectly. This turn I take Belgium with A RUH supported by A KIE, while F DEN moves to Sweden to secure that open center and block Russia from gaining too much. If England tries to contest Belgium, I'll need to negotiate him off or bounce him, but a supported move into BEL from RUH should hold.
ITALY
I'll convoy A ROM to TUN via F ION this fall to grab an undefended supply center, while A TUS moves into PIE to pressure France's MAR and set up a two-front attack on them in 1902. Austria and France both think I'm staying south and east, so this turn I take Tunisia and position to stab France next year when they're committed in Iberia and against England.
RUSSIA
I'll take Sweden with F BOT - SWE, move A UKR to support the southern flank, and position A WAR to push into Galicia. I want to secure Sweden, hold Rumania, and be ready to attack Austria in 1902 if they leave Galicia undefended.
TURKEY
I'm in a strong position: I control Bulgaria, my fleet in the Black Sea blocks Russia from the south, and I have a free hand to choose my next target. I'll move A BUL into Greece this fall to pick up a second neutral center while Russia is occupied with Rumania and Austria holds Serbia. In 1902, I want to be positioned to either push into the Mediterranean with a navy build or support Austria against Russia if the opportunity arises.

ITALY

Austria is taking Greece and repositioning to TRI as agreed, and France is moving into Portugal — both deals hold for this turn, giving me a clean gain and a springboard for 1902.
RUSSIA
I'll move F BOT to SWE to claim Sweden as agreed with England, move A WAR to GAL to finally secure that province as promised to Austria, hold A UKR in place to support the southern front, and hold F RUM in Rumania to keep my deal with Turkey and maintain pressure on the Balkans. After this fall, I'll have four supply centers (MOS, SEV, STP, WAR) plus RUM and SWE, giving me six total and two builds in winter—positioning me perfectly to strike against Austria or Turkey in 1902 with a reinforced army. My northern and southern flanks are secure, and I'm set to coordinate with England against Germany and with Turkey against Austria after the builds.
TURKEY
I'll move A BUL into GRE to secure a second neutral center, hold A SMY to guard the home center, and move F BLA into CON to free up that build slot for winter, positioning myself for a strong 1902 push against Austria while keeping my options open with Russia.
