SPRING 1907 MOVEMENT (S1907M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm at 5 centers with GRE secured and a stable position in the Balkans. My immediate goal is to coordinate with Turkey against Russia's RUM — I'll offer him support from SER and BUL in exchange for his help taking RUM next spring, which would put me at 6 centers and cut Russia down. In the longer term, I need to watch Germany at 7 centers finishing France; once he turns east, I'll need either a defensive pact with Italy or a quick consolidation with Turkey to hold my gains.
ENGLAND
Germany is at 7 centers and about to finish France, but I'm still at 4 and need to grow. This turn I'll hold my northern position and negotiate with Italy and Russia to finalize a coordinated squeeze on Germany's Baltic and southern flanks in the fall. In two turns I want to take Denmark with F NTH and force Germany to split his fleets between the Baltic and the Med, setting up a three-front containment that cuts him down to size.
FRANCE
I need to survive this turn at all costs. My best chance is to convince England to support my hold on PAR with F NTH—if he does, Germany's attack bounces and I keep my army. If England refuses, I'll pivot to a joint attack on BEL as a desperate alternative to deny Germany a center and salvage something from the year. Either way, I must keep F ENG active and positioned to contest the Channel.
GERMANY
France is finished as a threat — I hold 7 centers and he's down to 2 with no army to contest me. This spring I'll take Paris with A PIC supported by A BEL and A RUH, while my Baltic fleets hold position to keep Russia quiet and my northern fleets threaten Denmark and the North Sea. After France falls this year, I'll pivot to contain England's Channel fleet and negotiate with Italy and Austria to keep my eastern and southern flanks stable while I push toward 18.
ITALY
I'll take Spain with A POR - SPA to reach 8 centers, while moving F WES to SPA/SC to support the capture and position against Germany's southern flank. F ION will hold to keep the Adriatic quiet with Austria, and F TUN stays in position to threaten Germany's Med interests next turn. By Fall 1907 I want to consolidate at 8-9 centers, then coordinate with England and Russia to squeeze Germany from both sides while keeping Austria neutral in the Adriatic.
RUSSIA
Turkey refused to give up SEV and built a second fleet, so I'll take it by force this spring with A MOS supported by A RUM and A GAL, reclaiming my home center and reaching 5 centers. After securing SEV and building in WAR this winter, I'll pivot north in fall 1907 to hit Sweden from NWY with England, squeezing Germany's Baltic flank while he's overextended in the west. In two turns I want to hold 6-7 centers by taking SWE with England and possibly BUD from Austria if he's weakened.
TURKEY
Italy is at 8 centers and has abandoned the eastern Med, leaving me free to take SER this fall with A BUL supported by F AEG while Austria takes GRE. I'll hold the Black Sea line with F BLA and F ANK to deter Russia, then pivot to take RUM from Russia next spring with Austrian support from SER and GRE. My goal is 7 centers by end of 1907, then turn on Italy with Austria's help once his western fleets are committed against Germany.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'll order A SER to support Turkey's attack on RUM from SEV, as agreed, while A BUD holds to guard against any Russian incursion, A TRI holds to keep the Adriatic quiet per my deal with Italy, A VIE holds as a reserve, and A GRE holds to secure my newly gained center. After taking RUM together with Turkey this turn, I'll have 6 centers and can pivot toward coordinating with him against Italy's Med fleets or against Germany if he turns east, while keeping my home centers secure and my northern border quiet.
ENGLAND
I'll move F NTH to DEN this spring to challenge Germany's Baltic control directly, while F NWY holds to maintain pressure on Scandinavia and F LON holds to guard the Channel approach. A YOR will hold in reserve for future convoy operations. Russia can't hit Sweden this spring as planned, so I'll threaten Denmark now to force Germany's fleets to split and set up a coordinated squeeze with Italy and Russia this fall.
FRANCE
Germany has guaranteed my survival at BRE if I hold this turn, so I'll order A PAR to hold and F ENG to hold, accepting his deal. This buys me time to rebuild and seek a real coalition against Germany in 1907 with England, Austria, and Russia, who all have reason to fear his growing power. I will use the fall to negotiate a coordinated attack on Germany's flanks next year while holding my remaining centers.
GERMANY
This spring I'll take Paris with A PIC supported by A BEL and A RUH, honoring my deal with France to leave him at BRE through 1907 while I claim the center. My Baltic fleets hold position to keep Russia quiet, and my northern units stay in place to maintain pressure on England in the North Sea. After France falls, I'll pivot to negotiate with Italy and Austria about containing each other while I consolidate my western gains and plan the next push toward 18 centers.
ITALY
I'll move A POR into SPA to secure my 8th center, while F WES supports that move by moving to SPA/SC to secure the coast and position against Germany's southern flank. F ION holds to keep the Adriatic quiet with Austria, and all other units hold—F VEN, F NAP, F TUN, and A ROM—consolidating my position while I prepare to pivot against Germany in the fall with England and Russia, timing the squeeze for 1907.
RUSSIA
Turkey has refused every offer and now threatens to take RUM with Austrian support. I cannot trust that Austria will keep the border quiet under that pressure, so I must hold RUM defensively with A RUM holding and A GAL supporting it, while A MOS moves to SEV to reclaim my home center. F STP/NC will hold in position to maintain my northern flank and keep the option open for a fall push toward Sweden with England once I've secured SEV.
TURKEY
I'll order A SEV - RUM with support from A BUL and F BLA, while F AEG holds and F ANK holds, honoring my deal with Austria to take RUM together this spring. After RUM falls, I'll reach 6 centers and coordinate with Austria against Italy's Mediterranean fleets in 1908, while keeping the Black Sea line secure against any Russian counterattack.
