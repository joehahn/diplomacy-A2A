SPRING 1902 MOVEMENT (S1902M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll push into Bulgaria from Serbia with support from Greece, aiming to take it this turn while Russia is occupied elsewhere. I'll move my Trieste army toward Vienna to consolidate my home centers, and keep my Galicia threat alive to pressure Russia's flank. In two turns, I want to hold Bulgaria and Greece, with my armies positioned to either push into Rumania or defend against a Turkish counterattack.
ENGLAND
I'll take Belgium this spring with a convoy from YOR to BEL via F NTH, while F NWY holds to secure Norway and F LON moves into ENG to block French fleets from the Channel and position for a future attack on Brest. My goal is to hold Belgium and Norway, build a third fleet in London next winter, and then coordinate with Russia to pressure Germany from both sides in 1902-1903.
FRANCE
I'll take Spain this spring with A POR - SPA, convoying through F MAO, while moving A PIC to BUR to pressure Germany's flank and positioning F BRE to move into ENG or support into GAS. By fall I want to hold SPA and POR securely, have a fleet in the Channel or Western Med to threaten England or support a Tunisian play, and be ready to pivot against whichever neighbor overreaches first.
GERMANY
I'll seize Holland and Belgium this year to expand to 6 centers, using F BER and F DEN to secure the Baltic and Sweden while A KIE and A RUH push west into the Low Countries. I need to negotiate with England to avoid a bounce in Belgium and with France to ensure he stays out of my path, then pivot toward either England or France in 1903 depending on who grows fastest and who I can peel away from their ally. Russia is focused south and east, so I'll offer him a clean Baltic border and support against Turkey in exchange for Sweden.
ITALY
I need to grow this year. I'll move into Tunisia with F ION and A ROM to grab my first new supply center, while A PIE threatens MAR to pressure France or force them to defend — setting up a two-front attack on France in 1902 once they're committed against England and Germany. In two turns, I want Tunisia secured, a fleet in TYS to threaten the Western Med, and an opening to take MAR from France while Austria and Turkey are tangled in the Balkans.
RUSSIA
With Sweden in hand and five centers, I'm now the equal of Austria and England in supply count. My priority this spring is securing the Rumania-Galicia axis: I'll hold RUM to maintain pressure on the Balkans, move A UKR to support my southern flank, and position A MOS to reinforce either the north or south as needed. If Austria honors our split, I'll coordinate to take Bulgaria together; if not, I'm ready to pivot and hit him from Galicia while Turkey strikes from the south. In the north, F BOT will stay put to cover Sweden and threaten Germany's Baltic coast, setting up a two-front squeeze with England against Berlin in 1902.
TURKEY
I need to break out of my three-center stalemate. Russia holds RUM and has built a second army in MOS, while Austria has built two armies and now controls five centers. I'll move A CON to BUL to reclaim my lost supply center, send F ANK to BLA to threaten RUM and cut Russian influence, and move A SMY to ARM to position for a push into Sevastopol or Syria. In two turns, I want to have BUL, a fleet in the Black Sea pressuring RUM, and an army in ARM threatening Russia's southern flank, forcing him to split his forces between me and Austria.

After spring resolves, I'll coordinate with Russia on a fall squeeze of Germany's northern coast from both sides.
FRANCE

This keeps my deal with Germany intact while improving my position for a potential fall pivot — if England stays north and Germany takes Belgium cleanly, I'll consolidate Iberia and look toward either Tunis or a coordinated move against England. If Germany's bounce with England in Belgium repeats, I'll have A BUR and F ENG ready to support whichever side offers me the best opening into the Low Countries.
GERMANY

With Sweden and Belgium in hand this year, I'll grow to 6 centers and position to pivot against either England or France in 1903 depending on who consolidates fastest.
ITALY
I'll convoy A ROM to TUN via F ION this spring to take Tunisia, while A PIE holds position to maintain pressure on France's MAR without violating our Med line agreement. This gives me my first new supply center and a stronger position for 1903, keeping all my current deals intact while I build toward a two-front game against whoever emerges as the biggest threat.
RUSSIA
I will move F RUM to BUL/EC with Austrian support from Serbia as agreed, while keeping F BOT in the Baltic to hold Sweden and maintain pressure on Germany. A GAL will hold to reinforce the southern front, A UKR will hold to support the Rumania axis, and A MOS will remain in Moscow as a strategic reserve. After securing Bulgaria this spring, I'll pivot to coordinate with England against Germany's Baltic coast and with Austria against Turkey's remaining centers in the fall.
TURKEY
I will order A CON to BUL, F ANK to BLA, and A SMY to ARM, contesting Russia's broken promise and threatening his southern flank from three directions. By taking BUL back, putting a fleet in the Black Sea to pressure RUM, and moving an army toward ARM to threaten SEV, I force Russia to split his forces between me and Austria while positioning myself for a coordinated push into his territory this fall.
