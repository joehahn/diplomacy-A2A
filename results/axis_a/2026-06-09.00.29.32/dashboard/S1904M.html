SPRING 1904 MOVEMENT (S1904M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll push into Ukraine from RUM this spring with support from BUD, while VIE and TRI hold the home centers and GRE keeps the Aegean secure. Turkey holds SEV and I hold RUM, so together we can crush Russia's last real army at UKR and take his remaining supply centers this year. After Russia falls, I'll have 9+ centers and can pivot west to check Germany's 7-center position before he becomes unstoppable.
ENGLAND
I'll hold my northern positions this turn and negotiate with France and Germany to determine which side I'll commit to in the fall. My goal is to secure Denmark and possibly Holland within two turns, growing to 6-7 centers, then pivot against whichever neighbor is weakest—likely France if Germany cooperates with me against him.
FRANCE
Germany at 7 centers with builds in Munich and Kiel is still the biggest threat. I'll work with England to coordinate a two-front strike this spring and fall: I'll move F ENG to support an attack on Belgium while England pressures Kiel from HEL. My goal this year is to take Belgium and position for a push into Ruhr or Munich in 1905, while keeping Italy neutral with assurances about the Med line.
GERMANY
I'm at 7 centers with seven units and a strong central position, while France is the most immediate threat after his failed stab at Belgium last fall. This spring I'll reposition my forces to pressure France from the north through the Low Countries and from the east through Burgundy, using A BEL and A HOL to threaten PIC while A MUN and A KIE probe toward BUR. By Fall 1904 I want to have taken at least one French supply center, setting up a Winter build that pushes me toward 8-9 centers and makes me the clear board leader.
ITALY
Turkey has committed to moving F BLA to AEG this spring to support my attack on Greece in the fall, so I'll hold F ION in position and keep A PIE and A ROM steady, waiting one season to hit Greece with full force. I need to grow to 5 centers this year, so if the Greek opportunity stalls, I'll pivot to pressuring France's undefended MAR through PIE while keeping my fleet in ION to threaten Austria's flank. My long-term goal is to reach 6 centers by the end of 1904 by taking Greece and either MAR or a Balkan center, then build toward a decisive push against whoever is weakest.
RUSSIA
I'm down to 3 centers with a single fleet disbanded and Austria and Turkey coordinating against me, so my only play is to break the Austria-Turkey axis by hitting RUM this spring with A UKR supported by A GAL, while A MOS holds to defend Moscow. If I can take RUM from Austria and cut his supply line, I force him to defend instead of pushing east, and I open the door to negotiate with Turkey from a position of strength. Two turns from now I want to hold RUM and SEV, rebuilding to 5 centers, and then pivot against whichever of Austria or Turkey is weaker while keeping Germany neutral in the north.
TURKEY
I'll hold SEV with A SEV and F BLA to keep my new center secure while I move F SMY into AEG to position for a joint attack on Greece with Italy. I need Italy to commit to moving F ION into GRE this spring so we can take it together and break Austria's Balkan position. If Italy cooperates, I'll have five centers by fall and can push into RUM with Austria or turn on him depending on how the board shifts.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'll order A RUM - UKR with support from A BUD, F GRE to hold, A TRI to hold, and A VIE to hold, pushing into Ukraine to finish Russia while Turkey stalls—taking UKR alone this spring gives me the center and leaves Turkey with nothing from Russia's collapse, positioning me for 8 centers and two builds to pivot west against Germany in 1905.
ENGLAND
I'll order F HEL to support A PIC into BEL, F LON to hold, F NWY to hold, and A YOR to hold, honoring my commitment to France to squeeze Germany's western flank this spring. If we take Belgium, Germany loses a key supply center and I position myself to push into the North Sea or Channel in the fall, while keeping my northern fleet ready to threaten Denmark or Sweden depending on how Russia's southern campaign develops.
FRANCE
Italy has just betrayed me, threatening to take MAR while my forces are committed against Germany. I'll hold A PIC in place to defend Paris and the Channel, move A GAS to MAR to block the Italian advance, and reposition F ENG to support from the sea while F MAO and F LYO reposition to counter the Italian threat. This pivots my focus to crushing Italy's opportunistic strike before resuming pressure on Germany.
GERMANY
Italy has committed to attacking France's southern coast this spring, which gives me the perfect opportunity to squeeze France from both sides. I'll hold my position in Belgium and the Low Countries while pressing into Burgundy with A MUN and A KIE, threatening France's eastern flank and forcing him to split his forces between the north and south. This pincer with Italy should break France's position this year, setting me up to take his supply centers in the fall.
ITALY
I'll hold F ION in position this spring as agreed with Turkey, positioning for a fall attack on Greece with Turkish support from AEG. I'll move A PIE into MAR to take advantage of France's distraction with Germany, growing to 5 centers this year while keeping A ROM steady in central Italy. After securing MAR, I can pivot east in the fall to take Greece with Turkey, or hold both gains and build toward 6 centers in the winter.
RUSSIA
I'll order A UKR into RUM supported by A GAL, while A MOS holds in Moscow to defend my home center. This turn I reclaim RUM from Austria to reach 4 centers, then in the fall I can either consolidate or pivot A UKR and A GAL against Austria's weakened flank while keeping Germany neutral through our Baltic agreement. If I take RUM uncontested, I'm set to rebuild toward 5-6 centers by winter and then coordinate with England against Germany in 1905.
TURKEY
I'll hold SEV with F BLA support to secure my position, move F SMY to AEG to position for a fall attack on Greece with Italy, and hold A CON to maintain control of Constantinople. This keeps my gains secure while setting up the coordinated strike on Austria's Balkan position that Italy and I have been planning, even though Italy's attention may be divided after his message about focusing west.
