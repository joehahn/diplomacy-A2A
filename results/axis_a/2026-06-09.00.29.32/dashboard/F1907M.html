FALL 1907 MOVEMENT (F1907M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll support Turkey's RUM attack from SER as agreed, while holding GRE, BUD, TRI, and VIE to secure my 5 centers. After RUM falls, I'll have 6 centers and a strong position to pivot against Italy's Med fleets or Germany's eastward push, depending on who emerges as the greater threat.
ENGLAND
Germany is about to take Paris and hit 9 centers, which is unacceptable. I'll hold my northern position this fall and negotiate with Italy and Russia to finalize a coordinated three-front squeeze on Germany in 1908, aiming to take Denmark from NTH while Italy threatens his southern coast and Russia hits Sweden from NWY.
FRANCE
I'll hold PAR and ENG this turn as agreed with Germany, using the fall to rebuild and negotiate a coalition against him. My goal is to rebuild at BRE next winter, then coordinate with England and Russia to push Germany from both the Channel and the Baltic in 1908 while Italy remains focused on Turkey.
GERMANY
I'll take Paris this fall with A PIC supported by A BEL and A RUH, honoring my deal to leave France at BRE through 1907. I'll hold my Baltic and North Sea positions to keep Russia and England contained while I consolidate at 8 centers. After this turn, I'll pivot to coordinate with Italy and Austria against England's Channel fleet and Russia's northern ambitions, setting up my push toward 18.
ITALY
I have 7 centers and need to grow. Germany will take Paris this turn and hit 9 centers—he's the clear leader. My fleets in WES and ION are positioned to threaten his southern flank, and I need to coordinate with England and Russia to squeeze him from multiple sides this fall while keeping Austria neutral in the Adriatic. By the end of 1907 I want to take Spain with A POR and push F WES into MAO to threaten Germany's Channel coast, setting up a two-front war that forces him to split his forces.
RUSSIA
Turkey took RUM last spring and now holds 5 centers to my 4, with SEV still in his hands. I must retake RUM this fall with A UKR supported by A SEV and A GAL, then build in WAR this winter to reach 5 centers. After securing RUM, I'll coordinate with England against Germany's Baltic flank in 1908 while keeping Austria neutral with promises of Turkish territory.
TURKEY
I'll take RUM with A SEV supported by A BUL and F BLA as agreed with Austria, while F AEG holds to keep the eastern Med stable. After RUM falls, I'll have 6 centers and coordinate with Austria to pressure Italy's overextended Mediterranean fleets in 1908. Russia will be reduced to 3 centers and neutralized as a threat, allowing me to pivot west against Italy with Austrian help.

Revised strategies (post-negotiation), each power's updated plan after the negotiation above.
AUSTRIA
I'll order A SER to support Turkey's A SEV into RUM as agreed, while A BUD holds to guard against any Russian counterattack, A TRI holds to keep the Adriatic quiet per my deal with Italy, A VIE holds as a reserve, and A GRE holds to secure my newly gained center. After RUM falls to our joint operation, I'll have 6 centers and a strong position to pivot against Italy's Mediterranean overextension in 1908, coordinating with Turkey on a strike into GRE or ION while watching Germany's eastward ambitions.
ENGLAND
I'll hold F NTH in place to maintain my position and keep a credible threat on Germany's northern flank, while F NWY holds to threaten Sweden next year and F LON holds to guard the Channel. A YOR will hold as a reserve for future operations. My plan is to wait for Italy to move into MAO next spring and Russia to hit Sweden in 1908, then coordinate a three-front squeeze on Germany that cuts him down before he becomes unstoppable.
FRANCE
I'll hold A PAR this turn and keep F ENG in the Channel, honoring my deal with Germany to survive at BRE through 1907. I intend to rebuild at BRE this winter, and then coordinate with England, Russia, and Austria on a multi-front campaign against Germany in 1908, using my rebuilt fleet and army to strike his western flank while the others pressure his east.
GERMANY
I'll take Paris this fall with A PIC supported by A BEL and A RUH as agreed with France, holding my Baltic and North Sea positions to keep Russia and England contained. After this turn I'll have 8 centers and can pivot to negotiate with Italy about containing England's Channel fleet while keeping my eastern border quiet with Austria. My northern fleets in BAL, BER, and DEN will hold position to maintain the status quo with Russia and England through 1907.
ITALY
I'll move A POR into SPA to secure my 8th center, while F WES supports that move by moving to SPA/SC to secure the coast and position against Germany's southern flank. F ION holds to keep the Adriatic quiet with Austria, and all other units hold—F VEN, F NAP, F TUN, and A ROM—consolidating my position while I prepare to pivot against Germany in the fall with England and Russia, timing the squeeze for 1907.
RUSSIA
Turkey has taken RUM and Austria has committed to supporting him there, so I cannot retake RUM this fall — attacking into RUM with A UKR supported by A SEV and A GAL would face A RUM supported by A BUL, F BLA, and potentially A SER, which is at least 3 defending units vs my 2 attackers, a certain bounce. Instead, I will hold A UKR, hold A SEV, and move A GAL to VIE to threaten Austria's rear and force him to pull support from RUM, while F STP/NC moves to NWY to position for a future strike on Sweden with England. This secures my existing centers, threatens Austria's home territory to break his alliance with Turkey, and positions my northern fleet for the 1908 campaign against Germany's Baltic flank as England promised.
TURKEY
I'll order A SEV - RUM with support from A BUL and F BLA, while F AEG holds and F ANK holds, honoring my deal with Austria to take RUM together this turn. After RUM falls, I'll have 6 centers and coordinate with Austria to pressure Italy's overextended Mediterranean fleets in 1908, while keeping the Black Sea line secure against any Russian counterattack. Russia will be reduced to 3 centers and neutralized as a threat, allowing me to pivot west against Italy with Austrian help.
