SPRING 1905 MOVEMENT (S1905M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll take Greece this spring with F ALB and A SER working together, while A BUD holds to secure my position and A TRI and A VIE hold the home centers. After taking Greece, I'll have 6 centers and can push into Turkey's remaining holdings in 1905 while watching Italy's reaction to losing GRE.
ENGLAND
I'll hold my northern positions this turn while Germany and France tear each other apart in Belgium and Burgundy. My goal is to position F NTH and F LON to threaten Holland or Denmark in the fall, growing to 5-6 centers by 1906, then pivot against whichever neighbor is weakest—likely a depleted France or a stretched Germany after their war.
FRANCE
Italy has MAR and just built two new fleets, making him my primary target this year. I need to reclaim MAR while keeping Germany contained — I'll probe Belgium with A PIC and F ENG to keep the pressure on, while A GAS moves south to support a push into MAR. Over the next two turns I want to retake MAR, build a fleet at BRE, and resume the offensive against Germany with England.
GERMANY
I'll push into PIC from BUR this spring with support from BEL and RUH, coordinating with England's NTH fleet to crush France's northern flank. If Italy continues pressing France from MAR while I take PIC, we can eliminate him by Fall. I'll message England to lock in our attack, offer Italy a free hand in the south, and reassure Austria and Russia that our borders stay quiet.
ITALY
I've got 6 centers, three new fleets, and a solid position in Greece while Austria is reeling and Turkey is tied up in the east. This spring I need to move F GRE into AEG to consolidate control of the eastern Med and threaten Turkey's flank, while F NAP moves into ION to cover any Austrian or Turkish naval push. With F VEN and A ROM watching the Adriatic and A MAR holding the west, I'm positioning to take Bulgaria or Smyrna next fall and push toward 9 centers.
RUSSIA
I need to consolidate my position at 4 centers and reclaim SEV from Turkey this year. This spring I'll hold RUM with GAL's support while moving A MOS toward SEV, testing Turkey's willingness to negotiate. By Fall 1905 I want to hold both RUM and SEV, reaching 5-6 centers, and then decide whether to push into the Balkans against Austria or coordinate with England against Germany's growing power.
TURKEY
The board has shifted dramatically: Italy took GRE and MAR, Russia holds RUM, and Austria is contained in the Balkans with 5 centers. My position is solid at 4 centers with A CON, A SEV, F BLA, and F AEG — I'm the gatekeeper between the Black Sea and the Aegean. This spring I'll hold my line (A SEV, F BLA, A CON, F AEG) while probing Italy for cooperation against Austria's remaining Balkan holdings — if he's willing to push into BUL or SER from GRE next fall, we can carve up Austria together. My immediate goal is to grow from 4 to 6 centers by taking BUL and one more from Austria by Fall 1905, positioning myself for 8+ by end of 1906.

After we eliminate France, I expect Brest as my share in the fall, and I'll then pivot to contain Germany's growing power by coordinating with Russia and Italy against his flanks.
FRANCE

This defensive consolidation gives me a turn to assess whether Austria's promised attack on GRE materializes and whether Italy will honor any deal, while keeping my units positioned to strike back toward MAR or BEL next season.
GERMANY
England has committed to moving F NTH into ENG this spring, so I'll push A BUR into PIC with support from A BEL and A RUH, while A HOL holds and the Baltic fleets stay in position to keep the northern flank quiet. Italy is holding MAR and won't interfere, Austria and Russia are keeping their borders quiet, so my full force can crush France's northern army this turn and set up a fall push into Paris or Brest. After taking PIC, I'll have a strong springboard to eliminate France entirely by the end of 1905, then turn my attention to containing Italy's growing Med presence or England's Channel ambitions depending on how the board shifts.
ITALY
I'll move F GRE into AEG to open the sea route toward Bulgaria, positioning to support Turkey's fall push on BUL/SC as he requested while also threatening Austria's exposed flank. F NAP will move into ION to anchor the central Mediterranean and protect against any Austrian naval counterattack from ALB, while F VEN and A ROM hold the Adriatic line. A MAR will hold to consolidate my gains and keep the German alliance intact through spring, setting up a fall where I either take SPA or join Turkey in the Balkans depending on how the board shifts.
RUSSIA
I'll move F STP/NC into NWY to threaten Sweden as agreed with England, holding A MOS in place to defend Moscow and prepare for a push on SEV later this year. A RUM will hold with support from A GAL to consolidate my position against any Austrian or Turkish move, keeping my southern flank secure while I pressure Germany's northern coast. This sets up a two-front squeeze on Germany in the fall with England, while I maintain the option to reclaim SEV from Turkey if he breaks our deal.
TURKEY
I'll hold A CON and F AEG in position to maintain flexibility, support A SEV with F BLA to keep my center secure against any Russian move, and await the fall turn to coordinate with Italy on a joint push into BUL. My deal with Russia to leave SEV alone in exchange for his support into BUL is unreliable — he wants SEV back and will likely attack — so I'll keep my defensive posture and let the spring moves reveal his true intentions. If Italy follows through on his commitment to move F GRE into AEG, we'll be perfectly set to take BUL/SC together in the fall and continue growing in the Balkans.
