FALL 1909 MOVEMENT (F1909M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I betrayed Turkey last spring and he lost SEV and RUM to Russia, but I got nothing for it — Russia is now in RUM and holding SEV with no intention of giving me BUL. This fall I have a chance to finally gain a center by striking SER with A GRE supported by A BUD and A TRI, while A VIE holds the line against any Russian push from GAL. If I take SER from Italy this turn I reach 6 centers and can build; then I need to pivot against either Russia's flank at RUM or Turkey's at BUL before one of them grabs GRE from me next year.
ENGLAND
I'll take BEL from Germany this fall by convoying A BEL to HOL via NTH, cutting his supply line and forcing him to split his fleets between the Channel and the Baltic. I need Italy to finally push F WES into MAO this turn to threaten Germany's southern coast, and I'll coordinate with Russia to hit SWE from NWY next spring after he retakes SEV. Two turns from now, I want to hold HOL and BEL, have Italy threatening MAO, and be squeezing Germany down from 7 centers while I grow toward 7-8 myself.
FRANCE
I'll retreat A GAS to MAR this turn to reclaim my home center from Italy while Germany consolidates his gains. With MAR back, I'll rebuild to two units in winter and coordinate with England and Austria for a 1910 campaign against Germany's western flank. My goal is to control BRE, MAR, and PAR by the end of 1910.
GERMANY
I'll take BRE this turn with A PAR supported by A BUR, eliminating France and securing my eighth center. After that, I'll pivot to reclaim BEL from England in the fall, using my armies in HOL and RUH to pressure it while keeping my Baltic fleets in position to contain any Russian or English naval moves. Within two turns I aim to hold BEL and PAR, reaching 9 centers and positioning myself to negotiate with Italy or Austria against whichever power emerges as the biggest threat.
ITALY
I'm taking Spain this fall with A POR - SPA and F WES - SPA/SC to finally secure my 8th center, while holding my Med and Adriatic line quiet with Austria. After Spain falls, I'll push F WES into MAO next spring to threaten Germany's Channel flank as promised to England, timing the squeeze for when he's committed to reclaiming BEL. If Germany shifts east toward Austria, I'll pivot to hit his southern coast instead.
RUSSIA
I retake SEV this fall with A UKR supported by A SEV, while F STP/NC holds to keep the northern flank secure. After securing SEV, I build at WAR this winter and pivot north to hit Sweden in spring 1910 with England's support, while keeping Austria neutral by threatening BUL from SEV and UKR.
TURKEY
Russia pulled back last spring, so my eastern flank is secure. This fall I will hold my line and consolidate my 6 centers, making no aggressive moves, while I wait for a build at SMY in Winter. One turn from now, with 7 units and a stronger fleet position, I will push into the Mediterranean and begin carving up Italy's exposed centers.

FRANCE
I will order A GAS to move to MAR, seizing my home center from Italy while his forces are overstretched at POR, SPA/SC, and ION. This gives me a second supply center and a build slot in winter, positioning me to rebuild and coordinate with England against Germany in 1910. Italy cannot contest MAR effectively this turn, and Germany is focused on his own gains, so this is my best chance to reclaim a foothold and become a relevant power again.
GERMANY
I'll commit A HOL and A BUR to attack BEL with support from F DEN moving into NTH to cut England's fleet support, while A BRE holds and A RUH supports A BUR into BEL. F BAL and F BER hold to maintain the Baltic status quo with Russia. After taking BEL this turn, I'll have 8 centers and can pivot to negotiate with Italy about containing England's remaining centers or with Austria about a clean eastern border through 1910.
ITALY
I'll finally secure Spain this turn with A POR - SPA and F WES - SPA/SC, reaching 8 centers, while holding my entire eastern line—F ION, F VEN, F NAP, F TUN, and A ROM—to keep the Adriatic quiet with Austria and maintain my Med position. After Spain falls, I'll push F WES into MAO in spring 1910 to threaten Germany's Channel flank as promised to England, timing the squeeze for when he's committed to reclaiming BEL from the north. Turkey has warned me Austria is moving south against BUL, so I'll keep F ION and F VEN holding to avoid being drawn into that conflict while I focus on securing the west.
RUSSIA
I'll retake SEV this fall with A UKR supported by A SEV, while F STP/NC holds in position to keep my northern flank secure. After building an army at WAR this winter, I'll coordinate with England to hit Sweden from NWY in spring 1910, keeping Austria neutral by threatening BUL from SEV and UKR next year.
TURKEY
I will retake SEV by moving A ARM to SEV with support from F BLA, while holding BUL with support from F CON and keeping my other fleets in place. After securing SEV, I will build a fleet at SMY in Winter and begin pushing into the Mediterranean in 1910, targeting Italy's exposed southern flank while Russia and Austria are contained.
