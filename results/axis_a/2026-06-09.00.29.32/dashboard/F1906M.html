FALL 1906 MOVEMENT (F1906M)

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll take GRE this turn with A SER supported by A BUD, since Turkey won't interfere and Italy has no support to hold it. After securing GRE, I'll have 5 centers and can pivot toward coordinating with Turkey against Russia's RUM in 1907, while keeping my home centers secure and watching Germany finish France in the west.
ENGLAND
I'm moving F NTH to DEN this turn to challenge Germany's Baltic control directly, while F NWY holds to maintain pressure on Scandinavia and F LON holds to guard the Channel. Russia can't hit Sweden this spring as planned, so I'll need to negotiate with Italy to pressure Germany's southern flank in 1907, while positioning to take Denmark and grow to 5 centers before winter builds. My goal is to force Germany to split his fleets between the Baltic and the North Sea, setting up a two-front containment with Italy and Russia in 1907.
FRANCE
Germany has 7 centers and just threatened to take Paris, while England offered to support my attack on BEL but I don't trust him after last year's betrayal. I need to survive this turn by holding PAR and keeping my fleets in position, then use the fall to negotiate a real coalition against Germany with England or Italy. My goal is to reclaim MAR from Italy in 1907 while Germany's push stalls, then build at BRE and resume the offensive toward Belgium and the Channel.
GERMANY
I'll push A PIC into PAR this fall with support from A BEL and A RUH, while A HOL holds and my Baltic fleets stay in position to keep Russia quiet. France cannot defend both PAR and BRE, and Italy is taking POR as agreed — I'll secure Paris this turn and set up a fall push into Brest to eliminate France entirely. After France falls, I'll have the strength to pivot against England if he continues to block the Channel, or negotiate with Austria and Russia to contain Italy's growing Med presence.
ITALY
I'll take Portugal with A POR - SPA to claim the center and reach 8 supply centers, while repositioning my fleet from TYS toward WES to threaten Germany's southern flank next year. I need to keep Austria quiet in the Adriatic and Turkey stable in the east while I consolidate, then pivot toward containing Germany with England and Russia in 1907.
RUSSIA
Turkey has refused to give up SEV, so I'll take it by force this fall with A MOS supported by A RUM and A GAL, reclaiming my home center and reaching 5 centers. With SEV secured, I'll build in WAR this winter, then pivot against Austria's weakened 4-center position while England and Germany bleed each other in the west. In two turns I want to hold 6-7 centers by taking BUD or SER from Austria with Turkish help, setting up a stronger position to challenge the leader.
TURKEY
Italy took POR and now holds 8 centers — he's the clear leader and will turn on me once he's done in the west. I'll use this fall to position for a strike: move A BUL into SER with support from F AEG, while F BLA and F ANK hold the Black Sea against Russia. By Spring 1907, I aim to hold SER and BUL, reach 6 centers, and pivot to either take GRE from Italy with Austrian help or push into RUM against a weakened Russia.
