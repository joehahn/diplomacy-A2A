FALL 1908 MOVEMENT (F1908M)

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm holding my line quietly this turn and honoring my deal with Italy to keep the Adriatic quiet, while continuing to support Turkey at RUM against Russia. After the fall, I want to probe whether Germany is now the bigger threat or whether a joint push with Turkey against Italy's overextended Mediterranean can still work. My goal is to reach 6-7 centers by 1909 by picking off either SER from Italy or BUL from Turkey if the right opening appears.
ENGLAND
Germany has taken Paris and now holds 7 centers, with France shattered. My convoy to BEL bounced, so I need a new approach. This fall I will hold my northern position and negotiate with Italy to push F WES into MAO, forcing Germany to split his fleets between the Channel and the Med, while I prepare to strike DEN or BEL in spring 1909 with coordinated support from Italy and a rebuilt Russia.
FRANCE
I'll hold A BRE and F ENG this turn, keeping my remaining units safe while Germany consolidates Paris. I need to survive this year and rebuild to 3 units by winter, then coordinate with England, Russia, and Austria in 1909 for a multi-front assault against Germany's 8+ centers. My goal is to reclaim PAR and MAR within two years by striking Germany's western flank while the eastern powers pressure his rear.
GERMANY
I'll take Paris this turn with A PIC supported by A BEL and A RUH, finally eliminating France and reaching 8 centers. My Baltic and North Sea fleets hold position to maintain the status quo with Russia and England while I consolidate. After France falls, I'll pivot to negotiate with Italy about containing England's Channel fleet and with Austria about keeping the eastern border quiet while I prepare my push toward 18 centers.
ITALY
Germany has stalled at 7 centers and is repositioning east, leaving France weak and his Channel flank exposed. This fall I will push F WES into MAO to threaten his southern coast while England hits BEL from the north, forcing him to split his fleets between the Baltic and the Med. If I take Spain with A POR this turn, I hit 8 centers and position to claim France’s remaining centers in 1909 while coordinating with England to squeeze Germany from both sides.
RUSSIA
I'll retake RUM this fall with A UKR supported by A SEV while A GAL threatens VIE to split Austria from Turkey. After securing RUM and building in WAR this winter, I'll turn north to hit SWE from NWY with England against Germany in 1909, finishing with 6 centers and a path to 8.
TURKEY
Austria and I agreed to move into the Mediterranean this spring, but he only pushed A TRI and A GRE to ALB, while I moved F AEG into ION — neither of us gained ground against Italy. This fall, I need to secure a concrete gain: I’ll support a Rumanian attack on SEV from BUL and BLA, aiming to take SEV from Russia and reach 6 centers, while building a third fleet at CON in Winter to project power into the Med.

Taking BEL from Germany this turn cuts his supply line and gives us a foothold to retake Paris next spring. After this move, I will rebuild at BRE in winter and coordinate with England, Russia, and Austria for a multi-front campaign against Germany in 1909.

My Baltic fleets hold position to maintain the status quo with Russia while I consolidate my western gains, and after this turn I'll pivot to negotiate with Italy and Austria about containing each other while I prepare my push toward 18 centers.
ITALY

The timing is set: Spain now, MAO next spring, and the squeeze on Germany in 1909.
RUSSIA

Austria has refused every offer to drop Turkey and I cannot break RUM against a coordinated defense this turn, so I'll secure my existing line and build at WAR this winter, then turn north against Germany's Baltic flank with England in 1909 after consolidating.
TURKEY
I'll order A RUM to attack SEV with support from A BUL and F BLA, aiming to take Russia's last supply center and reach 6 centers for a winter build at CON. After SEV falls, I'll build a fleet at CON and pivot west against Italy's Mediterranean in 1909, while Austria holds his position and Russia is neutralized. This turn secures my eastern flank and sets up the naval push into ION next year.
