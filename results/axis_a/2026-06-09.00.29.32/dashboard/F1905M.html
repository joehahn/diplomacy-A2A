Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'll take GRE this fall with F ALB and A SER working together, while A BUD holds to secure my position and A TRI and A VIE hold the home centers. After taking GRE, I'll have 6 centers and can push into Turkey's remaining holdings in 1906 while watching Italy's reaction to losing GRE.
ENGLAND
Germany is about to finish France and will hit 9+ centers, making him the clear solo threat. I need to pivot: I'll hold my position this turn and negotiate with Italy and Russia to form a containment coalition against Germany in 1905-1906, while positioning to claim Denmark or Holland once Germany overextends. My goal is to grow to 6-7 centers by late 1906 by taking a German coastal center while the coalition grinds him down from three sides.
FRANCE
Italy stabbed me at MAR and has two new fleets, while Germany sits at 7 centers with a strong western front. This turn I need to secure my position by keeping A PAR in Paris to defend against the German push from PIC, holding F ENG and F MAO to maintain my naval presence, and holding A GAS to guard the Spanish border. Over the next two turns, I aim to reclaim MAR by coordinating with England against Germany or with Austria against Italy, depending on which alliance yields the best opening.
GERMANY
France is pulling back from PIC and Italy is consolidating MAR, so this fall I'll push into PIC to take it from France and set up a decisive 1906 campaign against Paris and Brest. England is committed to the Channel and expects Brest as his share, but I'll renegotiate that after I'm in PIC and France is broken. My Baltic fleets hold position to keep Russia quiet while I finish France, then I'll turn toward containing Italy's growing Med fleet presence or England's Channel ambitions depending on who moves first.
ITALY
I'll move F GRE into AEG to support Turkey's fall attack on BUL/SC as agreed, while holding F ION in position to cover the central Med and prevent any Austrian naval push from ALB. I'll keep A MAR and A ROM steady to consolidate my six centers, and I'll probe Austria and Turkey in negotiations to see if the Balkan alliance with Turkey is still solid or if I need to pivot toward containing Germany's northern dominance. Two turns from now, I want to be at 7-8 centers by taking SPA from France or BUL with Turkey, then building toward a decisive push against whichever power is weakest.
RUSSIA
I'll hold RUM with GAL's support while moving F STP/NC into NWY to threaten Sweden and force Germany to split his Baltic fleets, setting up a two-front squeeze with England in the fall. A MOS will hold to defend Moscow and prepare for a push on SEV if Turkey breaks our deal. Two turns from now I want to hold RUM and NWY, reclaim SEV from Turkey, and coordinate with England to cut Germany down from both north and east.
TURKEY
Italy's move into AEG this spring threatens my own Aegean position — he may be setting up to take BUL/SC for himself rather than support me. I'll hold my line this turn and see how the spring moves resolve before committing to any fall push, keeping A CON and F AEG flexible to either support an Italian attack on BUL or block him if he tries to take it alone. Russia's threat to SEV is real but he lacks the strength to dislodge me with F BLA in support, so I can focus on the Balkans where the real growth opportunity lies.
