FALL 1902 MOVEMENT (F1902M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
Russia took Bulgaria last spring with my support, but he now holds BUL/EC and RUM while I only have SER and GRE—he's outgrowing me. I need to pivot this fall: I'll move A SER into BUL to contest it from the land side, supported by F GRE, while keeping my home centers secure and probing Turkey for a realignment against Russia. In two turns, I want to hold Bulgaria and Greece, with my armies positioned to push into Rumania with Turkish help, stopping Russia's southern expansion before he becomes unstoppable.
ENGLAND
I'll hold my northern positions this turn while Russia takes Bulgaria, then in the fall I'll coordinate with Russia to squeeze Germany from both sides—my fleets in NTH and NWY will threaten Kiel and Denmark while Russia's fleet in BOT and armies in GAL press Berlin from the east. My goal is to take Denmark and Kiel in 1903, pushing Germany down to 2 centers while I grow to 6-7, then turn on France with Russian help once Germany is crippled.
FRANCE
I'll take Spain this fall by convoying A POR through F MAO, while A PIC holds to maintain pressure on Belgium without interfering with Germany's claim. I'll move F BRE into the English Channel to deter England from probing south and position for a potential 1903 strike against England's coast or a coordinated push with Germany. By next spring I want to hold SPA, POR, and have a fleet in ENG to threaten LON or support a landing, ready to pivot against whichever neighbor overreaches — England if he stays weak, or Germany if he consolidates the Low Countries and looks west.
GERMANY
This turn I’m moving F DEN into Sweden to seize that open neutral before Russia can, while A KIE supports A BEL into Holland to claim both Low Countries supply centers. F BAL will cover the Baltic approaches and screen against any Russian or English fleet movement, and A RUH stands ready to support the Low Countries push. In 1903 I’ll consolidate at 6 or 7 centers and begin positioning to either turn on England in the North Sea or pressure France through Burgundy, depending on who shows weakness first.
ITALY
I need to take Tunisia this fall to get to 4 centers and set up a build in 1903. My Ionian fleet will convoy A ROM to TUN while A PIE holds to keep pressure on France's MAR and A ROM moves to NAP to open a build slot. In 1903 I want to have Tunisia secured, a fleet in TYS threatening the Western Med, and the option to either push on France or turn east against Austria depending on who overextends.
RUSSIA
I'm taking Bulgaria this fall with my fleet in BUL/EC and Austrian support from Serbia, then pivoting to hit Turkey's remaining centers like Constantinople with a combined Russian-Austrian push while my northern fleet in BOT threatens Germany's Baltic coast to keep him off balance. After securing Bulgaria, I'll aim for Turkey's home centers in 1903 while coordinating with England to squeeze Germany from both sides, using my builds from Rumania and Bulgaria to reinforce both theaters.
TURKEY
Russia has broken our agreement and is moving into BUL/EC. I need to take BUL back this turn with A CON, position my fleet in BLA to cut his support into BUL and threaten RUM, and move A ARM toward SEV to pressure his southern flank. By Fall 1903, I want to have BUL secured and be coordinating with Austria to split Russia's territory — taking RUM and SEV while he takes the northern parts.

A BUD and A VIE will hold to keep my home centers secure, while A TRI holds to guard against any Italian or Russian flanking move. After taking BUL, I'll coordinate with Turkey to push into RUM next spring, splitting Russia's southern gains and keeping the board balanced.
ENGLAND
I'll order F NTH to support F NWY into SWE, F NWY to move to SWE, F LON to hold, and A YOR to hold, securing Sweden this fall to block Germany's northern expansion while keeping my fleet in NTH positioned to threaten Kiel next year. This gives me 5 centers, sets up a strong 1903 campaign where I can coordinate with Russia against Germany's Baltic coast, and leaves my forces intact for a potential turn against Germany or France depending on how the board shifts.
FRANCE
I'll convoy A POR to SPA via F MAO this fall, securing Iberia as agreed with Italy and England. A PIC will hold to maintain pressure on Belgium without interfering with Germany's claim, and F BRE will hold in Brest to keep my options open for a Channel move in 1903 depending on how Germany and England resolve the Low Countries. This keeps my deals intact while positioning for a pivot against whichever neighbor overreaches next year.
GERMANY
I'll move F DEN into Sweden to seize that open center, supported by F BAL from the Baltic, while A BEL holds in Belgium and A KIE holds in Kiel to consolidate my position. This turn I'm taking Sweden cleanly as I've signaled to England and Russia, keeping my Low Countries gains secure and setting up a 5-center position for Winter builds, with an eye toward coordinating against France or England in 1903 depending on who consolidates fastest.
ITALY
I'll take Tunisia with my Ionian fleet, holding both armies in place. This gives me my first new supply center, putting me at 4 centers for a build in 1903. I'll keep my options open for next year, whether that means coordinating with Turkey against Austria or pushing west against France.
RUSSIA
Austria has broken our deal and is moving into Bulgaria themselves, so I can no longer count on their support. I will hold F BUL/EC in place rather than risk a bounce, and pivot A UKR toward SEV to threaten Turkey's rear while keeping A GAL and A MOS in reserve to support either the southern or northern front. In the Baltic, F BOT will hold as well, maintaining pressure on Germany's flank while I assess how to respond to Austria's betrayal and Turkey's hostility in the coming turns.
TURKEY
I'll move A CON to BUL to retake my lost supply center, F BLA to BUL/EC to cut Russia's support and threaten his hold, and A ARM to SEV to pressure Russia's southern flank. This puts maximum force on Russia's exposed position while Austria's indecision leaves him isolated—if I can dislodge Russia from BUL and take SEV in the same turn, I'll have four centers and a clear path to split his territory with Austria next year.
