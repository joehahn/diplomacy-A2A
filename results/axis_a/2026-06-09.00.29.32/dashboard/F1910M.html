FALL 1910 MOVEMENT (F1910M)

Orders — start positions, arrows show moves

Initial strategies (pre-negotiation), each power's self-authored plan before talking.
AUSTRIA
I'm taking BUL this turn with A SER - BUL supported by A BUD and A TRI, reaching 6 centers and a Winter build. Germany is the clear leader at 9 centers with two new armies, so after securing BUL I'll pivot to build an army at VIE and coordinate with Italy and Russia to cut him down in 1911. Russia refused to support into BUL this spring but holds SEV and UKR, so I'll keep my northern border quiet with Germany while building toward a multi-front containment of his expansion.
ENGLAND
Russia is committed to taking Sweden this turn with my support from NWY, and I will execute that attack to cut Germany's supply line. I'll hold NTH and LON to guard the North Sea and Channel, keeping my fleet line intact while Germany's forces are split between the Baltic and the Med. Two turns from now, I want Sweden in Russian hands, Germany weakened by the loss of a center, and my fleets positioned to support a push into Denmark or the Channel in 1911.
FRANCE
I'll take SPA with A MAR this turn while Italy's forces are split at POR and SPA/SC — he can't stop me, and holding MAR while bouncing at SPA wastes my only chance to gain a center. Once I hold SPA, I'll rebuild at MAR in winter and coordinate with England and Austria to pressure Germany on multiple fronts in 1911, aiming to reclaim BRE and PAR within two years.
GERMANY
I have 9 centers to everyone else's 4–6, and no real coalition forming against me yet. This turn I move A KIE to DEN via convoy, F BAL supports A KIE's crossing, and F SKA moves to NWY to take Norway from England while my armies in BEL, PIC, and HOL consolidate the Channel and prepare for a two-front push: north against England's home islands and east against an overextended Russia or Austria. By the end of 1911 I want 12–14 centers, Norway, London, and either a decisive strike into Russia or a clean east-west split with Italy to contain the leader.
ITALY
I'm finally taking Spain this turn with A POR - SPA and F WES - SPA/SC to secure my 8th center, then pushing F WES into MAO in the spring to threaten Germany's Channel flank. My eastern line holds with F ION, F VEN, F NAP, and F TUN all staying put to keep the Adriatic quiet with Austria while I consolidate the west. After Spain falls, I'll coordinate with England to squeeze Germany between his Channel operations and my southern presence, forcing him to split his fleet advantage.
RUSSIA
I'll move F STP/NC to SWE with England's F NWY supporting, taking Sweden this fall and cutting Germany's supply line. I'll keep A UKR and A SEV holding to maintain the quiet border with Turkey as agreed, while A GAL holds in position to threaten Austria's VIE and keep him honest. After building at MOS this winter, I'll have 5 centers and be positioned to hit Germany's Baltic flank with England in 1911.
TURKEY
Germany is at 9 centers and pulling away—I need to break out of my static position this fall. I'll push F AEG into ION to contest Italy's naval dominance and open a path to his exposed southern centers, while A BUL moves into GRE to take that supply center from Austria and grow to 6 centers. In two turns I want to hold ION and GRE, build a fleet at SMY in Winter, and coordinate with Russia or Italy to start cutting Germany down before he reaches 18.
